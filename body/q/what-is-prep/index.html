Question

What is PrEP?

  • Tier A strong Tested in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

PrEP is a medicine you take before a possible exposure to HIV, and it is highly effective when taken as prescribed. Depending on where you live it comes as tablets, injections or a vaginal ring. WHO recommends four methods: oral tenofovir-based PrEP, the dapivirine vaginal ring, and long-acting injectable cabotegravir or lenacapavir.
