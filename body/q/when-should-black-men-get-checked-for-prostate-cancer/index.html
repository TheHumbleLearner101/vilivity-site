Question

When should Black men get checked for prostate cancer?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

If you have symptoms, go to a doctor: peeing more often, urgently or at night, blood in your pee, or erection problems. If you are worried, you can ask a doctor about a PSA test, and they will discuss the risks and benefits. In England there is no routine NHS PSA screening for Black men: the UK National Screening Committee advised against it, as reported on 28 May 2026.
