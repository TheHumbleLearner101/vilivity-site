Question

Is sarcoidosis serious?

  • Tier B thin Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Many cases settle without treatment, but a few people become severely affected, so follow-up matters. Keep monitoring appointments: X-rays, breathing tests and blood tests show whether it is settling or getting worse. Sarcoidosis can also affect skin, glands, joints, eyes and the heart. African Americans and women have higher death rates in the published literature.

What this is based on

  • Sarcoidosis is a rare condition where small patches of swollen tissue form, most often in the lungs, skin and lymph nodes. In studies it is more common, and in some studies more severe, in Black adults. Many cases settle without treatment, but a lasting cough, breathlessness or sore red eyes need a doctor.From the card's plain answer
