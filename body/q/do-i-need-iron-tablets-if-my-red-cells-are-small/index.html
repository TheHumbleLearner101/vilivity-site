Question

Do I need iron tablets if my red cells are small?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Ask what is behind small red cells before you start iron tablets. Iron tablets replace missing iron, and are advised once a blood test shows low red cells and the cause has been found; the gene change is a separate cause of small red cells. If you are anaemic and iron tablets have not helped, ask a doctor whether thalassaemia trait was considered.

What this is based on

  • Alpha-thalassaemia trait means you carry one or two missing copies of the genes that make part of haemoglobin. It is common in people of African descent and can show as small, pale red cells and mild anaemia that looks like low iron.
