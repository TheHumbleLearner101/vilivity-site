Home / Body / Breast cancer, triple-negative disease and screening

Question

what does breast cancer look like on black skin

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

See a doctor about a lump, swelling, dimpling or a change in your breast or nipple. On black or brown skin a colour change may look darker than usual. Breast pain by itself is not usually a symptom, and you can have these symptoms without pain. Do not wait for a screening letter if you have noticed a change.

From the card

In England, Black women did not have a higher breast cancer rate than White women, but short-term survival was worse in national data, and one London hospital saw younger, more aggressive cases. Know your own breasts and see a GP about any change. NHS breast screening starts between 50 and 53, bowel screening at 50.
