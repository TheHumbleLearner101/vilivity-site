Home / Body / Pregnancy risk and safe maternity care for Black women

Question

signs of depression in pregnancy

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Low mood, anxiety or thoughts of harm in pregnancy or in the year after are reasons to speak to a GP or midwife, or to use the NHS 111 mental health option, in England. If you are in danger, call 999. If you were not listened to and a symptom is getting worse, ask for the duty midwife or another doctor, or call 111.

From the card

In England, Black women are nearly three times as likely to die during pregnancy or within six weeks of it as White women, in the latest national report. A confidential enquiry found bias in the care some received. Go to every antenatal check, learn the warning signs, and call your maternity unit straight away if one appears.
