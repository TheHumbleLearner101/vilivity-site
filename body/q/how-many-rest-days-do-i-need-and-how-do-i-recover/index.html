Question

How many rest days do I need and how do I recover?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

The NHS (England) targets spread activity over 4 to 5 days or every day, with strengthening work on at least 2 days a week. Our search found nothing saying Black adults need a different programme or different rest days, and recovery time by ancestry or skin tone is untested. Get urgent care today for severe muscle pain, weakness or dark urine after hard exercise.

What this is based on

  • Nothing I found says Black adults need a different programme or rest days, so the NHS targets apply: strength work on two days, plus moderate or vigorous activity most days.
