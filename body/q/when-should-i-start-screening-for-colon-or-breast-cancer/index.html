Question

When should I start screening for colon or breast cancer?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

In England, breast screening invitations arrive between 50 and 53 and then every 3 years until your 71st birthday, and the bowel screening home test (FIT) comes from age 50, every 2 years to 74. If no breast letter has come by 53, contact your local breast screening service. Whether screening before 50 helps Black women is untested; the NHS offers it from 50.

What this is based on

  • In England, Black women did not have a higher breast cancer rate than White women, but short-term survival was worse in national data, and one London hospital saw younger, more aggressive cases.
