Question

Should I get tested for sickle cell trait before intense exercise?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

A blood test shows whether you have the trait: ask your GP. Some sports bodies test at the pre-participation exam. In US college football (NCAA, Division I), deaths associated with the trait fell from 1 in 28,145 athlete-years before legislation to 1 in 250,468 after it, but the policy bundled screening and education, so the two cannot be separated. See a GP if you are unsure of your status.

From the card

Sickle cell trait is one copy of the sickle gene, not sickle cell disease. It raises the risk of serious muscle breakdown in very hard exercise. A soldiers' study found no clear difference in deaths (a wide margin of error); a US college football study found far more exertional deaths. Know your status and train with care.
