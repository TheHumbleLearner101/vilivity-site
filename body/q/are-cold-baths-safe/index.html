Home / Body / Ice baths and cold exposure

Question

are cold baths safe

  • Tier B moderate Tested, but not in people like you
  • Harm signal
  • Founder's own practice
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Cold shock is a real risk. The cold shock response causes fast breathing and more irregular heartbeats, and raises drowning risk; repeated immersions reduced these responses after about four. Enter slowly and let your breathing settle, go with someone and tell them when you will be back, and get out if you shiver badly or feel weak, disorientated or short of breath.

What this is based on

  • Enter slowly and give your breathing time to settle before anything else.Clinical guidance Met Office blog, "Cold water shock: what it is and how to stay safe" (quotes RNLI and RLSS UK)
  • Get out if you shiver badly, feel weak, disorientated or short of breath, then warm up at once with dry clothes and a hot drink.Clinical guidance Met Office blog, "Cold water shock: what it is and how to stay safe" (quotes RNLI and RLSS UK)
  • Go with someone, and tell someone where you are and when you will be back.Clinical guidance Met Office blog, "Cold water shock: what it is and how to stay safe" (quotes RNLI and RLSS UK)
  • Cold water with a heart condition unless a doctor agrees: cold shock increases arrhythmias.Tested Barwood 2024
  • Who was tested: 17 groups, 145 participants in cold-shock response analyses. What was found: Cold-water immersion triggers a reflex cold shock response with hyperventilation, increased cardiac arrhythmias and increased drowning risk. Repeated immersions reduced these responses after about four immersions.Tested Barwood 2024 Study page

Also asked as

  • are ice baths bad for you

From the card

Ice baths eased muscle soreness after exercise in small, low-quality trials that did not report ethnicity. No trial has tested them in people of African descent, and cold injury was far more common in African American soldiers in one British army clinic. Cold shock is a real risk.
