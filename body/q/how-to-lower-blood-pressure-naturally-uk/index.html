Question

how to lower blood pressure naturally uk

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Cut down on salt and keep active, as for every adult with high blood pressure (NHS, UK guidance). In England, NICE says a high clinic reading is confirmed with home or 24-hour monitoring before any diagnosis, and in the UK you see your GP at 140/90 or above in clinic, or 135/85 at home.

From the card

The line for high blood pressure is the same for every adult. In England, blood pressure in people of African-Caribbean origin overtakes White people's at around 30 to 40 (about 28 for men's systolic pressure), so being young and fit does not rule it out. Get it checked. If you need tablets, NICE names a different first choice for you.
