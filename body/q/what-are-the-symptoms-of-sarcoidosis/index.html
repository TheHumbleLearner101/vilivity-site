Question

What are the symptoms of sarcoidosis?

  • Tier B thin Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Symptoms depend on which organs are affected: a persistent dry cough, breathlessness, tender bumps on the skin, or red, sore eyes. A red, painful eye or change in vision needs same-day eye care or an urgent doctor. Palpitations, irregular heartbeat or fainting need urgent care, and kidney-stone pain or tender lumps on the legs and arms with feeling unwell need a doctor.

From the card

Sarcoidosis is a rare condition where small patches of swollen tissue form, most often in the lungs, skin and lymph nodes. In studies it is more common, and in some studies more severe, in Black adults. Many cases settle without treatment, but a lasting cough, breathlessness or sore red eyes need a doctor.
