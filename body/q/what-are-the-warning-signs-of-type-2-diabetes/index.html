Question

what are the warning signs of type 2 diabetes

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Being very tired, very thirsty, peeing more than usual or losing weight without trying are reasons to ask a doctor for a blood glucose test. But not everyone with type 2 diabetes has any symptoms, so do not wait for them. If you are Black African or Black Caribbean and 25 or over and have never been checked, get checked.

What this is based on
