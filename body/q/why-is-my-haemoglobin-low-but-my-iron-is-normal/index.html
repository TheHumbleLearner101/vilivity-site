Question

Why is my haemoglobin low but my iron is normal?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Alpha-thalassaemia trait is one cause: it can show as small, pale red cells and mild anaemia that looks like low iron, so ask what is behind small red cells before you start iron tablets. In the UK, a GP does a full blood count and may do other tests. See a GP if tiredness, breathlessness or paleness is new or getting worse.

What this is based on

  • Alpha-thalassaemia trait means you carry one or two missing copies of the genes that make part of haemoglobin. It is common in people of African descent and can show as small, pale red cells and mild anaemia that looks like low iron.
