Question

why does endometriosis take so long to get diagnosed

  • Tier B thin Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Endometriosis can take time to diagnose because its symptoms overlap with fibroids, IBS and other conditions. Our search found nothing we could open on diagnosis delay in Black women in the UK. Keep notes of your symptoms, including pain when peeing, pooing or having sex, and see a GP (in the UK) if pain or heavy periods stop you doing normal things.
