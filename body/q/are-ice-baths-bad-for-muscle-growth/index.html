Home / Body / Ice baths and cold exposure

Question

are ice baths bad for muscle growth

  • Tier B moderate Tested, but not in people like you
  • Harm signal
  • Founder's own practice
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Skip an ice bath straight after strength training if gaining strength or size is your aim. In a trial of 21 active men, strength and muscle mass increased more with active recovery than with 10 minutes of cold-water immersion, and markers of muscle growth signalling were lower after immersion. It was men only and did not report ethnicity.

What this is based on

  • Skip it straight after strength training if gaining strength or size is your aim.Tested Roberts 2015
  • Who was tested: 21 physically active men (12-week study) and 9 men (muscle biopsy study). What was found: Strength and muscle mass increased more with active recovery than with cold-water immersion, and several markers of muscle growth signalling were lower after cold-water immersion.Tested Roberts 2015 Study page

Also asked as

  • Should you ice bath before or after a workout?

From the card

Ice baths eased muscle soreness after exercise in small, low-quality trials that did not report ethnicity. No trial has tested them in people of African descent, and cold injury was far more common in African American soldiers in one British army clinic. Cold shock is a real risk.
