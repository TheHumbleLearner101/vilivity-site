Question

Why does my blood count say anaemia when I feel fine?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Trait usually means mild anaemia only. In a US study of 1,491 African American and 31,005 white people, 19.8% of African American women would be classed as anaemic against 5.3% of white women; with iron-deficient and thalassaemic people removed, the figures were 6.1% and 2.77%. The authors say the same reference standards do not apply to all ethnic groups.

From the card

Alpha-thalassaemia trait means you carry one or two missing copies of the genes that make part of haemoglobin. It is common in people of African descent and can show as small, pale red cells and mild anaemia that looks like low iron. Ask what is behind small red cells before you start iron tablets.
