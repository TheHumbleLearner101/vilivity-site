Question

Why are Black people more likely to get kidney disease?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Two variants of the APOL1 gene, common on African chromosomes, raise the risk of some kidney diseases in African Americans. High blood pressure and diabetes are also blamed for kidney trouble in Black families. Early kidney disease usually has no symptoms, so if you have high blood pressure or diabetes, ask about regular kidney checks.
