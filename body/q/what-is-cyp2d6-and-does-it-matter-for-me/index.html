Question

What is CYP2D6 and does it matter for me?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

CYP2D6 is a gene for a drug-processing enzyme. Functional versions make up about 50% of copies in Africans and African Americans, reduced-function versions about 35% (mostly *17), and non-functional versions 14.5% in African Americans against 6.3% in Africans. In Europeans and their descendants, functional versions are about 71%. Tell your prescriber about any past reaction to a medicine.

From the card

Genes change how fast your body breaks down some medicines. The variable is the gene, and some versions are more common in people of African ancestry. Examples are ACE inhibitor tablets, which cause face or throat swelling more often in Black patients, and warfarin, whose dose needs gene variants the first formulas missed.
