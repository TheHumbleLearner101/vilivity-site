Question

How do I start training and what programme suits me?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Start with the NHS (England) targets: at least 150 minutes of moderate activity a week or 75 minutes of vigorous, plus strengthening work for all the major muscle groups on at least 2 days. If you have not exercised for some time or have a medical condition, speak to your GP first, and match intensity to your fitness. Nothing we found says Black adults need a different programme.

What this is based on

  • Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate activity a week or 75 minutes of vigorous, spread over 4 to 5 days or every day.Clinical guidance NHS, "Physical activity guidelines for adults aged 19 to 64"
  • Do strengthening work for all the major muscle groups on at least 2 days a week.Clinical guidance NHS, "Physical activity guidelines for adults aged 19 to 64"
  • Speak to your doctor first if you have not exercised for some time or have a medical condition, and match intensity to your fitness.Clinical guidance NHS, "Physical activity guidelines for adults aged 19 to 64"
  • Nothing I found says Black adults need a different programme or rest days, so the NHS targets apply: strength work on two days, plus moderate or vigorous activity most days.
