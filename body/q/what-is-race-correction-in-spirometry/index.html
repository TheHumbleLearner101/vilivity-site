Question

What is race correction in spirometry?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Your spirometry result is compared with a predicted normal, and race correction means that prediction also used self-reported race and ethnicity. Worry grew that this gives a false view of fixed differences between races and may mask the effect of different exposures such as air pollution and work. A body-proportion measure, sitting height, did not explain the differences in 6,984 NHANES III participants.

From the card

A spirometry test measures how much air you can blow out in one breath. For years the machine compared Black patients with a lower predicted normal than white patients, which can hide lung disease. Guidelines now recommend one race-neutral set of equations. The test is still useful, and your symptoms matter as much as the number.
