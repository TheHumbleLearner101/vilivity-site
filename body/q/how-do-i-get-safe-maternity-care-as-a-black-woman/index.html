Question

How do I get safe maternity care as a Black woman?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Go to every routine antenatal appointment: blood pressure and pee checks are how pre-eclampsia is picked up. Learn the warning signs, and in England call your maternity unit or NHS 111 straight away if one appears. Take up the sickle cell blood test your midwife offers before 10 weeks. If you were not listened to and a symptom is getting worse, ask for the duty midwife or another doctor.

From the card

In England, Black women are nearly three times as likely to die during pregnancy or within six weeks of it as White women, in the latest national report. A confidential enquiry found bias in the care some received. Go to every antenatal check, learn the warning signs, and call your maternity unit straight away if one appears.
