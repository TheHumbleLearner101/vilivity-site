Question

What is G6PD deficiency?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

G6PD deficiency is an inherited enzyme shortage, common in people with African, Mediterranean and Asian ancestry. Most carriers feel well. Red blood cells break down only when something stresses them, such as an infection, fava beans or certain medicines. A simple blood test finds it.
