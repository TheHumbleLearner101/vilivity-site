Question

Is PCOS associated with heavy periods?

  • Tier B thin Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Heavy periods can be a sign of fibroids, endometriosis, PMOS (the NHS name for PCOS) or, rarely, womb cancer, so do not assume they are always normal. In the UK, see a GP if you have irregular or missing periods with hair growth, acne or thick dark patches of skin on the neck or armpits.

What this is based on
