Home / Body / Magnesium supplements for sleep and cramps

Question

Do Black people need more magnesium, and has it been tested?

  • Tier B thin Tested, but not in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Our search found no magnesium supplement trial for sleep or cramps in Black participants. A 2003 review reports that low magnesium status may be more common in African Americans, but that is an association from observational evidence, and the authors say trials in African Americans still need to be done. The review does not show that a supplement helps.

What this is based on

  • Who was tested: African Americans (narrative review of observational evidence on magnesium status). What was found: The authors report that lower dietary and serum magnesium go with hypertension, insulin resistance and diabetes, and that occult magnesium deficiency may be more prevalent in African Americans than in other groups. They say trials that correct magnesium status in African Americans still need to be done.Tested Fox 2003 Study page
  • My PubMed search (title and abstract terms for the product, with African American, African-American, Black adults, Black women, Black men, African descent, African ancestry and similar terms) returned 38 records for magnesium supplementation; the relevant ones were observational (a 2003 review, intake surveys), and none was a magnesium supplement trial for sleep or cramps in Black participants. The same search style for "vitamin D" returned 930 records, so the search works. ClinicalTrials.gov (API v2, intervention search with "African American" OR Black anywhere in the record): 32 records matched (titles read only; most unrelated). The NIH Office of Dietary Supplements pages returned a security check and could not be opened. Untested: sleep or cramp benefit in Black participants; whether any low magnesium status in Black adults is corrected by supplements.Not tested, and gaps (from the card)

Checked against its sources on 2026-11-17.

From the card

Magnesium tablets did not clearly prevent leg cramps in older adults, and for sleep three small, low-quality trials in older adults hint at falling asleep a little faster. Our search found no supplement trial in Black participants. Up to four hundred milligrams a day from supplements is unlikely to harm; more can cause diarrhoea.
