Question

What does it mean if my white blood cell count is very low?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

See a doctor if a low neutrophil count comes with fevers, repeated infections, mouth ulcers or feeling unwell, and urgently if you have a fever. See a doctor, then a haematologist, if the count is very low or keeps falling. A Duffy-null count is lower with no sign of increased infection risk in one review.

From the card

Many people of African ancestry are Duffy-null, an inherited blood-cell variant that gives a lower neutrophil count (the white cells that fight infection). The main study calls it clinically insignificant. The risk is being misread: a normal count for you can be flagged as low.
