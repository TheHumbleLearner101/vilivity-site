Question

What are the first signs and symptoms of fibroids?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Many have no symptoms. See a doctor for heavy or painful periods, tummy or back pain, needing to pee often, pain during sex, trouble peeing or pooing, or a swollen tummy that is not going down; urgently for bleeding between periods or after sex, or sudden severe pelvic pain.

What this is based on

  • Fibroids are non-cancerous growths in or around the womb, and by 50 more than eight in ten Black women in one US study had them. Many have no symptoms and need no treatment.
