Question

What is a race-neutral lung function equation?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

It is one set of equations to predict normal lung function that does not require choosing a race. The 2022 GLI-Global race-neutral equations were built by reanalysing the original data from 74,185 people, and they fitted about as well as the older 'other' equations, with wider limits of normal. The American Thoracic Society (US) says the effects and possible harms of the change are uncertain.

From the card

A spirometry test measures how much air you can blow out in one breath. For years the machine compared Black patients with a lower predicted normal than white patients, which can hide lung disease. Guidelines now recommend one race-neutral set of equations. The test is still useful, and your symptoms matter as much as the number.
