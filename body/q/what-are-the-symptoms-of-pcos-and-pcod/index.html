Question

what are the symptoms of pcos and pcod

  • Tier B thin Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Signs of PCOS include irregular or missing periods with excess hair growth, acne or thick dark patches of skin on the neck or armpits. In the UK, a GP may arrange hormone and insulin resistance blood tests and, if you are over 18, a scan, so ask. How these skin and hair signs are recognised on dark skin is untested.

From the card

Heavy or painful periods are not something you have to accept. Fibroids are clearly more common in Black women. Endometriosis was the same or lower in US studies, though the data are unclear, and PCOS (now PMOS on the NHS) looked milder in one US trial cohort. See a GP with notes of your symptoms.
