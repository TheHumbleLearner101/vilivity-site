Question

Is a low neutrophil count normal for Black people?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Many people of African ancestry are Duffy-null, an inherited variant that gives a lower neutrophil count, so a lower count can be normal for you. In one US clinic study of 120 healthy Black adults, the authors called it clinically insignificant. The risk is being misread: a normal count for you can be flagged as low. See a GP (UK) if a low count comes with fevers, repeated infections, mouth ulcers or feeling unwell.

  • Who was tested: 120 healthy self-identified Black and African American adults at one US primary care centre. What was found: 66.7% (80 of 120) were Duffy-null. Median absolute neutrophil count was 2,820 cells per microlitre in Duffy-null people against 5,005 in Duffy non-null (P<.001). 23.8% (19 of 80) of Duffy-null people had a count below 2,000, against none of the non-null. The authors state the Duffy-null phenotype is clinically insignificant and advocate Duffy-null-specific reference ranges.Tested Merz 2023 Study page
