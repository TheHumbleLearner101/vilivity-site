Question

Why do ACE inhibitors cause swelling in Black patients?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Angioedema (swelling) from ACE inhibitors is uncommon: 0.68% in a 12,557-person enalapril trial, with Black race raising the odds 2.88 times. In Cape Town, 53% of people with ACE inhibitor angioedema had Black genetic ancestry against 29% of controls, and genetic studies suggest shared genetic risk. Get emergency help for swelling of the lips, tongue, face or throat (UK 999, US and Canada 911, Australia 000).

What this is based on

  • Genes change how fast your body breaks down some medicines. The variable is the gene, and some versions are more common in people of African ancestry. Examples are ACE inhibitor tablets, which cause face or throat swelling more often in Black patients, and warfarin, whose dose needs gene variants the first formulas missed.From the card's plain answer
  • In 12,557 people with hypertension given enalapril, angioedema occurred in 0.68%; black race raised the odds 2.88 times (95% CI 1.72 to 4.82).Tested Kostis 2005
  • In Cape Town, 237 people with ACE inhibitor angioedema were compared with 466 who tolerated the tablets for two years: 53% of cases had Black genetic ancestry against 29% of controls.Tested Day 2025
  • Swelling of the lips, tongue, face or throat, or trouble breathing, while on blood pressure tablets: emergency services now (NHS says throat swelling is a medical emergency; UK 999, US and Canada 911, Australia 000).When to see someone
  • In a genetic study of more than 1,000 European patients, the lead risk variants had similar effect sizes and direction in an African-American cohort.Tested Mathey 2024
