Home / Body / Pregnancy risk and safe maternity care for Black women

Question

Are Black women more likely to die in pregnancy in England or the UK?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

In England, women from Black ethnic backgrounds had nearly three times the risk of maternal death of White women, in the latest national report (MBRRACE-UK, 2022-24; rate ratio 2.72). The ratio is for England only, because ethnicity denominators exist only there. A confidential enquiry into earlier deaths found bias in the care some women received, including a lack of nuanced care among Black women who died.

  • Who was tested: All maternal deaths in the UK, 2022-24: 252 deaths among 1,969,321 maternities. What was found: The UK maternal death rate (during pregnancy or within 42 days) was 12.80 per 100,000 maternities. In England only, because denominators by ethnicity exist only there, women from Black ethnic backgrounds had nearly three times the risk of maternal death of White women (rate ratio 2.72, 95% CI 1.80-4.00, P<0.001). Asian women had a non-significantly higher risk. Thrombosis and thromboembolism were the leading cause of death, then cardiac disease; psychiatric causes accounted for 33% of late maternal deaths (six weeks to one year after pregnancy). Women in the most deprived areas had twice the rate of those in the least deprived.Tested MBRRACE-UK 2022-24 triennium (data brief)
  • Who was tested: 1,894 women who died during or up to a year after pregnancy in the UK, 2009-18; care of 54 re-examined (18 Black, 19 Asian, 17 White). What was found: Causes of death did not differ greatly by ethnic group; cardiovascular disease was the leading cause in all. Multiple areas of bias were found in care, including lack of nuanced care (notable among Black women who died) and microaggressions (most prominent among Asian women). The authors conclude that multiple structural and other biases exist in UK maternity care.Tested Knight 2022 Study page
