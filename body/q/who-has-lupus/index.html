Question

Who has lupus?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

In the Manhattan registry, lupus was about 9 times more common in women than in men. Among women it was more common in Black women than in white women, with prevalence of 210.9 against 64.3 per 100,000. It is a long-term autoimmune condition that causes joint pain, rashes and deep tiredness. Those figures are from one US county.

What this is based on

  • Lupus is a long-term autoimmune condition that causes joint pain, rashes and deep tiredness.   • Who was tested: Residents of New York County (Manhattan) with lupus, 2007 prevalence and 2007 to 2009 incidence, by race and ethnicity. What was found: Age-standardised prevalence was 62.2 and incidence 4.6 per 100,000 person-years by ACR criteria, about 9 times higher in women than men. Prevalence in non-Hispanic Black women was 210.9 per 100,000 against 64.3 in non-Hispanic white women, 138.3 in Hispanic and 91.2 in non-Hispanic Asian women. Incidence was 15.7 in Black women against 6.5 in white and Hispanic women and 6.6 in Asian women.Tested Izmirly 2017 Study page
