Question

What is the difference between sickle cell trait and sickle cell disease?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Sickle cell disease is a lifelong inherited blood condition. Trait means you carry one copy of the gene: you do not have the disease and cannot develop it. If both parents carry the gene, a child can have the disease. If you are not sure whether you carry it and are planning a pregnancy, ask a GP in the UK for a blood test.
