Question

How is sickle cell disease managed day to day (diet, pain, sport)?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Drink plenty of fluids and avoid sudden temperature changes, including very cold water, to help prevent a crisis. Try gentle to moderate exercise such as walking or yoga; the NHS in the UK says avoid high-intensity exercise for the disease. Keep up with vaccinations and check-ups. Avoid alcohol and smoking. In the UK, call 999 for severe pain, breathing difficulty or a high temperature.

From the card

Sickle cell disease is a lifelong inherited blood condition; trait means you carry one copy of the gene and do not have the disease and cannot develop it. If both parents carry it, a child can have the disease. In pregnancy, carriers had low risks in a review, but more anaemia and bacteria in the urine, so ask about checks.
