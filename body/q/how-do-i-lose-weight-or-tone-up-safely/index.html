Question

How do I lose weight or tone up safely?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Lose weight steadily, about 0.5 to 1 kg a week, with a balanced diet and activity. Eat plenty of fruit and vegetables and fewer high-fat or sugary foods, cut down on alcohol, and be active for 45 to 60 minutes a day if you are aiming to lose weight; shorter sessions count. Toning and muscle gain are untested here.

What this is based on

  • Eat a balanced diet with plenty of fruit and vegetables and fewer high-fat or sugary foods.Clinical guidance NHS, "Overweight and obesity in adults"
  • Aim to lose weight steadily, about 0.5 to 1 kg a week.Clinical guidance NHS, "Overweight and obesity in adults"
  • Get active for 45 to 60 minutes a day if you are aiming to lose weight; shorter sessions count.Clinical guidance NHS, "Overweight and obesity in adults"
  • Cut down on alcohol, which is high in calories.Clinical guidance NHS, "Overweight and obesity in adults"
  • Untested or not opened: NICE (UK) NG246 evidence for the ethnic-specific BMI cut-offs (the page I opened did not show them); body composition at equal BMI in Black adults; weight-loss medicines and surgery in Black adults (the UK's NHS gives lower BMI thresholds for surgery, 37.5, or 32.5 to 37.4 with a related condition, but I opened no study); muscle gain and "toning". The "get active 45 to 60 minutes" line is NHS weight-loss advice, not the general 150-minute target.Not tested, and gaps (from the card)

From the card

The NHS says people from Black African or African-Caribbean backgrounds are at risk of health problems at a slightly lower BMI: 23 to 27.4 is overweight and 27.5 or more is obese. Lose weight steadily, about half to one kilogram a week, with a balanced diet and activity. BMI cannot tell muscle from fat.
