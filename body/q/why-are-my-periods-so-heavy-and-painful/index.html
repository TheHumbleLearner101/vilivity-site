Home / Body / PCOS, endometriosis and heavy periods

Question

why are my periods so heavy and painful

  • Tier B thin Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Heavy periods can be a sign of fibroids, endometriosis, PMOS or, rarely, womb cancer, so do not assume they are normal. In the UK, see a GP if they affect your life, you bleed between periods or after sex, or the pain is severe. If bleeding is heavy, ask for a blood test for iron deficiency anaemia.

From the card

Heavy or painful periods are not something you have to accept. Fibroids are clearly more common in Black women. Endometriosis was the same or lower in US studies, though the data are unclear, and PCOS (now PMOS on the NHS) looked milder in one US trial cohort. See a GP with notes of your symptoms.
