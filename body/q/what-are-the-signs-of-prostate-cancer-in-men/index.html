Home / Body / Prostate cancer risk and PSA testing

Question

what are the signs of prostate cancer in men

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Go to a GP if you pee more often, urgently or at night, have blood in your pee, or erection problems, according to the NHS (UK). Blood in your pee needs a GP soon. A raised PSA result is not a diagnosis: expect a repeat test and possibly referral for a biopsy or MRI.

From the card

In England, about one in four Black men will be diagnosed with prostate cancer in their lifetime, against about one in eight White men. There is no routine NHS PSA screening for Black men: the UK National Screening Committee advised against it in 2026. You can still ask your GP about a PSA test and weigh the risks and benefits.
