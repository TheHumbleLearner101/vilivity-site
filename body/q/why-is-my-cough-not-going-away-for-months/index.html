Question

why is my cough not going away for months

  • Tier B thin Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

See a doctor about a dry cough or breathlessness lasting more than a few weeks, and ask whether a chest X-ray is needed. Long coughs are often put down to a chest infection, asthma or tiredness. Sarcoidosis is one possible cause, so a persistent cough deserves a check, and you should not wait years with symptoms.

From the card

Sarcoidosis is a rare condition where small patches of swollen tissue form, most often in the lungs, skin and lymph nodes. In studies it is more common, and in some studies more severe, in Black adults. Many cases settle without treatment, but a lasting cough, breathlessness or sore red eyes need a doctor.
