Home / Body / Vitamin D and dark skin

Question

do black people need more vitamin d

  • Tier A moderate Tested in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

UK advice says people with dark skin should consider a daily 10 microgram supplement, but the evidence disagrees: total vitamin D is lower in Black Americans, so is the protein that carries it, and one study found the usable amount similar. Two trials in older Black American women found supplements did not slow bone loss.

What this is based on

  • Who was tested: 2,085 Black and White adults in Baltimore (Healthy Aging in Neighborhoods of Diversity across the Life Span). What was found: Total 25-hydroxyvitamin D was 15.6 ng/ml in Black participants against 25.8 in White. Vitamin D-binding protein was also lower (168 against 337 ug/ml). In people homozygous for the same genotype, bioavailable 25(OH)D was similar (2.9 against 3.1 ng/ml). Bone mineral density was higher in Black participants (1.05 against 0.94 g/cm2).Tested Powe 2013 Study page
  • Total vitamin D in the blood is lower in Black Americans, but so is the protein that carries it, and one study found the usable amount similar. Two trials in older Black American women found supplements did not slow bone loss. UK advice still says dark-skinned people should consider a daily supplement.
