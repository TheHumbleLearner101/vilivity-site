Home / Body / Colonoscopy: when to start and how often

Question

How often should you have a colonoscopy in your 50s?

  • Tier B moderate Tested, but not in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-17
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

In England, the NHS starts at 50 with a home stool test every 2 years; a colonoscopy follows a positive result.

Also asked as

  • When should you get your first colonoscopy?
  • What are the recommended repeat colonoscopy guidelines?

From the card

In the US, screening starts at 45 and a colonoscopy every 10 years is one way to do it. In England, the NHS starts at 50 with a home stool test every 2 years; a colonoscopy follows a positive result. Our search found no study showing Black adults should start before 45. Symptoms, polyps or family history change timing.
