Home / Body / Lactose intolerance and dairy

Question

Why does milk give me stomach pain?

  • Tier B thin Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Lactose intolerance means tummy pain, bloating and diarrhoea after eating or drinking something with lactose, a sugar in animal milk. The NHS (UK) says it is usually caused by the body not making enough lactase, the enzyme that digests lactose. See a GP if tummy pain, bloating or loose stools after dairy keep happening.

From the card

Not every Black person is lactose intolerant, and there is no race rule. Whether you keep making lactase, the enzyme that digests milk sugar, depends on your genes, and several separate gene variants that keep it switched on are known in East African groups. Symptoms after dairy are checked with a food trial or a breath test.
