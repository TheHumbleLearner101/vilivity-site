Question

Why are some periods worse than others?

  • Tier B thin Observed in Black populations
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-17
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

In a small US study, about two thirds of 44 African American women had iron deficiency; about half reported heavy periods.

From the card

The NHS (UK) says heavy periods can be normal after pregnancy or approaching menopause; ACOG (US) says heavy bleeding is not normal. Either way, get it checked if bleeding disrupts your life or you feel tired or breathless.
