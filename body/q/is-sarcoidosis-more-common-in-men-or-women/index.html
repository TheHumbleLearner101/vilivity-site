Question

is sarcoidosis more common in men or women

  • Tier B thin Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

A review of the published literature says African Americans and women have the highest incidence of sarcoidosis, with more advanced presentations and higher death rates, and African American women have the highest mortality. The author says the differences may reflect healthcare disparities and income more than genetics or biology alone.

What this is based on

From the card

Sarcoidosis is a rare condition where small patches of swollen tissue form, most often in the lungs, skin and lymph nodes. In studies it is more common, and in some studies more severe, in Black adults. Many cases settle without treatment, but a lasting cough, breathlessness or sore red eyes need a doctor.
