Question

Are fibroids more common in Black women?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Fibroids are very common in Black women and start earlier than in White women. In a US ultrasound study of 1,364 women, more than 80% of Black women had them by age 50, against nearly 70% of White women. In a study of 1,693 African American women aged 23 to 35, women with existing fibroids were more than twice as likely to develop new ones.

What this is based on

  • Who was tested: 1,364 randomly selected women aged 35-49 in a US urban health plan (Black and White). What was found: By age 50, cumulative incidence of fibroids was above 80% for Black women and nearly 70% for White women (odds ratio 2.9, 95% CI 2.5-3.4). Fibroids developed at earlier ages in Black women. Fifty-one percent of premenopausal women with no previous diagnosis had ultrasound evidence of fibroids.Tested Baird 2003 Study page
  • Who was tested: 1,693 African American women aged 23-35 with no prior fibroid diagnosis. What was found: Among 1,123 fibroid-free women with follow-up, incidence was 9.4% over about 18 months, rising from 6% at ages 23-25 to 13% at 32-35. Women with existing fibroids were more than twice as likely to develop new ones (relative risk 2.3). Average fibroid growth was 89% per 18 months.Tested Baird 2020 Study page
