Question

When should Black people start bowel or colon cancer screening?

  • Tier B moderate Tested, but not in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

In England the NHS posts a home stool test (FIT) every 2 years to people aged 50 to 74. In the US, screening is recommended from 45 to 49 (a B recommendation) and strongly recommended from 50 to 75 (an A recommendation). Our search found no trial or review testing whether Black adults should start earlier. See a GP if a close relative had bowel cancer, especially before 50.
