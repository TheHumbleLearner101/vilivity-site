Question

what are the symptoms of high blood pressure

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

High blood pressure does not usually cause any symptoms, so feeling well does not mean your reading is fine. In the UK, get it checked at a pharmacy or GP surgery. A high reading with a severe headache, blurred vision or chest pain needs NHS 111 urgently, or 999 for chest pain that does not go away.

From the card

The line for high blood pressure is the same for every adult. In England, blood pressure in people of African-Caribbean origin overtakes White people's at around 30 to 40 (about 28 for men's systolic pressure), so being young and fit does not rule it out. Get it checked. If you need tablets, NICE names a different first choice for you.
