Question

how do you test sickle cell

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

In the UK, if you are pregnant, take the blood test your midwife offers before 10 weeks. Anyone can ask a GP for a carrier test at any time. If you are not sure whether you carry the gene and are planning a pregnancy, ask a GP for a blood test.

From the card

Sickle cell disease is a lifelong inherited blood condition; trait means you carry one copy of the gene and do not have the disease and cannot develop it. If both parents carry it, a child can have the disease. In pregnancy, carriers had low risks in a review, but more anaemia and bacteria in the urine, so ask about checks.
