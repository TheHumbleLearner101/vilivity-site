Home / Body / Creatine supplements: strength, kidneys and your kidney test

Question

Can creatine change my kidney blood test (creatinine, eGFR)?

  • Tier B moderate Tested, but not in people like you
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Creatine can raise serum creatinine without harming your kidneys, so tell any clinician who reads your kidney blood tests that you take it. A low kidney reading from a creatinine-based test may not be real if that is the only evidence, so ask whether another test is needed. If your creatinine or eGFR looks abnormal on creatine, see a doctor and ask about repeating it off creatine or a cystatin C test.

  • Who was tested: Adults in 21 studies (12 in the creatinine meta-analysis: 177 on creatine, 263 controls). What was found: Creatine produced a small, statistically significant rise in serum creatinine, with no significant change in glomerular filtration rate (GFR). The authors call the creatinine rise modest and probably due to metabolic turnover with no sign of kidney impairment.Tested Naeini 2025 Study page

From the card

Creatine helps strength gains when you train with weights, in trials of mostly young men, and reviews found no kidney harm in healthy adults. Our search found no creatine trial in Black participants. It raises the creatinine kidney marker, so tell anyone reading your kidney tests, and check with a doctor first if you have kidney problems.
