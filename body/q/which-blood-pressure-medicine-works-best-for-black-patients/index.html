Home / Body / High blood pressure in Black adults

Question

Which blood pressure medicine works best for Black patients?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

In England, NICE suggests a calcium-channel blocker as the first tablet for Black African or African-Caribbean adults without type 2 diabetes. Your prescriber decides what you take. We read the NICE recommendations, not the trial evidence behind them, so we cannot say how it compares with other tablets.

What this is based on
