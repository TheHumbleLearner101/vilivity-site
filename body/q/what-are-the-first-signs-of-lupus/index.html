Home / Body / Lupus: symptoms, risk and getting diagnosed early

Question

What are the first signs of lupus?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Lupus symptoms include joint and muscle pain, tiredness that rest does not fix, and a rash after sun. In 61 people with lupus in Casablanca, fatigue (82.0%), joint pain (70.5%) and malar rash (59.0%) were most common. Do not wait for one classic rash, because lupus has many possible symptoms. See a GP (UK) if they keep returning for weeks, and ask about lupus tests.

What this is based on

  • Lupus is a long-term autoimmune condition that causes joint pain, rashes and deep tiredness. It is more common in women of African and Caribbean background and can affect the kidneys, so finding it early matters. See a GP if symptoms keep coming back.
