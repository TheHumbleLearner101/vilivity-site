Question

Why is my warfarin dose hard to set?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Studies of warfarin dose check the VKORC1 and CYP2C9 genes, but in African Americans these two explain less of the dose variation, so another variant on chromosome 10 was identified. In 533 African American adults on stable warfarin, a new variant in the CYP2C cluster was linked to dose and replicated in 432 more. Ask your GP or anticoagulant clinic whether gene-guided dosing is offered.

From the card

Genes change how fast your body breaks down some medicines. The variable is the gene, and some versions are more common in people of African ancestry. Examples are ACE inhibitor tablets, which cause face or throat swelling more often in Black patients, and warfarin, whose dose needs gene variants the first formulas missed.
