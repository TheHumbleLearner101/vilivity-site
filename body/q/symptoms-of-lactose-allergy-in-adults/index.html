Question

symptoms of lactose allergy in adults

  • Tier B thin Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Lactose intolerance is not a food allergy. An allergy can cause swelling and breathing difficulty and needs 999 (UK). Call 999 (UK) for swelling of the lips, mouth, throat or tongue, fast breathing or a tight throat after eating. Lactose intolerance gives tummy pain, bloating or diarrhoea after eating or drinking something with lactose.

From the card

Not every Black person is lactose intolerant, and there is no race rule. Whether you keep making lactase, the enzyme that digests milk sugar, depends on your genes, and several separate gene variants that keep it switched on are known in East African groups. Symptoms after dairy are checked with a food trial or a breath test.
