Home / Body / Lung function tests and race correction in spirometry

Question

Do lung function tests use a different normal for Black people?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Until recently, spirometry standards promoted equations that also used self-reported race and ethnicity, so for years the machine compared Black patients with a lower predicted normal than white patients. The American Thoracic Society (US) now recommends replacing them with race-neutral equations. Which equations UK, African, Caribbean and other national services use is something our search found no list of. Ask which equation was used for your result.

  • Who was tested: Official American Thoracic Society workshop (2021) and review of newer evidence. What was found: Current ATS standards promoted race- and ethnicity-specific reference equations; concern grew that this gives a false view of fixed differences and may mask differential exposures and norm health disparities. The statement recommends replacing them with race-neutral average reference equations, alongside a broader re-evaluation of how lung function tests are used for clinical, employment and insurance decisions, a call to include missing stakeholders, and a caution about uncertain effects and potential harms.Tested Bhakta 2023 Study page
