Question

what are the symptoms of heat stroke

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Hot skin without sweating, a very high temperature, fast breathing, confusion or a seizure are signs of heatstroke: in the UK, call 999. Heat exhaustion that has not eased after 30 minutes of cooling down needs NHS 111. A change in skin colour can be harder to see on brown or black skin, so do not rely on it.

What this is based on

  • Hot skin without sweating, a very high temperature, fast breathing, confusion or a seizure (call 999 in the UK, or your local emergency number: heatstroke).When to see someone
  • Heat exhaustion that has not eased after 30 minutes of cooling down (NHS 111 in the UK).When to see someone
  • Relying on skin colour to spot heat exhaustion: the UK's NHS says a change in skin colour can be harder to see on brown or black skin.Clinical guidance NHS, "Heat exhaustion and heatstroke"

From the card

I found no study that shows Black people tolerate heat better or sweat differently. What exists is old, small, and in men who were already acclimatised. Heat risk follows fitness, acclimatisation, body size and exposure. Follow the UK's NHS heat advice, and take extra care if you or your child has sickle cell disease or trait.
