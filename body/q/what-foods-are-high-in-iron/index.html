Home / Body / Iron, heavy periods and fibroids

Question

What foods are high in iron?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Iron-rich foods include leafy greens, pulses and fortified cereals. Tea and coffee with meals reduce absorption. Food is general advice here: whether diet or any home remedy helps heavy periods from fibroids in Black women is untested. If periods are heavy or you are exhausted, ask a doctor for a full blood count.

From the card

Fibroids are very common in Black women and start earlier than in White women. Heavy periods can drain iron, and in a small US pilot most African American women had low iron. Do not treat tiredness or heavy bleeding yourself: ask a doctor for a blood test.
