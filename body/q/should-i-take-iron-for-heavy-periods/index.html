Question

Should I take iron for heavy periods?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Ask for a full blood count first if periods are heavy or you are exhausted, and ask about iron stores (ferritin) too: in a small US pilot of 44 African American women, low iron was common without anaemia. The NHS in the UK has a GP recommend iron tablets once a test shows low levels. Taking them without a test was not studied here, so do not treat tiredness or heavy bleeding yourself.

Fibroids are very common in Black women and start earlier than in White women. Heavy periods can drain iron, and in a small US pilot most African American women had low iron. Do not treat tiredness or heavy bleeding yourself: ask a doctor for a blood test.
