Question

How do I find out if I am lactose intolerant?

  • Tier B thin Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

A GP may suggest a lactose elimination diet or a hydrogen breath test, and blood tests are sometimes used (NHS, UK). See a GP if symptoms keep coming back, or if you have bloating, loose stools or weight loss for 3 weeks. See a GP too if you notice blood in your poo, or weight loss without trying.

From the card

Not every Black person is lactose intolerant, and there is no race rule. Whether you keep making lactase, the enzyme that digests milk sugar, depends on your genes, and several separate gene variants that keep it switched on are known in East African groups. Symptoms after dairy are checked with a food trial or a breath test.
