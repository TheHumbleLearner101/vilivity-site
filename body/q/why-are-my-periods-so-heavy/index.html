Question

Why are my periods so heavy?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Fibroids are listed by the NHS in the UK as a cause of heavy periods, and they are very common in Black women. Note how heavy your periods are and how tired you feel, and take it to a doctor. See a GP if periods are so heavy they affect daily life or last a long time, or for bleeding between periods or after sex, or pelvic pain.

From the card

Fibroids are very common in Black women and start earlier than in White women. Heavy periods can drain iron, and in a small US pilot most African American women had low iron. Do not treat tiredness or heavy bleeding yourself: ask a doctor for a blood test.
