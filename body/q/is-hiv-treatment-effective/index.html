Question

Is HIV treatment effective?

  • Tier A strong Tested in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

HIV treatment is effective and available, so knowing your status is a good thing. WHO says people who take treatment and have no virus in the blood will not pass HIV on sexually. After a positive test, ask for a same-week appointment at an HIV clinic (where you live, ask the testing service).

What this is based on

  • PrEP is a medicine you take before a possible exposure to HIV, and it is highly effective when taken as prescribed. It comes as tablets, injections or a vaginal ring depending on where you live. Get an HIV test first and then regularly. HIV treatment is effective, so knowing your status is a good thing.From the card's plain answer
