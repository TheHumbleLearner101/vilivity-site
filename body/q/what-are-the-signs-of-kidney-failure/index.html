Question

what are the signs of kidney failure

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Early kidney disease usually has no symptoms, so many people only learn something is wrong when it is advanced. See a doctor for blood in your pee, swollen ankles or feet, or breathlessness that is new. If you have high blood pressure or diabetes and no kidney blood or urine test in the last year or so, ask for one.

What this is based on

  • Two variants of the APOL1 gene, common on African chromosomes, raise the risk of some kidney diseases in African Americans. Early kidney disease usually has no symptoms.
