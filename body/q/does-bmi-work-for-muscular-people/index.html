Question

Does BMI work for muscular people?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

The NHS in the UK notes that BMI cannot tell muscle from fat. If you carry a lot of muscle, it may put you in the overweight or obese category without much body fat, so a muscular person can have a high BMI and you should not rely on BMI alone. Lifters say BMI is useless.
