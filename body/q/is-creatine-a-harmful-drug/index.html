Home / Body / Creatine supplements: strength, kidneys and your kidney test

Question

is creatine a harmful drug

  • Tier B moderate Tested, but not in people like you
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Reviews found no kidney harm from creatine in healthy adults, though it raises the creatinine kidney marker. NCCIH reports claims of liver and kidney effects and a link with compartment syndrome. Teenagers should avoid it: NCCIH says no safety data exist in children. See urgent care for severe muscle pain, swelling or very dark urine after hard exercise.

What this is based on

  • Creatine helps strength gains when you train with weights, in trials of mostly young men, and reviews found no kidney harm in healthy adults. Our search found no creatine trial in Black participants.
