Question

How do I stay safe training in hot weather?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Drink extra fluids in hot weather, and have cold drinks and cold food. Stay in the shade and avoid exercise or hard activity between 11am and 3pm. Avoid alcohol, caffeine and hot drinks during a heatwave. If someone has heat exhaustion, move them somewhere cool, remove extra clothing and give plenty of water.

From the card

I found no study that shows Black people tolerate heat better or sweat differently. What exists is old, small, and in men who were already acclimatised. Heat risk follows fitness, acclimatisation, body size and exposure. Follow the UK's NHS heat advice, and take extra care if you or your child has sickle cell disease or trait.
