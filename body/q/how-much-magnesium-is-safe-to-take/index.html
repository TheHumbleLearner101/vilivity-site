Home / Body / Magnesium supplements for sleep and cramps

Question

How much magnesium is safe to take?

  • Tier B thin Tested, but not in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

The NHS (England) says up to 400 mg a day from supplements is unlikely to cause harm. Higher doses can cause diarrhoea, and long-term effects are not known. Food comes first: the NHS (England) says a varied, balanced diet should give you all the magnesium you need. Magnesium is in leafy greens, nuts, beans and whole grains.

From the card

Magnesium tablets did not clearly prevent leg cramps in older adults, and for sleep three small, low-quality trials in older adults hint at falling asleep a little faster. Our search found no supplement trial in Black participants. Up to four hundred milligrams a day from supplements is unlikely to harm; more can cause diarrhoea.
