Home / Body / Stroke: signs to act on and what raises the risk

Question

What are the unhealthy habits that can increase the risk of stroke?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

In a West African study of 2,118 stroke cases, current smoking carried odds of 4.42, adding salt at the table 2.14, physical inactivity 2.13, low green leafy vegetable intake 2.43, stress 1.89 and regular meat consumption 1.59. Quit smoking, eat a balanced diet, be active and cut down on alcohol.

What this is based on

  • Adding salt at the table: in a West African study it was linked to about double the odds of stroke.Tested Owolabi 2018
  • Smoking: in the same study current smokers had over four times the odds of stroke.Tested Owolabi 2018
  • Who was tested: 2,118 adults with stroke confirmed by CT or MRI and 2,118 age- and gender-matched stroke-free controls at 15 sites in Nigeria and Ghana, 2014 to 2017. What was found: Eleven modifiable risk factors accounted for 98.2% of stroke risk. Hypertension had odds ratio 19.36 (95% CI 12.11 to 30.93) and a population-attributable risk of 90.8%. Dyslipidaemia (1.85), regular meat consumption (1.59), a raised waist-to-hip ratio (1.48), diabetes (2.58), low green leafy vegetable intake (2.43), stress (1.89), added salt at the table (2.14), cardiac disease (1.65), physical inactivity (2.13) and current smoking (4.42) followed. Of the cases, 68% had ischaemic and 32% haemorrhagic stroke.Tested Owolabi 2018 Study page
  • Do not wait for symptoms to check your risk: in one South London register, 12% of strokes in Black African participants, at a median age of 59, came with no earlier risk factor diagnosis.Tested Emmett 2026

From the card

A stroke is an emergency: a drooping face, a weak arm, slurred speech, then call emergency services at once, even if it passes. Black adults have strokes more often and younger in the studies we found, and high blood pressure is the biggest risk. Having your blood pressure checked and treated is the most useful step.
