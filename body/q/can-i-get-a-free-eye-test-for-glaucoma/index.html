Question

Can I get a free eye test for glaucoma?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

In the UK, ask for a free NHS sight test if you are 40 or over and a parent, brother, sister or child has glaucoma. The NHS list does not include ethnicity as a reason. Ask an optician for the free test, and do not wait for symptoms, as glaucoma usually has none and develops slowly over years.

From the card

Glaucoma is more common in Black populations at every age, and often has no symptoms until sight is lost. A pooled analysis found the highest prevalence in Black populations at all ages. Have an eye test at least every two years, and ask whether you qualify for a free NHS (UK) test, for example if a close relative has glaucoma.
