Home / Body / Glaucoma and eye tests

Question

what are the signs of glaucoma in your eyes

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Glaucoma often has no symptoms until sight is already lost, and it develops slowly over years. It is usually found during a routine eye test, so have one at least every 2 years instead of waiting for signs. In the UK, a free NHS test is available from 40 if a parent, brother, sister or child has glaucoma.

What this is based on

  • Glaucoma is more common in Black populations at every age, and often has no symptoms until sight is lost. A pooled analysis found the highest prevalence in Black populations at all ages. Have an eye test at least every two years, and ask whether you qualify for a free NHS (UK) test, for example if a close relative has glaucoma.From the card's plain answer
