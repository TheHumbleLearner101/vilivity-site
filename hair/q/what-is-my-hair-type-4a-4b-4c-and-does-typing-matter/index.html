Question

What is my hair type (4a, 4b, 4c) and does typing matter?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

The 4a, 4b and 4c letters sort curl by eye and were not checked against measured hair, so typing does not predict which product or routine will work. Describe your hair by how tight the curl is, how wide the strand is and how dense it is. Treat the letter as a label for how hair looks.
