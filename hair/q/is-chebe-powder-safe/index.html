Question

Is chebe powder safe?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Our search found no study of chebe powder's safety. PureWow quotes a doctor who says its side effects are unknown and advises a patch test, and a stylist who warns it is heavy on thin strands. See a GP or dermatologist (UK) if you get itching, a rash or burning on your scalp after using it.

From the card

Chebe is a traditional herbal hair powder, mixed with oil or butter and coated on the lengths. My search found no study of it on hair or in people. A doctor quoted by PureWow says it does not grow hair. Any benefit is untested, so patch test first and avoid heavy coating on thin strands.
