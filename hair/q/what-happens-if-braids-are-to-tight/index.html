Question

what happens if braids are to tight

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Pain after installation means pull on the scalp, and braids that hurt are too tight. In a South African study of 1,178 schoolgirls and women, traction alopecia was present in 17.1% and 31.7%, and the odds were highest when traction was added to relaxed hair (odds ratio 3.47 against natural hair). This shows association, not cause. Avoid tight braids on relaxed hair, and see a GP if hair thins at the edges or temples.
