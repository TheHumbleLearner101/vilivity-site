Question

Are keratin and Brazilian blowout treatments safe for Black hair?

  • Tier B thin Observed in Black populations
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Keratin smoothing products can release formaldehyde. In a simulated salon on wigs, airborne formaldehyde averaged 0.08 to 3.47 ppm during blow-drying, so avoid keratin smoothing in a poorly ventilated space. This measured air, not health outcomes. If you get eye, nose or breathing irritation during or after a treatment, see a doctor.

What this is based on

  • Heat damage on afro hair has not been measured in people. Dermatologists advise a heat protectant, the lowest setting that works and no more than one press a week. One small survey links frequent heat to hairline loss.   • Keratin smoothing in a poorly ventilated space: simulated salon air held formaldehyde.Tested Pierce 2011
  • Who was tested: Four professional keratin smoothing products applied to wigs on mannequins in a simulated salon. What was found: Airborne formaldehyde averaged 0.08 to 3.47 ppm during blow-drying and 0.08 to 1.05 ppm during flat-ironing. Bulk samples held 11.5% formaldehyde in Brazilian Blowout, 8.3% in Global Keratin, 3% in Coppola and 0% in La Brasiliana, and other products that break down into formaldehyde were found in three of the four.Tested Pierce 2011 Study page
