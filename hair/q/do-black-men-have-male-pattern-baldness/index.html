Home / Hair / Receding hairline and hair transplants in Black men

Question

Do Black men have male pattern baldness?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Inherited pattern baldness is one cause of receding hairlines. In 2,136 men from Ghana, Nigeria, Senegal and South Africa, genetic scores from European studies predicted self-reported baldness poorly, so do not assume such a test applies to you. Treatment response in Black men is untested.

What this is based on

  • Receding hairlines have several causes: inherited pattern baldness, pulling from tight styles, or scarring conditions such as CCCA, which men can get. Minoxidil and finasteride are the standard treatments but I found no trial in Black men. Transplants can be done on curly hair but need a specialist and are private in the UK.From the card's plain answer
