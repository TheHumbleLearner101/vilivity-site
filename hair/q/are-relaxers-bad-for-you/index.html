Home / Hair / Chemical relaxers and hair straighteners

Question

Are relaxers bad for you?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Studies link frequent relaxer use to uterine cancer and fibroids, but they disagree and none can prove cause; breast cancer results were largely null. Relaxed hair under tight styles carried the highest risk of hair loss. Have a professional apply it, space treatments out, and see a doctor or dermatologist about scalp burns that blister, ooze or do not heal.

What this is based on

  • Studies disagree on whether relaxers raise cancer risk, and none can prove cause.
