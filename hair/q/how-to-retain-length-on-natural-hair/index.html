Home / Hair / Hair breakage, growth and keeping length

Question

how to retain length on natural hair

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

No study we found measures length kept under different routines, so none of this is proven. The best-supported step is to detangle gently with as few passes as you can. Use a soft wide-spaced brush or wide-toothed comb, protect hair at night, space out relaxers, heat and braids, and take regular trims and a break between long tight styles (4 to 6 weeks).

From the card

Tightly coiled hair does grow, but it breaks more easily when combed or handled, so the length you keep depends on breakage. Company studies measure slower growth in African hair, but nobody has tested how to keep length. Gentle detangling is the best-supported step. No product is shown to repair a strand.
