Home / Hair / Winter care and shrinkage for coily hair

Question

why does my hair shrink so much when i wash it

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Shrinkage is simply how tightly coiled hair behaves: it springs up much shorter than its stretched length. I found no study of shrinkage, so what drives it, whether moisture, curl or breakage, is untested. Do not treat shrinkage as damage or as proof of health, because no source we opened says either.

What this is based on

  • No study has tested winter care or shrinkage in coily hair. General dermatology advice for winter applies: keep hair moisturised, air dry when you can, limit heat, and space out chemical services.
