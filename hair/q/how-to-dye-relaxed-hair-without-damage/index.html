Home / Hair / Colouring and bleaching afro-textured hair

Question

how to dye relaxed hair without damage

  • Tier D Untested
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Our search found no study of a colour routine in people with coiled hair; these steps are guidance. The NHS (UK) advises a strand test at least 48 hours before dyeing. Leave 2 weeks between a relaxer and dyeing, because dyeing soon after causes cumulative damage (British Association of Dermatologists). Do not stack bleach, straightening and heat on the same hair: combined treatments changed the fibre more than single ones in the lab.

Bleaching and dyeing damage the hair surface whatever the curl, in lab tests on hair samples; no study has tested a colour or bleach routine in people with coiled hair.
