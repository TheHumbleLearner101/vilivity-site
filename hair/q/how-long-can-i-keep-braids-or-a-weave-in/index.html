Home / Hair / Traction alopecia and 'protective' styles

Question

How long can I keep braids or a weave in?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Do not keep braids in longer than 6 to 8 weeks, and rotate styles to give your hair breaks from tight ones. This is guidance, not a tested limit: whether particular styles or lengths of time are safe has not been studied. Avoid tight styles on chemically relaxed hair, the highest-risk combination in the South African study.

What this is based on

  • Untested: which styles and how long; whether 'protective' styles done loose protect against breakage; whether rest periods reverse early loss; any treatment in a trial. Not measured: tension. No RCT found in my search.Not tested, and gaps (from the card)

From the card

Tight braids, weaves and ponytails can cause traction alopecia, hair loss from pulling, and the risk is highest when tension is added to relaxed hair. Caught early it can recover; left, it can become permanent. "Protective" describes the intention: tension is what matters.
