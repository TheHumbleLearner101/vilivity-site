Home / Hair / Twist-outs, braid-outs and wash-and-gos: technique

Question

how long do twist outs last on 4c hair

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

No study has tested how long a twist-out lasts. Tradition says twist-outs and braid-outs, like wash-and-gos, give definition for days. How many days a set lasts is untested, and creator tips disagree. Keep twists and braids loose at the scalp, and see a doctor for an itchy, sore or flaking scalp that does not settle after you take a style out.

What this is based on

From the card

No study has tested how to set a twist-out, braid-out or wash-and-go, or how long one lasts. Creators and stylists give tips and they disagree, for example on starting on wet or stretched hair. The one dermatology point is to keep twists and braids loose at the scalp.
