Question

How do I prepare for and look after braids, twists and cornrows?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Ask for loose installation by a trained professional, and consider leaving the delicate hairline out. Cleanse the scalp between installs with diluted shampoo, condition mainly the lengths, blot dry and let it dry fully. Alternate the direction of parts, avoid heavy beads or very long extensions, and take braids out within 6 weeks and extensions within 8.

From the card

Braids that hurt are too tight; pain after installation means pull on the scalp. Dermatologists advise taking braids out within about six weeks and extensions within eight, giving your scalp a break, and keeping the hairline loose. Itch can come from build-up or an allergy to synthetic hair or glue. "Protective" has never been tested.
