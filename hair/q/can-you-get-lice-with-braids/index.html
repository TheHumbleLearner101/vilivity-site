Home / Hair / Head lice in afro-textured hair and locs

Question

can you get lice with braids

  • Tier B thin Tested or observed (see the evidence)
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Anyone with hair can get head lice, and treatment in braids is untested. The old school surveys that found fewer cases in children with African hair conflict. If you cannot treat lice through braids, ask a pharmacist before cutting anything.

What this is based on
