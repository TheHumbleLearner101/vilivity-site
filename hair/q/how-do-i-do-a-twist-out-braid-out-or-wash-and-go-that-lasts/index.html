Home / Hair / Twist-outs, braid-outs and wash-and-gos: technique

Question

How do I do a twist-out, braid-out or wash-and-go that lasts?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Work product through small, even sections of damp hair so each twist is coated, then twist from the root. A twist-out is set by twisting or plaiting product-coated hair and unravelling it dry. No study has tested how to set one, so these are creator and stylist tips, and they disagree on points such as starting on wet or stretched hair.
