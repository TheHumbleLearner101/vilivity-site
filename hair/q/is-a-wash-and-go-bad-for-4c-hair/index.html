Question

Is a wash-and-go bad for 4c hair?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

One channel says a wash-and-go on tightly coiled hair dries out unless redone every day or two, but that view is not tested, and others disagree. No study has tested how to do a wash-and-go, or how long one lasts, so there is no tested answer for 4c hair either way.

No study has tested how to set a twist-out, braid-out or wash-and-go, or how long one lasts. Creators and stylists give tips and they disagree, for example on starting on wet or stretched hair. The one dermatology point is to keep twists and braids loose at the scalp.
