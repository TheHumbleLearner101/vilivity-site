Question

how to get rid of lice in afro hair

  • Tier B thin Tested or observed (see the evidence)
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

The NHS (England) advises wet combing with conditioner and a fine-toothed comb, or a medicated lotion if combing does not work, only when you find a live louse; with long, frizzy or curly hair allow 20 to 30 minutes. Treatment in afro hair is untested. A US pharmacy commentary (not the NHS, and untested) suggests asking a pharmacist about applying the lotion and skipping the nit comb in tight coils.

What this is based on

Anyone with hair can get head lice. Old school surveys found fewer cases in children with African hair, but they conflict and none looked at locs. Treatment in locs and braids is untested. The UK's NHS advises wet combing or a medicated lotion and does not mention locs; a nit comb may not pass through tight coils.
