Question

Are braids and tight styles safe for children's hair?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Tight braids and updos are linked to traction alopecia in Black girls. In a South African study, 17.1% of 574 schoolgirls had it; risk was highest when traction was added to relaxed hair (schoolgirls and women analysed together; association only). Choose larger braids or locs, fewer extensions, remove extensions at least monthly and allow 4 to 6 weeks between long styles. Safe age for first braiding is untested.
