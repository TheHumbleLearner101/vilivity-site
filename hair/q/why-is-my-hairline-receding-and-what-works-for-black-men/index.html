Question

Why is my hairline receding, and what works for Black men?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

A receding hairline has several causes: inherited pattern baldness, pulling from tight styles, or scarring conditions such as CCCA. Minoxidil 5% and finasteride are the standard treatments, but my search found no trial of them in Black men. The British Association of Dermatologists says minoxidil takes 6 to 12 months and works only while used; finasteride up to 14 months, about 2% report libido or erection problems. See a GP first.
