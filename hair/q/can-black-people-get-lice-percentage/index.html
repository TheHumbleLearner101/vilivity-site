Home / Hair / Head lice in afro-textured hair and locs

Question

can black people get lice percentage

  • Tier B thin Tested or observed (see the evidence)
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

The surveys we found conflict. In a Kenya survey of 1,270 schoolchildren, 17.1 per cent were infested and Negroid children, in the paper's own term, had lower rates. In a 1972 to 1973 Buffalo, New York survey, one school had 20 per cent of white pupils and no Black pupils infested. A US study found 15 per cent of African American pupils infested.

What this is based on

  • Who was tested: 1,270 schoolchildren, 33 urban and rural primary schools, Kenya. What was found: 17.1% were infested (8% with living lice or nits). Negroid children had lower infestation rates than non-Negroid children, and the authors say the criterion seems to be hair type. Infestation was higher in older children, in children with longer hair and in those who washed less; most had one or two lice.Tested Chunge 1986 Study page
  • Who was tested: School pupils in Buffalo, New York, 1972 to 1973. What was found: In one elementary school 20% of white pupils and no Black pupils were infested; city-wide 7.2% of pupils were infested. Hair length was apparently not a factor.Tested Slonka 1977 Study page
  • Who was tested: School children, elementary to high school (US). What was found: Hispanic children had the highest rate of infestation, then Caucasian children. African American children showed a 15% infestation rate, which the authors say is not supported by other studies.Tested Estrada 2000 Study page

Checked against its sources on 2026-11-17.

Also asked as

  • Is it rare for African Americans to get lice?

From the card

Anyone with hair can get head lice. Old school surveys found fewer cases in children with African hair, but they conflict and none looked at locs. Treatment in locs and braids is untested. The UK's NHS advises wet combing or a medicated lotion and does not mention locs; a nit comb may not pass through tight coils.
