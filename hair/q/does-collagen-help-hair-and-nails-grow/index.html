Home / Hair / Collagen for hair, nails and joints

Question

Does collagen help hair and nails grow?

  • Tier B thin Tested, but not in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-17
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

For hair and nails we found a few small trials of branded products with mixed results.

Also asked as

  • Does marine or bovine collagen work better for hair and nails?

From the card

Collagen may ease knee osteoarthritis pain a little in pooled trials, though those trials disagreed with each other. For hair and nails we found a few small trials of branded products with mixed results. Our search found no trial in Black participants. It is not a medicine: for hair loss or sore joints, get the cause checked first.
