Home / Hair / Hair shedding after pregnancy, illness or stress

Question

why is my hair falling out on top of my head

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Thinning at the crown, part or hairline, or a shiny scalp, can be a different condition from temporary shedding, and some women have both. Look at the crown, part and edges rather than assuming it will pass. See a dermatologist about it, and see a GP (UK) or dermatologist if the hair loss comes with burning, itching or pain.

What this is based on

  • A sudden all-over shed, usually three months after a trigger such as childbirth, illness, stress or crash dieting, is called telogen effluvium. It usually gets better on its own within months.
