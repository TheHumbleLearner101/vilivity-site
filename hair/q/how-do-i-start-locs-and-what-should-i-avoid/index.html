Question

How do I start locs and what should I avoid?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Locs are traditionally started by twisting, coiling or interlocking and left to knit over months. No study has tested starter methods. Whichever you use, avoid twisting so tightly that your scalp aches or stings, and do not ignore tenderness, bumps or thinning at the roots or hairline. If hair thins or recedes there, see a doctor (in the UK, your GP).

From the card

No study has tested how often to wash or retwist locs. The British Association of Dermatologists warns that very long locs and tight twisting at the roots strain the scalp and raise the risk of traction alopecia, and that locs must dry fully to avoid mould. Loc-care sites suggest washing every week to month, depending on how much you sweat.
