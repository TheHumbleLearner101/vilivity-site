Question

Do hats damage my hair in winter?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Our search found no study of winter weather and hair breakage in coily hair. General advice from Cleveland Clinic, not specific to coily hair, is to avoid rubber bands and very tight updos under hats. It also says to skip rubber-based bands and avoid tight buns or braids, which cause breakage.

From the card

No study has tested winter care or shrinkage in coily hair. General dermatology advice for winter applies: keep hair moisturised, air dry when you can, limit heat, and space out chemical services. Shrinkage is simply how tightly coiled hair behaves; I found nothing saying it is a problem or a sign of health.
