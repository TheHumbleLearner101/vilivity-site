Question

What is the difference between a relaxer, a texturiser, a perm and a keratin treatment?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

A relaxer straightens, a texturiser loosens curl by being left on for less time, and a perm adds curl; stylists say relaxer and texturiser use the same kind of chemical. Keratin smoothing products are applied with blow-drying and flat-ironing, and some release formaldehyde in a simulated salon on wigs. Keratin outcomes in afro hair are untested.

  • Who was tested: Four professional keratin smoothing products applied to wigs on mannequins in a simulated salon. What was found: Airborne formaldehyde averaged 0.08 to 3.47 ppm during blow-drying and 0.08 to 1.05 ppm during flat-ironing. Bulk samples held 11.5% formaldehyde in Brazilian Blowout, 8.3% in Global Keratin, 3% in Coppola and 0% in La Brasiliana, and other products that break down into formaldehyde were found in three of the four.Tested Pierce 2011 Study page
