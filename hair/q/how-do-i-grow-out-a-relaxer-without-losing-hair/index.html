Question

How do I grow out a relaxer without losing hair?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

There is no study of how to grow out a relaxer, so this is guidance, not tested. Never relax hair that is already relaxed: touch up new growth only, and only every few months, if you keep relaxing. Detangle with a wide-tooth comb from the ends up to the roots, in sections, and use a heat protectant with the lowest heat setting.
