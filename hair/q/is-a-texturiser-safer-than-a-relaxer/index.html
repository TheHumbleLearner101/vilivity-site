Question

Is a texturiser safer than a relaxer?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Whether a texturiser is safer than a relaxer is untested: our search found no study comparing them. Stylists describe a texturiser as a milder or shorter relaxer, not a different chemical, so do not assume it is much safer. If you have a relaxer, have it done by a trained, experienced professional, on new growth only, at least 6 weeks apart.

What this is based on

  • A relaxer straightens, a texturiser loosens curl by being left on for less time, and a perm adds curl. Stylists say relaxer and texturiser use the same kind of chemical.
