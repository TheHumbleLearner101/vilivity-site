Question

What not to do with relaxed hair?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Do not put tight braids or weaves on relaxed hair, and do not apply relaxer to hair that has already been relaxed. Do not leave scalp burns: more burns tracked with more fibroids in one cohort. Have a professional apply it, and see a dermatologist about hair thinning, particularly at the crown or edges.

What this is based on

  • Putting tight braids or weaves on relaxed hair.Tested Khumalo 2008
  • Applying relaxer to hair that has already been relaxed.Clinical guidance American Academy of Dermatology, "Black hair: tips for everyday care"
  • Leaving scalp burns: more burns tracked with more fibroids in one cohort.Tested Wise 2012
  • Have a professional apply it.Clinical guidance American Academy of Dermatology, "Black hair: tips for everyday care"
  • Hair thinning, particularly at the crown or edges (dermatologist).When to see someone

From the card

Studies disagree on whether relaxers raise cancer risk, and none can prove cause. In the large Black women's cohort, heavy use showed no clear rise in uterine cancer overall but a higher risk after the menopause; breast cancer results were largely null. Relaxed hair under tight styles carried the highest risk of hair loss.
