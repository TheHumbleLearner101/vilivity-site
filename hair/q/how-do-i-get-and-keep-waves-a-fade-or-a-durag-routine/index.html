Question

How do I get and keep waves, a fade or a durag routine?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

No study has tested a waves routine, so what keeps waves is untested. Barber guides say brush, moisturise and wear a satin durag at night; that advice is untested too. Start with a healthy scalp by treating dryness or dandruff first, wash 4c hair every 1 to 3 weeks with conditioner, and see a doctor about bumps, pus or scars at the nape.
