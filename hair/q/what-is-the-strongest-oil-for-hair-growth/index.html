Home / Hair / Hair growth oils and supplements: castor, rosemary, black seed, biotin

Question

What is the strongest oil for hair growth?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Rosemary oil has more behind it, and still not much. One trial of 100 people with pattern baldness found it matched 2% minoxidil at six months, with no untreated group and none chosen for afro hair. Black seed oil has not been tested for hair growth in people, in our search. Castor oil alone has not either.

  • Who was tested: 100 Iranian patients with androgenetic alopecia (pattern hair loss). What was found: No significant change at 3 months in either group. At 6 months both groups had a significant rise in hair count, with no significant difference between them. Scalp itching was higher in both groups than at baseline and more frequent with minoxidil.Tested Panahi 2015 Study page
  • Who was tested: Castor oil and black seed oil. What was found: My search found nothing relevant: the string returned 3 results: two reviews and an eyelash cosmetics paper: Phong 2022 (PMID 35816075), whose abstract says there is no strong evidence for castor oil for hair growth, a 2023 narrative review of hair oils in the Black community (PMID 37017321, abstract opened) and a paper on prostaglandin analogues in eyelash products (PMID 42269767); no human trial of castor or black seed oil. Control: PubMed search "Khumalo NP AND hair" returned 71 results on the same day. Earlier note kept: My search found nothing: no human trial of castor oil or black seed oil on hair growth; the one castor-containing result is a branded rosemary-castor blend (Patel 2025) and a mouse pilot (Mathenge 2026). The control searches for rosemary and biotin returned trial abstracts.Tested This 2026
