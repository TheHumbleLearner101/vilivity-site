Home / Hair / Scalp oiling and greasing

Question

should i grease my scalp everyday

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Whether oiling or greasing helps growth, itch, flaking or breakage in people is untested. The American Academy of Dermatology advises washing once a week or every other week to limit product build-up, and a hot oil treatment about twice a month for added moisture. In the UK, see a GP or dermatologist if the scalp is itchy, flaking, sore or has scabs that persist.

What this is based on

  • Untested: effect of scalp oiling on growth, itch, flaking or breakage in people; whether petrolatum-based grease differs from plant oils; whether washing frequency matters (the one survey found no link to two conditions, but was small); whether lab effects on hair strands show up in real-world breakage.Not tested, and gaps (from the card)

From the card

Nobody has tested scalp oiling or greasing for hair growth or scalp health in a trial. In the lab, coconut oil protected hair strands from protein loss, which is about the strand, not the scalp. Whether the routine does what tradition says is untested.
