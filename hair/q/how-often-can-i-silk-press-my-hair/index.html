Home / Hair / Heat, silk press, keratin treatments and colour

Question

how often can i silk press my hair

  • Tier B thin Observed in Black populations
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

The American Academy of Dermatology (US) advises pressing or heat-straightening no more than once a week, with a ceramic iron or a comb with a dial. How often is truly safe is untested. Use a heat protectant on wet hair and the lowest setting that gets the style.

From the card

Heat damage on afro hair has not been measured in people. Dermatologists advise a heat protectant, the lowest setting that works and no more than one press a week. One small survey links frequent heat to hairline loss. Keratin smoothing products can release formaldehyde; bleaching and dyeing damage the hair surface whatever your curl.
