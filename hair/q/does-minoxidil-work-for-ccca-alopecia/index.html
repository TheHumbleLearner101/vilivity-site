Home / Hair / CCCA: scarring hair loss at the crown

Question

Does minoxidil work for CCCA alopecia?

  • Tier B thin Observed in Black populations
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Over-the-counter minoxidil alone is not enough to rely on: a Black Health article attributes to the AAD that it cannot stop CCCA, which is common advice and untested. A review says minoxidil and similar agents are used as camouflage. See a dermatologist early rather than trying quick-fix online products.

From the card

CCCA is a scarring hair loss that starts as a thin patch at the crown and spreads outwards. It mostly affects women of African descent and men can have it. Scarred follicles do not regrow, so treatment aims to stop it spreading. The earlier a dermatologist sees it, the more hair is kept.
