Home / Hair / Hair transplants in afro-textured hair

Question

Can Black men have a hair transplant?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Our search found nothing on hair transplant outcomes in Black men, so how well they work for you is untested. The two-patient report of transplants in end-stage CCCA was in women. See a doctor, then a dermatologist, about thinning or a receding hairline before a surgeon.

What this is based on

  • Untested: graft survival, satisfaction and complications in Black men; scarring and keloid rates after FUE. Not opened: Singh 2013 (no abstract). Closes the 12 Nov partial as "technique evidence only; no outcomes".Not tested, and gaps (from the card)
  • Who was tested: 2 African American women with end-stage CCCA. What was found: Hair grew at the recipient sites from 4 to 5 months, with no scarring in recipient or donor areas; the authors call transplantation safe and well tolerated in end-stage CCCA with no inflammation on biopsy.Tested Callender 2014 Study page
  • Thinning or a receding hairline, or bald patches: a doctor, then a dermatologist, before a surgeon.When to see someone

Checked against its sources on 2026-11-16.

Also asked as

  • Does hair transplant work for Black men?

From the card

Transplants can be done on tightly curled hair, but the evidence is a small case series and a review, with no outcome data in Black men. Curl makes extraction harder, so the surgeon's tools and experience matter. Scarring hair loss such as CCCA must be ruled out first.
