Home / Hair / Hair shedding after pregnancy, illness or stress

Question

What vitamin am I lacking if my hair is thinning and falling out?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Ask about blood tests for thyroid and iron, the checks the British Association of Dermatologists leaflet lists. Avoid buying quick-fix supplements for a scarring or patchy loss before a diagnosis. Whether iron or other supplements help in a trial is untested. See a GP (UK) if hair loss comes with fatigue, heavy periods, weight change or other symptoms.

From the card

A sudden all-over shed, usually three months after a trigger such as childbirth, illness, stress or crash dieting, is called telogen effluvium. It usually gets better on its own within months. But thinning at the crown, part or hairline can be a different condition, and some women have both. Ask a doctor to check iron and thyroid if it persists.
