Question

How do you know if your edges are permanently damaged?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

A smooth, shiny hairline with no visible hair openings points to scarring: see a dermatologist soon. Long-standing loss can be permanent, while hair can regrow within months when caught early and the pulling stops. See a GP (UK), then a dermatologist, if your edges or temples are thinning or receding, and a GP for pain, bumps or itch at the hairline that does not settle.
