Question

Does chebe powder grow hair?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Our search found no study of chebe on hair or in people. Dr Sunitha Posina, an internist quoted by PureWow, says there is no evidence it grows hair, and any benefit is untested. If your hair keeps breaking despite careful care, see a doctor or a hair and scalp specialist.

What this is based on

  • Chebe is a traditional herbal hair powder, mixed with oil or butter and coated on the lengths. My search found no study of it on hair or in people.
