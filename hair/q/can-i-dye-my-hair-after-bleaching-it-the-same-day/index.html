Home / Hair / Colouring and bleaching afro-textured hair

Question

can i dye my hair after bleaching it the same day

  • Tier D Untested
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

No study tested dyeing the same day as bleaching; my search found nothing in people. In the lab, applying bleach, straightening and heat to the same hair fibres changed their structure more than single treatments. The British Association of Dermatologists advises 2 weeks between a relaxer and dyeing. See a GP or dermatologist if hair breaks or thins.

What this is based on

  • Stacking bleach, straightening and heat on the same hair: lab work found combined treatments changed the fibre more than single ones.Tested Dos 2019
  • Who was tested: Caucasian and Afro hair samples, treated with heat, bleach and straightening alone and together. What was found: Bleaching, straightening and heat changed chemical bonds and protein structure; excessive heat caused the largest change, and applying several treatments to the same fibres caused even more pronounced changes. The two hair groups showed small differences for each treatment.Tested Dos 2019 Study page
  • Who was tested: Colour or bleach routines in coiled hair. What was found: My search found nothing in people: of 7 results I read all titles; the relevant ones are Bloch 2019, Dos Santos 2019 and a three-ethnicity chemical-damage comparison (Lee 2014, PMID 24261682); all are hair-fibre lab studies. None tests a routine or a bond-repair product in people. Control: PubMed search "Khumalo NP AND hair" returned 71 results on the same day.Tested This 2026
  • Leave 2 weeks between a relaxer and dyeing or heat straightening.Clinical guidance British Association of Dermatologists, patient leaflet "Caring for Afro-textured hair" (July 2023)
  • Hair breaking or thinning after colouring or bleaching (doctor or dermatologist).When to see someone

Also asked as

  • Can I bleach my hair again if I just bleached it?

From the card

Bleaching and dyeing damage the hair surface whatever the curl, in lab tests on hair samples; no study has tested a colour or bleach routine in people with coiled hair. Dermatologists advise 2 weeks after a relaxer, and the UK's NHS a strand test at least 48 hours before dyeing, because dye can cause allergy.
