Question

What is the best way to treat dandruff and seborrheic dermatitis on Black hair?

  • Tier B moderate Tested, but not in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Use an anti-dandruff shampoo with ketoconazole, coal tar or salicylic acid for up to 4 weeks, massaged in and left 5 to 10 minutes before rinsing. Once clear, use the antifungal shampoo once or twice a week. Ketoconazole beat placebo in trials of mixed people, though they were not selected for afro hair or dark skin. See a doctor if symptoms continue after 4 weeks.

From the card

Flaking and itch are often dandruff or seborrhoeic dermatitis, which is a skin reaction, not simple dryness. An anti-dandruff shampoo, such as ketoconazole, coal tar or salicylic acid, is the UK's NHS first step; ketoconazole beat placebo in trials of mixed people (low-quality evidence). Lice can affect any hair; find a live louse before treating.
