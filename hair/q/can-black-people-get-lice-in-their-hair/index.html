Home / Hair / Head lice in afro-textured hair and locs

Question

can black people get lice in their hair

  • Tier B thin Tested or observed (see the evidence)
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Anyone with hair can get head lice. Old school surveys found fewer cases in children with African hair, but they conflict: one US study found 15 per cent of African American pupils infested. Do not assume afro hair or oils make lice impossible. Surveys of this kind are old, and none looked at locs.
