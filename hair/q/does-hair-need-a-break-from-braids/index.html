Question

Does hair need a break from braids?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Dermatologists advise giving your scalp a break between styles. Whether rest periods reverse early hair loss is untested. Consider leaving out the delicate hairline edges, and alternate the direction of parts so stress does not sit in one place. See a doctor if hair thins at the edges or temples.

What this is based on

  • Braids that hurt are too tight; pain after installation means pull on the scalp. Dermatologists advise taking braids out within about six weeks and extensions within eight, giving your scalp a break, and keeping the hairline loose.
