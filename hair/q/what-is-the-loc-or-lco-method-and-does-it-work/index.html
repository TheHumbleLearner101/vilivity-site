Home / Hair / LOC and LCO: layering liquid, oil and cream

Question

What is the LOC or LCO method and does it work?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

LOC and LCO are ways of layering moisture: a water-based liquid or leave-in (L), an oil (O) and a cream (C), with cream before oil in LCO. The oil is said to seal the water in. No human study of either method was found, so whether they work is untested.

What this is based on

  • Moisture is layered in order: a water-based liquid or leave-in (L), an oil (O) and a cream (C), or cream before oil (LCO). The oil is said to seal the water in so hair stays soft for days.Tradition and common belief, not evidence
  • Nobody has tested layering liquid, oil and cream on people, so there is no proof that LOC or LCO beats one product or that either order is better. Dermatologists say to moisturise regularly between washes. Lab work shows African hair holds lipids differently, but that does not test a routine.From the card's plain answer
