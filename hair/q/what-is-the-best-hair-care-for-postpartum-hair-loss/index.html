Home / Hair / Hair shedding after pregnancy, illness or stress

Question

What is the best hair care for postpartum hair loss?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Avoid harsh brushing, heat tools, harsh chemicals and tight hairstyles while the hair recovers. Camouflage fibres or volumising shampoos can help for severe thinning. Postpartum shedding in Black women has not been studied, so this is general advice. See a GP (UK) if shedding does not improve, and a dermatologist for thinning at the crown, part or hairline.

From the card

A sudden all-over shed, usually three months after a trigger such as childbirth, illness, stress or crash dieting, is called telogen effluvium. It usually gets better on its own within months. But thinning at the crown, part or hairline can be a different condition, and some women have both. Ask a doctor to check iron and thyroid if it persists.
