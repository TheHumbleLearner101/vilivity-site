Home / Hair / Colouring and bleaching afro-textured hair

Question

Is hair dye safe on natural or relaxed hair?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Dye can cause allergy. The NHS (UK) advises a strand test at least 48 hours before dyeing; then watch for stinging, burning, itch, swelling or blisters for up to 72 hours; a rash can look darker or be harder to see on brown and black skin. If you react, wash with mild shampoo and stop. Call 999 (UK) for swelling of the lips, mouth, throat or tongue, or fast or difficult breathing.

From the card

Bleaching and dyeing damage the hair surface whatever the curl, in lab tests on hair samples; no study has tested a colour or bleach routine in people with coiled hair. Dermatologists advise 2 weeks after a relaxer, and the UK's NHS a strand test at least 48 hours before dyeing, because dye can cause allergy.
