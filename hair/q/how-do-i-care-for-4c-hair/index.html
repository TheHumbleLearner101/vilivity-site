Question

How do I care for 4c hair?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

UK dermatologists advise washing about every one to three weeks, or sooner if there is build-up or oiliness. Use a rinse-out conditioner every wash, focused on the ends, then a leave-in. Detangle gently with a soft wide-spaced brush or wide-toothed comb. At night, loosely plait or twist and use a satin or silk cap, scarf or pillowcase. No wash-day routine has been tested in tightly coiled hair.

What this is based on

  • Nobody has tested a wash-day routine for tightly coiled hair.
