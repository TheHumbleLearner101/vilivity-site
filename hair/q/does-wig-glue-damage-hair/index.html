Home / Hair / Wigs, weaves and lace glue

Question

does wig glue damage hair

  • Tier D Untested
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Adhesives and synthetic hair can cause an itchy allergic rash, and glue or gel on the hairline itself can pull edges out when removed. Apply adhesive just in front of the hairline, not on it. See a doctor for a dry, itchy, red rash at the glue line, or for pain, bumps or pus at the hairline.

From the card

No study has tested whether wigs protect your own hair. Dermatologists advise keeping your own hair clean and moisturised underneath, sewing in loosely, wearing a satin wig cap, and putting glue just in front of the hairline, not on it. Adhesives and synthetic hair can cause an itchy allergic rash, and removal can pull out edges.
