Question

What is traction alopecia and can it grow back?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Traction alopecia is hair loss from pulling. Caught early it can recover; left, it can become permanent. Whether rest periods reverse early loss, and any treatment, has not been tested in a trial. Take a style out straight away if your scalp stings, aches or crusts, and in the UK see a GP, then a dermatologist, if edges thin.

What this is based on

  • Tight braids, weaves and ponytails can cause traction alopecia, hair loss from pulling, and the risk is highest when tension is added to relaxed hair.
