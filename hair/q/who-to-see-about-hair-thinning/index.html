Home / Hair / Finding a stylist, trichologist or dermatologist who knows afro hair

Question

who to see about hair thinning

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Thinning at the crown, hairline or part should be seen by a dermatologist, and do not wait because a stylist says it is just breakage. The American Academy of Dermatology (US) says to see a board-certified dermatologist for texture change or even slight thinning. CCCA mostly presents as progressive thinning of the central scalp.

From the card

A stylist or loctician is the right person for styling and a trichologist or dermatologist for hair loss. In the UK a GP can refer you to a dermatologist; trichologists are not medically qualified, so check that they are registered and have experience with afro-textured hair. Ask directly what share of their patients have hair like yours.
