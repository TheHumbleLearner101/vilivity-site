Question

how long can you keep braids in men

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Dermatologists' advice for everyone is to take braids out within about 6 weeks and leave the delicate hairline out of them. No study has tested braid styles in men or boys. Keep braids loose: scalp pain after installation means they are too tight. See a doctor if your hairline is thinning or receding.

No study has tested braid styles in men or boys. A South African school survey found traction alopecia mostly in girls with relaxed hair, and neck bumps (acne keloidalis nuchae) in boys with frequent haircuts. Dermatologists' advice is the same for everyone: loose braids, hairline left free, rest between styles.
