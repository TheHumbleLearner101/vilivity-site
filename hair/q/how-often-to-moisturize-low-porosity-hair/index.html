Home / Hair / Hair porosity and moisture

Question

how often to moisturize low porosity hair

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

This entry gives no number of times to moisturise, and no study tested moisturising routines by porosity. Dermatology guidance is to wash once a week or every other week and condition every wash, focusing on the ends. Whether moisture products change breakage over several weeks is untested.

What this is based on

  • Wash once a week or every other week, and condition every wash, focusing on the ends.Clinical guidance American Academy of Dermatology, "Black hair: tips for everyday care"
  • Untested: float test validity; whether any routine chosen by porosity reduces breakage in people; whether 'moisture' products change breakage over weeks; LOC or LCO methods; protein against moisture; heat and humidity effects. The consumer-perceived porosity contact-angle method is lab-only and does not validate home tests.Not tested, and gaps (from the card)
  • Who was tested: Human use of the float test or porosity-based routines. What was found: My search found nothing: no validation of the float test and no trial of routines chosen by porosity type.Tested PubMed 2026

Checked against its sources on 2026-11-16.

Also asked as

  • how often should you moisturise low porosity hair

From the card

The glass-of-water float test, and routines chosen by "low, normal or high porosity", have never been tested in people. Porosity is real in the lab, but nobody has shown that choosing products by it helps. The lab does show tightly coiled hair breaks easily under rough grooming, so gentle handling is the safest advice.
