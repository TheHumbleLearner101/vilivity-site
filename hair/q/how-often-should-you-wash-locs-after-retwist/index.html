Home / Hair / Locs: starting, washing and keeping them healthy

Question

how often should you wash locs after retwist

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Loc-care advice is to wait about four to six weeks before first washing starter locs, and about two weeks after a fresh retwist. This is common advice and untested; no study of locs exists. Dry them fully afterwards, and avoid twisting so tightly that your scalp aches or stings.
