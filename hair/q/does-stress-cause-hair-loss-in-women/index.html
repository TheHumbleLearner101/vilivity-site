Home / Hair / Hair shedding after pregnancy, illness or stress

Question

does stress cause hair loss in women

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Stress can trigger a sudden all-over shed (telogen effluvium) that usually recovers without treatment over many months. Do not assume it is only stress: in 200 Egyptian women with postpartum loss, most also had another hair loss disorder. A Japanese questionnaire study found postpartum hair loss associated with anxiety; association only. See a doctor if shedding does not improve.

What this is based on
