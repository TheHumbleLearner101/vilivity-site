Home / Hair / Hair porosity and moisture

Question

does hair porosity test work

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

The glass-of-water float test has never been tested in people: our search found no study showing it works. Porosity is a real, measurable property of hair in the lab, but a home result is not a diagnosis. Describe your hair by its shape (coil, curl or wave) when you talk to a stylist or doctor.

What this is based on

  • The glass-of-water float test, and routines chosen by "low, normal or high porosity", have never been tested in people. From the card

The glass-of-water float test, and routines chosen by "low, normal or high porosity", have never been tested in people. Porosity is real in the lab, but nobody has shown that choosing products by it helps. The lab does show tightly coiled hair breaks easily under rough grooming, so gentle handling is the safest advice.
