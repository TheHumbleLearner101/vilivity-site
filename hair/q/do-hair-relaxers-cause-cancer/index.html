Home / Hair / Chemical relaxers and hair straighteners

Question

Do hair relaxers cause cancer?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Studies disagree, and none can prove cause. In the Sister Study of 33,947 US women, straightening products were linked to uterine cancer. In the Black Women's Health Study, heavy use showed no clear rise overall but a higher risk after the menopause, and breast cancer results were largely null. See a doctor promptly about any bleeding after the menopause.

  • Who was tested: 33,947 US women aged 35-74 in the Sister Study (racially and ethnically diverse). What was found: Ever use of straightening products in the previous year was associated with higher uterine cancer rates (hazard ratio 1.80, 95% CI 1.12-2.88); more than 4 uses in the previous year, 2.55 (1.46-4.45). Dyes and perms were not associated. The authors call it the first epidemiological evidence of this link and say more research is warranted.Tested Chang 2022 Study page
  • Who was tested: 44,798 Black women with an intact uterus in the Black Women's Health Study; 347 uterine cancers. What was found: Heavy use (15 or more years, at least 5 times a year) against never or light use gave a hazard ratio of 1.18 (95% CI 0.81-1.71), not significant. Among postmenopausal women, heavy use was 1.64 (1.01-2.64) and 20 or more years of use 1.71 (1.08-2.72). Premenopausal women: null.Tested Bertrand 2023 Study page
  • Any bleeding after the menopause (doctor, promptly).When to see someone
