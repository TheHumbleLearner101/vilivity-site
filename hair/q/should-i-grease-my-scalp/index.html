Home / Hair / Scalp oiling and greasing

Question

Should I grease my scalp?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Whether greasing or oiling your scalp does what tradition says is untested: our search found no randomised trial on scalp condition or hair growth. Many Black families pass it down, often weekly. Oil the strand if you like the feel, and do not use oil on flaking, scale or sores instead of getting them looked at. See a doctor or dermatologist if the scalp stays itchy, flaking or sore.

What this is based on

  • Nobody has tested scalp oiling or greasing for hair growth or scalp health in a trial. In the lab, coconut oil protected hair strands from protein loss, which is about the strand, not the scalp. Whether the routine does what tradition says is untested.From the card's plain answer
