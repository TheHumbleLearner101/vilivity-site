Question

Is a silk press or blow-out safe for natural hair?

  • Tier B thin Observed in Black populations
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Heat damage on afro hair has not been measured in people. Dermatologists advise a heat protectant, the lowest setting that works and no more than one press a week. In 197 Ghanaian women, more than two heat-styling sessions a month went with higher odds of hairline recession, in a small self-reported survey.

More than two high-frequency heat-styling sessions a month was associated with higher odds (adjusted odds ratio 5.65, 95% CI 1.35-23.62). Keeping one hairstyle for over 6 weeks showed higher estimated odds that were not significant (1.81, 95% CI 0.59-5.60).Tested Ayugane 2026 Study page
