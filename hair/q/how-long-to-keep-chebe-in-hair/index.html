Question

How long to keep Chebe in hair?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

A stylist quoted by PureWow suggests a weekly chebe treatment left on for at least an hour, on the lengths of your hair. This is common advice and untested, as our search found no study of chebe on hair. Patch test first, since its side effects are unknown.

From the card

Chebe is a traditional herbal hair powder, mixed with oil or butter and coated on the lengths. My search found no study of it on hair or in people. A doctor quoted by PureWow says it does not grow hair. Any benefit is untested, so patch test first and avoid heavy coating on thin strands.
