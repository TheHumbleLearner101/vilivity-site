Home / Hair / Receding hairline and hair transplants in Black men

Question

why is my hairline receding on one side

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

A receding hairline has several causes: inherited pattern baldness, pulling from tight styles, or scarring conditions such as CCCA, which men can get. If you have a tight style, wig or head wrap, loosen it. See a doctor if your hairline is receding, and a dermatologist if there is itch, burning, tenderness or a shiny scalp. See a doctor for bumps or pus at the hairline.
