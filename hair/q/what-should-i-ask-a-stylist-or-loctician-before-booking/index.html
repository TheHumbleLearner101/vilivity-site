Question

What should I ask a stylist or loctician before booking?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

This is common advice, untested, mostly from a natural-hair blog. Ask about their experience with your hair type, the products they use and the price before you book, and book a consultation or a simple wash and condition first. Say so if it hurts: the British Association of Dermatologists says scalp pain after installation means braids are probably too tight. For locs, ask a loctician how often to retwist.

From the card

No study shows that a stylist's training or specialism gives better hair or scalp outcomes. In one small US survey, most Black cosmetologists had never heard of two scarring hair-loss conditions. Book a consultation or a simple wash first, watch how they handle your hair, and tell them when a style hurts.
