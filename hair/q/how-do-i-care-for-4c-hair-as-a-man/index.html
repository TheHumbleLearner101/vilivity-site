Question

How do I care for 4c hair as a man?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Wash 4c hair every 1 to 3 weeks and use conditioner, as for any coiled hair. Start with a healthy scalp: treat dryness or dandruff before a brushing routine. This entry found no men-specific wash-interval study. See a GP (UK) if an itchy, flaky scalp has not settled after 4 weeks of an anti-dandruff shampoo.

What this is based on

Also asked as

  • How to take care of hair for men?

From the card

No study has tested a waves routine or durags for scalp or hair health. Barber guides say brush, moisturise and wear a satin durag; that is untested. The part with a medical record is the nape: bumps from very close cuts or shaving there can be acne keloidalis nuchae, a scarring condition in men of African descent.
