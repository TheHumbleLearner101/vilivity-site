Question

How do I use chebe powder?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Use chebe as a conditioning treatment on the lengths: a stylist quoted by PureWow suggests a weekly treatment left on for at least an hour. Patch test first, since its side effects are unknown, and avoid a heavy coat on thin or fine strands. This is common advice and untested.

From the card

Chebe is a traditional herbal hair powder, mixed with oil or butter and coated on the lengths. My search found no study of it on hair or in people. A doctor quoted by PureWow says it does not grow hair. Any benefit is untested, so patch test first and avoid heavy coating on thin strands.
