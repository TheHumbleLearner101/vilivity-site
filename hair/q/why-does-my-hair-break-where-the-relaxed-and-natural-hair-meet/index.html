Home / Hair / Going natural: transitioning from a relaxer and the big chop

Question

Why does my hair break where the relaxed and natural hair meet?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

People say the join between the two textures is where hair snaps, and the hair media article says direct heat on that line weakens it and can lead to snapping. Our search found no study of breakage at the join between relaxed and natural hair. Avoid direct heat there and tight styles that pull at the join or the edges.

What this is based on

  • Going natural means either a big chop, cutting off all the relaxed hair at once, or growing the relaxer out and trimming over months. People say the join between the two textures is where hair snaps.Tradition and common belief, not evidence

From the card

There is no study of the big chop or of how to grow out a relaxer, so none of this is tested. Dermatology guidance on relaxers is clear: never relax hair that is already relaxed, and avoid tight styles. The advice about the weak join between the two textures and trimming comes from hair media, not research.
