Question

Which shampoo, conditioner and leave-in works best for natural hair?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Which shampoo, conditioner or leave-in is best is untested: my search found no trial of a wash-day routine in tightly coiled hair. UK and US dermatologists agree on conditioner every wash and gentle detangling. The British Association of Dermatologists adds focusing conditioner on the ends, then a leave-in.
