Question

Can cornrows and braids cause hair loss in men?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

No study reports braid-related hair loss in adult men; our search found nothing. In a South African school survey, traction alopecia was mostly in girls with relaxed hair. Dermatologists advise loose braids, the hairline left free and rest between styles. See a doctor if a hairline or crown is thinning or receding, or a child has scalp pain, bumps or patchy hair loss after braids.

  • Who was tested: Braids and cornrows in men and boys. What was found: My search found nothing in men: the 2 results were the South African schoolchildren survey above (PMID 17553035) and a review of therapies for alopecias in Black women (Callender 2004, PMID 15113284). No study reports braid-related hair loss in adult men. Control: PubMed search "Khumalo NP AND hair" returned 71 results on the same day.Tested This 2026
  • Who was tested: 1,042 schoolchildren (45% boys), South Africa. What was found: Acne keloidalis nuchae (neck bumps) was found in 0.67% overall and 4.7% of final-year boys, all with frequent haircuts; most boys (72.8%) kept natural hair with haircuts within 4 weeks. Traction alopecia was 9.4% overall and 17.1% in girls, and was more common with relaxed than natural hair; 8.6% of boys had relaxed hair.Tested Khumalo 2007 Study page
