Question

How do I treat dark circles and eye bags on dark skin?

  • Tier B thin Tested, but not in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

The cause decides the treatment: a review of 39 studies found creams and peels for pigment-type circles, fillers or fat grafting for volume loss, surgery for loose skin, and lasers mildly to moderately for vascular and pigment types. The evidence is weak and not specific to Black skin. Apply SPF 30 or higher sunscreen daily, and see a dermatologist before fillers, lasers or surgery.

What this is based on

  • Who was tested: Participants in 39 studies of periorbital hyperpigmentation; groups not stated in the abstract. What was found: Fillers and fat grafting work best for dark circles from volume loss; blepharoplasty for loose skin; creams and peels for pigment; lasers mildly to moderately for vascular and pigment types. From the card

Dark circles under the eyes can come from pigment, thin skin showing blood vessels, or lost volume, and the cause decides the treatment. A systematic review found many treatments help some people, from creams and peels to fillers, but the studies are weak and not specific to Black skin. Daily sunscreen is a sensible start.
