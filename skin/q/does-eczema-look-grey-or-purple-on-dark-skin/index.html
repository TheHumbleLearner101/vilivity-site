Question

Does eczema look grey or purple on dark skin?

  • Tier B thin Tested or observed (see the evidence)
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

On dark skin, eczema often looks grey, purple, brown or darker rather than red, with very dry, thickened or bumpy skin. The American Academy of Dermatology (US) says babies with darker skin are more likely to get small bumps, extremely dry skin and dark brown, grey or purple skin where eczema sits. It is easy to miss or mistake for something else. See a GP if treatments are not helping.

What this is based on
