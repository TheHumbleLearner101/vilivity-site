Home / Skin / Moisturising and 'ashy' skin

Question

Why does dark skin look ashy and how do I fix it?

  • Tier A thin Tested in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Ashiness is dry skin showing as a grey-white look on dark skin. In a 3-week trial of 55 women with skin types IV to VI and visible ashiness, regular use of a moisturising body wash or a mild syndet bar reduced dryness and ashiness, with no untreated comparison group. Moisturising straight after bathing is the guidance. In the trial, ashiness behaved as dryness, not dirt or illness.

What was found: The body wash improved transepidermal water loss and expert- and self-assessed dryness; participants reported reduced ashiness at all sites.
