Question

How do I get glowing, clear skin on dark skin?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

A short routine of gentle cleanser, fragrance-free moisturiser and daily SPF 30 or higher sunscreen is what dermatologist guidance recommends. Scrubbing is often treated as what dark skin needs, but it irritates and can worsen any skin condition, including acne. See a doctor or dermatologist if dark patches, spots or irritation are not fixed by a basic routine.
