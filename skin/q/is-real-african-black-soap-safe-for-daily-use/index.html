Question

Is real African black soap safe for daily use?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Our search found no harm signal, and the irritation and pH of the traditional bar were not studied. Patch-test a small area first, start two or three times a week and moisturise afterwards. If skin that was fine becomes sore, red or flaky, see a GP or dermatologist (UK).

What this is based on

  • Untested: effect on acne, eczema, scars or pigmentation in people; irritation and pH of the traditional bar; differences between traditional and commercial black soaps; effect on the skin barrier in dry or ashy skin (see moisturising entry). From the card

Nobody has tested African black soap on acne, eczema or dark marks in people. A dermatologist says it cleans about as well as other soaps, and some people find it drying. Treat it as a cleanser, not a treatment. Whether it helps your skin is untested.
