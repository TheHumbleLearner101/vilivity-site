Home / Skin / Skin cancer and moles on dark skin

Question

Can Black people get skin cancer, and what are the signs?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Skin cancer is much rarer in Black people, but it can happen. Signs to check: a mole that changes size, shape or colour, a dark streak in a nail, a new patch on a palm or sole, or a sore, scaly or lumpy area that does not heal. See a doctor or dermatologist about any of them, and check your whole skin.

Skin cancer is much rarer in Black people, but it can happen, and when melanoma does, outcomes are worse. It is often on palms, soles or under nails rather than sun-exposed skin. Check your whole skin, including those places, and have any new, changing or non-healing mark looked at early.
