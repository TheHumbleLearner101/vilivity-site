Home / Skin / Eczema on dark skin (atopic eczema, including babies)

Question

can eczema cause dark skin

  • Tier B thin Tested or observed (see the evidence)
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Skin can stay darker, lighter or thickened after eczema flares, and eczema on dark skin often looks grey, purple, brown or darker. If that happens, see a doctor or dermatologist. Keep moisturising with an emollient at least twice a day, and carry on even when it improves. Which moisturiser or routine works best on dark skin is untested.

What this is based on

  • Eczema on dark skin often looks grey, purple, brown or darker, not red, with very dry, thickened or bumpy skin. Moisturising at least twice a day, washing with emollient instead of soap, and a pharmacist or doctor for steroid creams are the standard care.
