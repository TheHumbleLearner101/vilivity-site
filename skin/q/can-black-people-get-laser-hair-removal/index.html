Home / Skin / Laser hair removal on dark skin

Question

can black people get laser hair removal

  • Tier B thin Tested, but not in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Laser hair removal can work in medium to dark skin. A review of 12 comparative trials in skin types III to VI found alexandrite laser reduced hair counts more than IPL, though the trials are small and mixed. Choose a clinician who treats dark skin regularly and ask which laser they use. Permanence in Black skin is untested.

What this is based on

  • Laser hair removal can work in medium to dark skin, but the laser also targets skin pigment, so burns and dark or pale marks are more likely. A review of trials found Nd:YAG lasers gave fewer dark marks than light-based IPL devices. Choose an experienced clinician and ask which laser they use.From the card's plain answer
  • Who was tested: Participants with Fitzpatrick skin types III-VI in 12 comparative trials (9 randomised). What was found: Alexandrite laser was superior to IPL for hair count; Nd:YAG and diode were not significantly different from IPL for hair count. Nd:YAG had fewer dark marks than IPL (OR 0.26). Diode and alexandrite had comparable safety to IPL, with more pain.Tested Dorgham 2020 Study page
  • Choose a clinician who treats dark skin regularly, with conservative settings and sequential, lower-energy treatments.Tested Dreifus 2026
  • Untested or not found: the safest settings in Fitzpatrick VI; long-term permanence in Black skin (a 2023 PFB review calls for more study); why unwanted hair grows (hirsutism and hormone conditions) has no source opened and is not answered; the "see someone when" trigger about new fast hair growth is common medical caution, not drawn from an opened source. Cost and regulation of providers in the UK not covered. 12 Nov: causes and options for hirsutism rest on the NHS page only; I did not open a study of prevalence or treatment in Black women (a PubMed search for hirsutism with African or Black and prevalence returned 36 records, titles not read).Not tested, and gaps (from the card)
