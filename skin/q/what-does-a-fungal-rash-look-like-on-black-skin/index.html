Home / Skin / Rashes on dark skin: what to look for

Question

What does a fungal rash look like on Black skin?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-17
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

On brown and black skin, the redness of a rash can be harder to see, so it may look darker, or show mainly as raised, rough or itchy skin.

From the card

On brown and black skin, the redness of a rash can be harder to see, so it may look darker, or show mainly as raised, rough or itchy skin. Check texture, not just colour. For a fever with a rash, press a clear glass on it and check palms and soles: spots that do not fade are an emergency.
