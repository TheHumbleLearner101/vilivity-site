Home / Skin / Finding a dermatologist who knows dark skin

Question

What should I ask a dermatologist about dark skin?

  • Tier B thin Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Ask what share of their patients have skin of colour and where they trained for it. Photograph the area in bright natural light on a neutral background, with close-ups. Both are common advice and untested. In a test of 656 images, dermatologists did worse on dark skin, so it is fair to ask about experience.

What this is based on

  • Ask what share of their patients have skin of colour and where they trained for it.Common advice, untested Black Health (blackhealth.org), "How to find a Black dermatologist", updated Sep 2026
  • Photograph the area in bright natural light on a neutral background and take close-ups.Common advice, untested Black Health (blackhealth.org), "How to find a Black dermatologist", updated Sep 2026
  • Who was tested: 656 skin images (207 Fitzpatrick V-VI) in the Diverse Dermatology Images dataset; dermatologists and AI. What was found: AI models did worse on Fitzpatrick V-VI than I-II (for example ROC-AUC 0.55 against 0.64 for ModelDerm, with overlapping 95% intervals of 0.46 to 0.64 and 0.55 to 0.70; DeepDerm 0.50 against 0.61, HAM10000 0.57 against 0.72; figures from the full text, PMC9374341, opened 12 Nov 2026); dermatologists were also worse on dark skin; fine-tuning on the dataset closed the AI gap.Tested Daneshjou 2022 Study page
  • Dark skin is under-represented in textbooks, and in one test dermatologists and AI both did worse on dark-skin photos, so it is fair to ask a clinician about their experience. In the UK a GP referral is the route; the BAD hub shows how common conditions look on black and brown skin.From the card's plain answer

From the card

Dark skin is under-represented in textbooks, and in one test dermatologists and AI both did worse on dark-skin photos, so it is fair to ask a clinician about their experience. In the UK a GP referral is the route; the BAD hub shows how common conditions look on black and brown skin.
