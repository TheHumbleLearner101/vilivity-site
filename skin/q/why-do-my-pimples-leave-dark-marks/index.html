Home / Skin / Dark marks after acne (post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation)

Question

Why do my pimples leave dark marks?

  • Tier A thin Tested in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Dark marks after spots are common in dark skin, which tends to be affected more often and more severely than lighter skin. They follow inflammation, so treating the spot early matters most. Picking or scrubbing can worsen the pigmentation, so leave spots alone.

What this is based on

  • Dark marks after spots are common in dark skin and slow to fade. One trial in Black adults found a prescription retinoid cream lightened them, though many users were irritated.   • Who was tested: Review of treatment options in skin of colour. What was found: Post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation tends to affect darker skin more often and more severely. Treatment should begin early, starting with management of the original inflammation. First-line therapy is typically topical depigmenting agents plus photoprotection including sunscreen. The authors warn to use caution with all treatments to avoid irritation and worsening of the pigmentation.Tested Davis 2010 Study page
