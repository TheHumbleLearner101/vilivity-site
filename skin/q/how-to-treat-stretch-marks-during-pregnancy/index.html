Question

How to treat stretch marks during pregnancy?

  • Tier B moderate Tested, but not in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

No cream, butter or oil has been shown to stop stretch marks in pregnancy: a cocoa butter trial found no difference from placebo lotion. Avoid retinoid creams in pregnancy, as they may harm the baby. Stretch marks usually fade over time. Ask a pharmacist or GP (UK) about retinoid cream or hyaluronic acid once you are not pregnant.
