Question

does african black soap dry out your skin

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Some people find African black soap drying, according to a dermatologist quoted by Cleveland Clinic (US). Patch-test a small area first, especially if your skin is dry or sensitive, start two or three times a week and moisturise afterwards. If skin turns red or stings, switch brand or stop.

What this is based on
