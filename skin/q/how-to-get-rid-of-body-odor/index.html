Home / Skin / Sweat, body odour and deodorant

Question

how to get rid of body odor

  • Tier B thin Tested, but not in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Antiperspirants, which reduce sweat, are the main tool; deodorants only mask smell. Ask a pharmacist for a stronger antiperspirant rather than a deodorant. Wear loose-fitting clothes and avoid tight clothes and synthetic fabrics such as nylon. Avoid alcohol and spicy food if they set it off. Families often treat sweat and odour with deodorants, powders and strong washes.
