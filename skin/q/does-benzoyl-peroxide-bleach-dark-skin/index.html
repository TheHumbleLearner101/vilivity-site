Question

Does benzoyl peroxide bleach dark skin?

  • Tier B thin Tested or observed (see the evidence)
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Benzoyl peroxide does not bleach skin: the British Association of Dermatologists calls that a debunked myth, so do not skip it for fear of that. In a trial subgroup of Black participants, adapalene-benzoyl peroxide gel was well tolerated and no treatment-related dark marks were seen. Retinoid creams and benzoyl peroxide are the usual first treatments.

From the card

Acne in dark skin often leaves dark marks that last longer than the spots, so treating acne early matters most. Retinoid creams and benzoyl peroxide are the usual first treatments, and benzoyl peroxide does not bleach skin. Hair oil, cocoa butter and shea butter on spot-prone skin can clog pores.
