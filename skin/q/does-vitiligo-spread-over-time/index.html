Question

Does vitiligo spread over time?

  • Tier B thin Tested, but not in people like you
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-17
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

It can spread over years, and nobody can predict how far.

Also asked as

  • Does vitiligo spread to the whole body?
  • Does vitiligo spread with stress?
  • Does vitiligo spread from mother or father to child?

From the card

Vitiligo can start at any age, but a Cochrane review says more than half of people develop it before 20. It can spread over years, and nobody can predict how far. No treatment cures it. Light therapy and some prescription creams bring colour back in some people, slowly, and it can fade again.
