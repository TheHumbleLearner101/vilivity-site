Question

can black soap be used as cleanser

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

A dermatologist says it cleans about as well as other soaps, so treat it as a cleanser. Patch-test a small area first, start two or three times a week, and moisturise afterwards. Avoid it with chemical exfoliants, or if you have eczema or dandruff, and if skin turns red or stings, switch brand or stop.
