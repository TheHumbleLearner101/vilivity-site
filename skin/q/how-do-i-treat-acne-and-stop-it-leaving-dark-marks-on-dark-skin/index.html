Question

How do I treat acne and stop it leaving dark marks on dark skin?

  • Tier B thin Tested or observed (see the evidence)
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Treat acne early, because the earlier it is treated, the less likely dark spots and scarring. For mild acne, try a retinoid, salicylic acid or benzoyl peroxide, wash twice a day with a gentle cleanser, and choose non-comedogenic products. Ask a pharmacist, then a doctor if it is not better. See a doctor if spots are deep, painful, many, or leaving scars or dark marks.

From the card

Acne in dark skin often leaves dark marks that last longer than the spots, so treating acne early matters most. Retinoid creams and benzoyl peroxide are the usual first treatments, and benzoyl peroxide does not bleach skin. Hair oil, cocoa butter and shea butter on spot-prone skin can clog pores.
