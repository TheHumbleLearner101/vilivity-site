Home / Skin / Collagen supplements for skin and joints

Question

Do collagen supplements work for skin?

  • Tier B thin Tested, but not in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Collagen may add a little skin moisture in trials, but the best-quality trials and the ones not paid for by the industry found no clear effect on hydration, elasticity or wrinkles. Treat promised skin changes as unproven and do not pay for them. Our search found no trial in Black participants.

  • Paying for it to change wrinkles, hydration or elasticity: the careful meta-analysis found no clinical evidence for that.Tested Myung 2025
  • Who was tested: Adults in 23 randomised trials of collagen supplements for skin ageing. What was found: Across all trials, collagen improved hydration, elasticity and wrinkles. Trials not funded by pharmaceutical companies found no effect, as did high-quality trials, while industry-funded and low-quality trials found benefit. The authors conclude there is currently no clinical evidence to support collagen supplements to prevent or treat skin ageing.Tested Myung 2025 Study page

The same search style for "vitamin D" returned 930 records, so the search works. ClinicalTrials.gov (API v2, intervention search with "African American" OR Black anywhere in the record): 20 records matched (titles read only; none a skin supplement trial).
