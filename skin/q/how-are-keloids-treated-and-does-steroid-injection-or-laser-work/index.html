Home / Skin / Keloids and piercings

Question

How are keloids treated, and does steroid injection or laser work?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

No treatment removes a keloid, says the NHS in the UK: treatments only improve how it looks. It lists steroid injections or cream, silicone dressings or gels, cryotherapy and laser, and does not compare them. No source we opened compares steroid injection with laser, or says what works best in Black patients. See a dermatologist or doctor early, as early treatment may stop a keloid growing.

From the card

Keloids run in families and are much more common in people of African ancestry. Nobody has tested whether avoiding piercings prevents them, but the UK's NHS advises prone people to avoid piercings and tattoos. If one forms, early treatment is the advice.
