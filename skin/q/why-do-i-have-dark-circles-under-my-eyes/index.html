Question

Why do I have dark circles under my eyes?

  • Tier B thin Tested, but not in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Dark circles under the eyes can come from pigment, thin skin showing blood vessels, or lost volume, or a mix, and the cause decides the treatment. Tiredness is the usual family explanation, but the review of 39 studies lists these four kinds of cause. Ask a dermatologist to work out which kind you have.

  • Who was tested: Participants in 39 studies of periorbital hyperpigmentation; groups not stated in the abstract. What was found: Fillers and fat grafting work best for dark circles from volume loss; blepharoplasty for loose skin; creams and peels for pigment; lasers mildly to moderately for vascular and pigment types. The authors note the scarcity of high-quality evidence and ask for more randomised trials.Tested Michelle 2021 Study page
