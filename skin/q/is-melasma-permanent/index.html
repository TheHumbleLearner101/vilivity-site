Question

Is melasma permanent?

  • Tier B moderate Tested, but not in people like you
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Melasma has no cure, and it can return, including in another pregnancy. Sun protection plus a prescribed cream usually helps. Ask your prescriber about triggers such as the contraceptive pill and hormone therapy before changing anything. Avoid hydroquinone for more than a few weeks at a time without advice, as it can irritate, over-lighten or occasionally darken skin.
