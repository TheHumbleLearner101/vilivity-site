Home / Skin / Dark velvety patches on the neck and folds (acanthosis nigricans)

Question

Is a dark neck a sign of diabetes?

  • Tier B thin Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Dark, thick, velvety patches on the neck, armpits or groin, called acanthosis nigricans, are often a sign of insulin resistance. In a 1997 study of 89 African American people with the patches, 19 had diabetes, and fasting insulin rose with how much of the neck was affected. See a GP for new patches like these.
