Question

Are collagen powders safe?

  • Tier B thin Tested, but not in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Check the source on the label before you take it: marine collagen may not suit you if you have a fish allergy. Cleveland Clinic notes supplements are not well regulated, and stomach upset can happen. Get urgent care for itching, swelling or breathing trouble after taking it, and in the UK see a pharmacist or GP if stomach upset continues.

From the card

Collagen supplements may add a little skin moisture in trials, but the best-quality trials and the ones not paid for by the industry found no clear skin effect. Our search found no trial in Black participants or on dark skin. Check labels for fish if you are allergic, and treat promised skin changes as unproven.
