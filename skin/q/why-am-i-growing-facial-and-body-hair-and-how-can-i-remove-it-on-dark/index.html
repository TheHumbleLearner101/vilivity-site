Home / Skin / Laser hair removal on dark skin

Question

Why am I growing facial and body hair, and how can I remove it on dark skin?

  • Tier B thin Tested, but not in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

The NHS (UK) says hormones are the usual cause of thick, dark hair on the face, neck or chest, so see a GP before only removing it; a blood test may check hormones. NHS options: shaving or waxing, eflornithine cream for the face, the pill, weight loss. Laser and electrolysis last longer but are usually not permanent or on the NHS. For laser on dark skin, choose a clinician who treats dark skin regularly.

From the card

Laser hair removal can work in medium to dark skin, but the laser also targets skin pigment, so burns and dark or pale marks are more likely. A review of trials found Nd:YAG lasers gave fewer dark marks than light-based IPL devices. Choose an experienced clinician and ask which laser they use.
