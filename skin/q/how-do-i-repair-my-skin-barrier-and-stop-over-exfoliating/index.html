Question

How do I repair my skin barrier and stop over-exfoliating?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Stop the irritants, such as scrubbing and overused exfoliating products, and moisturise with a fragrance-free product. This is American Academy of Dermatology guidance, and no trial was opened. Avoid hot water and long baths on dry skin. See a pharmacist or GP if skin keeps burning, stinging, peeling or breaking out after a product.

From the card

A short routine is what dermatologist guidance recommends: gentle cleanser, moisturiser and sunscreen. Scrubbing irritates and over-exfoliating is a common reason people see a dermatologist. I found no human study showing a long multi-step routine, Korean or otherwise, improves dark skin. Stop any product that burns or stings.
