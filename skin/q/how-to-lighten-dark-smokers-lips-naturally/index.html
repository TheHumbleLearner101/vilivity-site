Home / Skin / Dark or swollen lips

Question

how to lighten dark smokers lips naturally

  • Tier B thin Tested, but not in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Tell your doctor if you smoke or use tobacco: it was linked to pigmentation of the lips in a small Indian study of 49 people, an association. A laser lightened lips in 20 Thai adults with no untreated group, so see a dermatologist before any laser or peel on the lips. Home lightening remedies are untested.

What this is based on

  • Tell your doctor if you smoke or use tobacco: it was linked to pigmentation of the lips in a small Indian study.Tested Sarswathi 2003
  • See a dermatologist before any laser or peel on the lips: a Thai study found benefit in 20 people, with no untreated group.Tested Ng 2022
  • Who was tested: 20 healthy Thai adults with physiologic lip hyperpigmentation. What was found: Average melanin index fell significantly after the fourth treatment and at 1 month, and 70% of patients reported moderate to marked improvement at 6 months. One patient had a transient lip swelling. The authors describe physiologic lip hyperpigmentation as a common aesthetic concern, especially in Southeast Asia.Tested Ng 2022 Study page
  • Who was tested: 41 tobacco users and 8 controls in India. What was found: In smokers, 95.24% had pigmentation of the lip and cheek lining, the lip more (81%) than the cheek (33.3%), with more melanocytes on tissue examination. Pigmentation was absent at the site where smokeless tobacco was held.Tested Sarswathi 2003 Study page
  • Untested or not opened: lip pigmentation in Black participants; B12 and other deficiencies (my search found nothing opened); medicine-related lip darkening; home lightening remedies; the 2017 review of diseases of the lips (PMID 29289276) has no detail in its abstract. Swollen-lip causes other than angioedema were not covered beyond the UK's NHS page. Laugier-Hunziker syndrome (PMID 29121134) abstract was empty and was not used.Not tested, and gaps (from the card)

From the card

Dark lips are often a harmless variation in pigment. Smoking and an allergy to a lip product can also darken lips, and a single new dark spot needs a clinician to look at it. Sudden lip swelling can be an allergic reaction, so call emergency services (999 in the UK) if your throat tightens or breathing is hard.
