Home / Skin / How skin conditions look on dark skin (redness, rashes, rosacea, sunburn)

Question

How do skin conditions and rashes look on dark skin compared with textbook photos?

  • Tier B thin Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Textbook photos mostly show light skin, so they are a poor guide to your own: in four core medical textbooks, 4.5% of 4,146 images showed dark skin, and a dermatology audit found 10.5% were Fitzpatrick V to VI. Rashes on dark skin can look pink, purple, brown, grey or a subtle darkening rather than red. Compare the affected area with your unaffected skin, and tell your doctor what changed.
