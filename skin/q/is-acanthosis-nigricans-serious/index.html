Home / Skin / Dark velvety patches on the neck and folds (acanthosis nigricans)

Question

is acanthosis nigricans serious

  • Tier B thin Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

The risk is a missed diabetes or hormone condition: in 89 African Americans with the patches, 19 had diabetes, and the NHS (England) says there is no specific treatment for the patches themselves. See a GP for new dark, velvety patches on the neck, armpits or groin.

From the card

Dark, thick, velvety patches in the neck, armpits or groin are called acanthosis nigricans. They are often a sign of insulin resistance, and the UK's NHS says to have them checked by a doctor. Scrubbing and lightening creams do not treat the cause. They fade over time once the cause is treated.
