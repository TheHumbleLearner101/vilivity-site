Home / Skin / Moisturising and 'ashy' skin

Question

How do I moisturise dry skin on Black skin?

  • Tier A thin Tested in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Moisturise straight after bathing, once you have patted the skin dry, and smooth it on rather than rubbing hard. Use a mild or moisturising cleanser regularly, and fragrance-free or sensitive-skin products if your skin is easily irritated. Stop any product that stings or burns. See a doctor or dermatologist if patches are cracked, red, weeping, itch badly or keep returning.

From the card

Ashiness is dry skin showing as a grey-white look on dark skin. In a small trial of women with darker skin types, a moisturising body wash or mild bar used regularly reduced dryness and ashiness, with no untreated comparison group. Whether Black skin is naturally drier is contested.
