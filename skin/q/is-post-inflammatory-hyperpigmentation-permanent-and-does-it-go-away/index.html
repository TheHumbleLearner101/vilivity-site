Question

Is post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation permanent, and does it go away?

  • Tier A thin Tested in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

The American Academy of Dermatology says a mark a few shades darker than your skin usually fades in 6 to 12 months, so be patient. In the one trial, a prescription retinoid cream showed improvement after about 4 weeks, over a 40-week study. See a dermatologist if a mark is growing or does not fade over many months.

What this is based on

From the card

Dark marks after spots are common in dark skin and slow to fade. One trial in Black adults found a prescription retinoid cream lightened them, though many users were irritated. Treating spots early and wearing sunscreen are the main advice. Bleaching products can do real harm.
