Home / Skin / Dark or swollen lips

Question

why are my lips dark even though i don't smoke

  • Tier B thin Tested, but not in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Smoking is one possible reason. Dark lips are often a harmless variation in pigment, and an allergy to an ingredient in a lip product can darken lips, sometimes with itching and scaling. A single new or changing dark spot should be looked at by a clinician. Our search found no study of dark lips in Black participants.

What this is based on

  • Dark lips are often a harmless variation in pigment. Smoking and an allergy to a lip product can also darken lips, and a single new dark spot needs a clinician to look at it. Sudden lip swelling can be an allergic reaction, so call emergency services (999 in the UK) if your throat tightens or breathing is hard.From the card's plain answer
  • Stop a lip product you think is causing itching, scaling or darkening, and ask a doctor or dermatologist: allergy to lip product ingredients can darken lips.Tested Figueiredo 2024
  • Ignoring a single new or changing dark patch on a lip: a benign type exists, but it can look like melanoma and needs a clinician.Tested Ho 1993
