Question

How do I fade hyperpigmentation and dark spots on dark skin?

  • Tier A thin Tested in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Start by treating spots early so fewer marks form. Wear broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher every day, iron-oxide tinted if you can, and be patient. For stronger fading, ask a doctor or dermatologist about a prescription retinoid: in one 1993 trial of 54 Black adults it lightened marks, though half of users got irritation. Avoid picking, scrubbing and bleaching creams of unknown content.

What was found: Facial lesions in the tretinoin group were significantly lighter than vehicle (P<0.001). Colorimetry showed 40% lightening of lesions against 18% with vehicle (P=0.05). Improvement began after about 4 weeks. Retinoid dermatitis developed in 12 of 24 tretinoin-treated people (50%) but eased with time.
