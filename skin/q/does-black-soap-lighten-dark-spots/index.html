Question

Does black soap lighten dark spots?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Nothing in the sources we opened shows black soap lightens dark spots, and our search found no human trial. Treat a lightening or scar-fading claim on the label with caution. If dark marks are spreading, or you are tempted to lighten your skin, see a dermatologist, and read our skin lightening entry.

From the card

Nobody has tested African black soap on acne, eczema or dark marks in people. A dermatologist says it cleans about as well as other soaps, and some people find it drying. Treat it as a cleanser, not a treatment. Whether it helps your skin is untested.
