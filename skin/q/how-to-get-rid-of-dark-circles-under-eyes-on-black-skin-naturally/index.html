Home / Skin / Dark circles under the eyes

Question

how to get rid of dark circles under eyes on black skin naturally

  • Tier B thin Tested, but not in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Sleep, cold spoons, cucumber and eye creams are the family remedies; home remedies are untested here. What the review found useful depends on the cause, so ask a dermatologist which kind you have. Daily SPF 30 or higher is a sensible start.

What this is based on

  • Dark circles are blamed on tiredness and treated with sleep, cold spoons, cucumber and eye creams.Tradition and common belief, not evidence
  • Untested or not found: home remedies (sleep, cold compresses, tea bags); trials in Black skin; which creams (vitamin C, retinol, kojic acid) work. UK-specific guidance on dark circles was not found in my search.Not tested, and gaps (from the card)
  • Ask a dermatologist to work out which kind you have: pigment, structure, blood vessels or a mix.Tested Michelle 2021
  • Apply sunscreen daily, SPF 30 or higher, broad-spectrum.Clinical guidance American Academy of Dermatology, "Skin care tips dermatologists use"
  • Dark circles under the eyes can come from pigment, thin skin showing blood vessels, or lost volume, and the cause decides the treatment. A systematic review found many treatments help some people, from creams and peels to fillers, but the studies are weak and not specific to Black skin. Daily sunscreen is a sensible start.From the card's plain answer

From the card

Dark circles under the eyes can come from pigment, thin skin showing blood vessels, or lost volume, and the cause decides the treatment. A systematic review found many treatments help some people, from creams and peels to fillers, but the studies are weak and not specific to Black skin. Daily sunscreen is a sensible start.
