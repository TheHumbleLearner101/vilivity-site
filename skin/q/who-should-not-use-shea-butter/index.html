Question

Who should not use shea butter?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

If you have a latex allergy, use refined shea only, because raw shea contains latex. Dermatologists say to avoid it on acne-prone skin or with dandruff. Contact allergy to shea butter is documented and looked rare in our small search (unverified beyond titles). Patch-test a small area first, and see someone if you get a rash or swelling after using it.

From the card

Shea butter has been used on skin and hair in West Africa for generations, but no controlled trial has tested plain shea butter on skin. The studies that exist used shea-containing creams or compounds taken from shea, which are not the same thing. Whether it beats other moisturisers is untested.
