Question

How do I find a dermatologist who knows dark skin?

  • Tier B thin Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

In the UK the route is a GP referral: ask your GP if treatment is not helping, and the NHS may refer you to a dermatologist. Our search found no UK directory of dermatologists by experience with skin of colour. Read the British Association of Dermatologists hub on how common conditions look on black and brown skin first. A US directory is not a UK guide.

From the card

Dark skin is under-represented in textbooks, and in one test dermatologists and AI both did worse on dark-skin photos, so it is fair to ask a clinician about their experience. In the UK a GP referral is the route; the BAD hub shows how common conditions look on black and brown skin.
