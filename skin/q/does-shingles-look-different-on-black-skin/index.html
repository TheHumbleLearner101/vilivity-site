Home / Skin / How skin conditions look on dark skin (redness, rashes, rosacea, sunburn)

Question

Does shingles look different on Black skin?

  • Tier B thin Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Look for blotches on one side of the body that become itchy blisters. The NHS in the UK says the redness can be harder to see on brown and black skin. Look for colour change by comparing with your unaffected skin, and for hot, swollen skin as an infection sign. See a GP or NHS 111 (UK) for warm, swollen, painful or spreading skin, even without redness.

From the card

Redness on dark skin can look pink, purple, brown, grey or just a subtle darkening, so it is easy to miss. Eczema, rosacea and sunburn all happen on dark skin and are often under-diagnosed. Compare the affected area with your unaffected skin, and tell your doctor what changed.
