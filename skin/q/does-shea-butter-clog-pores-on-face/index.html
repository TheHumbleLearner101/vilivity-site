Question

does shea butter clog pores on face

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Dermatologists say shea butter may clog pores on acne-prone skin, and the yeast linked to dandruff can grow in it, so they advise avoiding it on acne-prone skin or with dandruff. Our search found no controlled trial of plain shea butter on skin, so this rests on dermatologist advice, not testing. If you get a rash or swelling after using it, see someone.

From the card

Shea butter has been used on skin and hair in West Africa for generations, but no controlled trial has tested plain shea butter on skin. The studies that exist used shea-containing creams or compounds taken from shea, which are not the same thing. Whether it beats other moisturisers is untested.
