Home / Skin / Baby skin care (newborns and infants)

Question

how to take care of baby skin

  • Tier B moderate Tested, but not in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Bathe in plain water only for at least the first month, with no cleansers, lotions or medicated wipes, and leave vernix, the white coating at birth, on the skin. Use sunscreen only on babies 6 months or older; protect younger babies with shade and clothing. Skin that is blistered, crusty, leaking or hot needs an urgent GP or NHS 111 in the UK.

Also asked as

  • how can i make my baby's skin clear

From the card

Newborn skin needs very little: plain water baths for the first month, no cleansers or lotions, and leave vernix on. Jaundice and rashes can be harder to see on brown and black skin, so check the whites of the eyes. A large review, mostly in babies at high risk of eczema, found routine moisturiser probably made no difference to eczema.
