Question

Is raw shea butter better than shea in products?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Whether raw shea is better than refined is untested. If you have a latex allergy, use refined shea only, because raw shea contains latex. Butter that smells sour, sharp or chemical has gone off (common advice, untested). Keep it cool, dry and out of sunlight, and scoop with a clean, dry spoon (also common advice, untested).

What this is based on

  • Untested: plain shea butter against placebo or another emollient on skin, on eczema, on scalp, on scars or on dark skin specifically. Untested: raw versus refined shea. Untested: shea on hair. From the card

Shea butter has been used on skin and hair in West Africa for generations, but no controlled trial has tested plain shea butter on skin. The studies that exist used shea-containing creams or compounds taken from shea, which are not the same thing. Whether it beats other moisturisers is untested.
