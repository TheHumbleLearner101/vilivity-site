Home / Skin / Laser hair removal on dark skin

Question

is it normal to have discoloration after laser hair removal

  • Tier B thin Tested, but not in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Dark or pale marks are among the risks of laser on dark skin, because melanin competes for the laser energy. See a dermatologist if you get blisters, burns, or dark or pale patches after treatment. Strict sun protection before and after treatment is advised. A review found Nd:YAG laser gave fewer dark marks than IPL in skin types III to VI, so ask which device will be used.

What this is based on

From the card

Laser hair removal can work in medium to dark skin, but the laser also targets skin pigment, so burns and dark or pale marks are more likely. A review of trials found Nd:YAG lasers gave fewer dark marks than light-based IPL devices. Choose an experienced clinician and ask which laser they use.
