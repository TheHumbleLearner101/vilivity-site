Question

Is skin lightening or bleaching cream safe?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Many skin lightening creams contain mercury, strong steroids or high-strength hydroquinone, and reported cases show serious skin and kidney harm. Lightening the whole body is not supported as safe. In a test of 549 products from 32 countries, 33 (6.0 per cent) held mercury above 1,000 ppm. Read the label for mercury, mercurous chloride, calomel, mercuric or mercurio.
