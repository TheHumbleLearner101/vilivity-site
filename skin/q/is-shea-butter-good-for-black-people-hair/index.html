Question

is shea butter good for black people hair

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Shea butter has been used for centuries in West Africa on skin, scalp and hair, but shea on hair is untested: no study found. Dermatologists advise avoiding it with dandruff, because the dandruff yeast can grow in it. Patch-test a small area first (common advice, untested).

What this is based on

  • Used for centuries in West Africa to moisturise skin, scalp and hair and to treat wounds, burns and skin complaints. Widely believed to be a gentle, healing moisturiser for dark skin.Tradition and common belief, not evidence
  • Untested: plain shea butter against placebo or another emollient on skin, on eczema, on scalp, on scars or on dark skin specifically. Untested: raw versus refined shea. Untested: shea on hair. From the card

Shea butter has been used on skin and hair in West Africa for generations, but no controlled trial has tested plain shea butter on skin. The studies that exist used shea-containing creams or compounds taken from shea, which are not the same thing. Whether it beats other moisturisers is untested.
