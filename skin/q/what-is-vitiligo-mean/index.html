Question

what is vitiligo mean

  • Tier B moderate Tested, but not in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Vitiligo is loss of skin colour in patches. It is an autoimmune condition, not an infection. In dark skin the patches are very visible and can cause distress. If pale or white patches appear on your skin, see a GP in the UK, who can confirm it is vitiligo and not another condition.
