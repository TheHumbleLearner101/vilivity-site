Question

Why are my lips dark or swollen, and what helps?

  • Tier B thin Tested, but not in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Dark lips are often a harmless variation in pigment. Smoking and an allergy to a lip product can also darken lips, and a single new dark spot needs a clinician to look at it. Sudden lip swelling can be an allergic reaction, so call 999 (UK) if your throat tightens or breathing is hard.
