Home / Skin / Stretch marks and fading scars

Question

How do I fade scars and stretch marks on dark skin?

  • Tier B moderate Tested, but not in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Retinoid creams or hyaluronic acid may help new stretch marks: ask a pharmacist or GP (UK), if you are not pregnant. For laser or other devices, see a dermatologist who treats dark skin often and ask for cautious settings and strict sun protection, because pigment competes with the target and raises the risk of dark or pale marks, blisters and scars. Treatment trials in Black skin are lacking.

What this is based on

  • No cream, butter or oil has been shown to stop stretch marks, and a trial of cocoa butter in pregnant women found it made no difference. They usually fade over time.
