Money and independence

Where we stand today

Today the site earns nothing. There are no adverts, no sponsors, no affiliate links and nothing for sale.

Money does not touch the advice

Money never buys a grade. Money never buys a place on a page. A grade describes how well something has been tested and on whom, and nothing else can change it. We want you to be able to trust that.

Research from anyone

Research from any source is welcome. Universities, charities, companies and individuals can all send us their work. It is graded by the same rules as everything else, so a study we are given is treated like a study we found ourselves.

If that changes

We may take paid partnerships in future. If we do, each one will be labelled where it appears, so you can see it before you read the advice.

Your images and data

We do not sell or license anyone's images or personal data. Not now, and that is not something a partner can pay for.

Telling you first

If any of this changes, this page will say so first. You should never have to find out by accident.

Why we write this down

Many health sites mix advice with selling. We are made by us, for us, and we want the advice to be the only thing on the page. Saying how we are paid, and that today the answer is not at all, is part of that.

This is information, not medical advice. If you are worried, see a doctor or pharmacist.
