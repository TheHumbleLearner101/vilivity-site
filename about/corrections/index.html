Corrections

Tell us when we are wrong

Anyone can report a mistake. We would rather hear about it than leave it on the page. You might spot:

  • a wrong fact
  • a broken or wrong source
  • advice that does not apply in your country
  • something that is missing

How to report

Email an address we will publish on this page before launch.

What to send

  • the page
  • the line
  • a source, if you have one

What we do

We check your report against the source. If you are right, we fix the page and update the date on it. Answers show the date they were last checked against their sources, so you can see how fresh they are.

We correct in the open

We do not quietly delete. If something was wrong, we change it and keep the record of the change, so you can see what moved and why.

Lived experience

Your experience matters to us. What families pass down is recorded as tradition or common practice. We never blend it with trial evidence, and we never throw it out. If a page gets your tradition wrong, tell us and we will fix it.

A note on AI

Our pages are drafted with AI and checked in several ways, but mistakes still get through. Our page on how we use AI explains the checks. Your report is one of them.
