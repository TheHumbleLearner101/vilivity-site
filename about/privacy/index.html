Privacy

The short version

You can read this site without telling us anything about yourself.

What the site does not have

  • no accounts
  • no sign-up
  • no adverts
  • no cookies
  • no tracking scripts

Health information

We do not ask for or store health information about you. Please do not put your own health details in an email to us. We do not need them to fix a page.

Server logs

The company that hosts the site may keep standard server logs, such as your IP address and the time of your visit. They are kept to keep the site running and to protect it from misuse. We do not use them to build a picture of you.

If you email us

If you email us, we keep your email only to answer you and fix the page.

If this changes

If this changes, for example if we add visit counting, this page will be updated before the change, not after.

Why it matters

Health is personal. A site about looking after yourself should not follow you around. We would rather know less about you and keep your trust.

Questions

If you have a question about this page, send it the way you would send a correction. You will find that on our corrections page.
