Using this site

Information, not medical advice

This site is information, not medical advice. It does not replace a doctor, nurse, pharmacist or other qualified professional who knows you.

In an emergency

In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Different countries, different rules

Health services, medicines and rules differ by country. Where a page names a service, it names the country too, so you can tell whether it applies to you.

What a grade means

Grades describe the evidence, not you. A strong grade does not mean something is right for your body. A weak grade, or the word untested, does not mean something is useless. It means we found little or no testing, so we say that plainly.

Treatment you already have

Do not stop or change prescribed treatment because of something you read here without speaking to a professional. If a page worries you, take it to someone who knows you and your history.

Tradition and evidence

Where we record what families pass down, we label it as tradition. It sits apart from trial evidence and is never mixed with it.

Quoting us

You may quote and link to our pages.

Mistakes

If you find something wrong, please tell us. Our corrections page explains how.

Not yet clinically reviewed

Pages say "not yet clinically reviewed" until a named clinician has reviewed them. Please read that label as it is written.
