Verdict

The bank has no head-to-head of mineral, chemical and tinted sunscreens on dark skin, so it cannot rank them. What the entry supports is a broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher daily, with an iron-oxide tint as one way to blend in and guard against visible light.
