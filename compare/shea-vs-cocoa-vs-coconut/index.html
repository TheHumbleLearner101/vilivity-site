Tier and harm are those of the linked entry as a whole, so rows that share an entry share its tier. Sentences in the evidence column are quoted from the entry.

Verdict

None of the three has been shown to beat another: plain shea is untested on skin, cocoa butter made no difference to stretch marks in one trial, and coconut oil's evidence is lab work on hair strands. The linked entries report no comparison between them.
