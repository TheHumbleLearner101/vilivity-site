Braids, locs or wigs and your edges

Three long-wear styles and the hairline, compared only from what the linked entries say.

OptionWhat the evidence saysTierHarmEntry
Braids

Braids that hurt are too tight; pain after installation means pull on the scalp.

"Protective" has never been tested.

Tier B moderateHarm signal

Traction alopecia is common in surveys of African girls and women (17% and 32% in one South African sample, Khumalo 2008; 41% with hairline recession in 197 Ghanaian women, Ayugane 2026) and can scar if the pulling continues.

Braids, twists and extensions: tension, how long, itch
Locs

very long locs and tight twisting at the roots strain the scalp and raise the risk of traction alopecia

Tier DNo harm flag set.

Traction alopecia is a risk of tight twisting and heavy locs (BAD leaflet); no study of locs found.

Locs: starting, washing and keeping them healthy
Wigs and weaves

No study has tested whether wigs protect your own hair.

Adhesives and synthetic hair can cause an itchy allergic rash, and removal can pull out edges.

Tier DHarm signal

Contact dermatitis from synthetic hair allergens such as acrylates and disperse dyes (Collins 2025, case reports); adhesives can irritate the scalp and pull edges out (BAD leaflet).

Wigs, weaves and lace glue
For reference: what thins edges

Edges thin mainly from pulling: tight styles, glue on the hairline and frequent heat are linked to hairline loss.

Tier B moderateHarm signal

Hairline recession in 41% of 197 Ghanaian women (Ayugane 2026) and traction alopecia that can become permanent (BAD leaflet).

Protecting your edges and hairline

Tier and harm are those of the linked entry as a whole, so rows that share an entry share its tier. Sentences in the evidence column are quoted from the entry.

Verdict

In each entry the risk to the hairline is pull, whatever the style is called: tight braids, tight twisting at the roots, and glue or tight sew-ins. Braids carry the firmest harm evidence, from observational data, while locs and wigs have not been tested for protecting hair.
