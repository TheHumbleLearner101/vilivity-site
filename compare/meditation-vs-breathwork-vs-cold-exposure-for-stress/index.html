Meditation, breathwork or cold exposure for stress

Three practices Joel uses himself, shown beside the evidence and never blended with it. Compared only from what the linked entries say.

OptionWhat the evidence saysTierHarmEntry
Meditation Founder's own practice

A trial in Black adults with heart disease found fewer deaths, heart attacks and strokes with Transcendental Meditation.

A large review found benefit for anxiety, depression and pain, but no better than other treatments.

Tier A moderateNo harm flag set.

No harm signal found in what I read.

Meditation
Breathwork Founder's own practice

Slow breathing practice lowers stress and blood pressure a little in trials of adults generally.

In the few small studies of African American adults the results were mixed

Tier B moderateNo harm flag set.

No harm signal for slow breathing in what I read.

Cold exposure Founder's own practice

Ice baths eased muscle soreness after exercise in small, low-quality trials that did not report ethnicity.

Believing it treats depression or anxiety.

Cold shock is a real risk.

Tier B moderateHarm signal

Cold shock response can cause hyperventilation and arrhythmia (Barwood 2024); in one lab study men of African descent had stronger finger vasoconstriction in cold (Maley 2014) and in one military clinic series cold weather injuries were far more frequent in Black soldiers (Burgess 2009).

Ice baths and cold exposure

Tier and harm are those of the linked entry as a whole, so rows that share an entry share its tier. Sentences in the evidence column are quoted from the entry.

Verdict

Meditation has the best-tested evidence of the three, including a trial in Black adults, though that result is about heart events and the stress benefit comes from a large review in which it was no better than other treatments. Breathwork lowers stress a little in adults generally with mixed results in African American studies, while the cold exposure entry rests on muscle soreness, warns against believing it treats depression or anxiety, and carries a harm signal.
