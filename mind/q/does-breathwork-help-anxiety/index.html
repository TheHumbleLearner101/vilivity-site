Home / Mind / Breathwork and slow breathing

Question

does breathwork help anxiety

  • Tier B moderate Tested, but not in people like you
  • Founder's own practice
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

In a meta-analysis of 20 trials in adults (ethnicity not given in the abstract), breathwork was associated with lower anxiety than control conditions (g = -0.32), a small-to-medium effect with most studies at moderate risk of bias. Do not treat it as a cure for anxiety or high blood pressure. In the UK, see a GP or a talking therapy service if anxiety or low mood is stopping you functioning.

What this is based on

  • Who was tested: 785 adults in 12 randomised trials (various countries; ethnicity not given in the abstract). What was found: Breathwork was associated with lower stress than control conditions (effect size g = -0.35, 95% CI -0.55 to -0.14). For anxiety (20 trials) g was -0.32 and for depressive symptoms (18 trials) -0.40. The authors urge caution to avoid a miscalibration between hype and evidence.Tested Fincham 2023 Study page

From the card

Slow breathing practice lowers stress and blood pressure a little in trials of adults generally. In the few small studies of African American adults the results were mixed, and one found breathing alone did nothing straight away. It is an extra, not a replacement for treatment.
