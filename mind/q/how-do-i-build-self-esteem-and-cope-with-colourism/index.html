Home / Mind / Colourism, skin tone and mental health

Question

How do I build self-esteem and cope with colourism?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Our search found no trial or evaluated programme for coping with colourism or building self-esteem in response to it, so use ordinary mental health support. Talk to someone you trust, and self-refer to NHS talking therapies (UK) if it is weighing on you. It is reasonable to raise colourism with a GP or therapist as part of why you feel low or anxious.

What this is based on

  • In US surveys of Black adults, experiencing colourism within the community went with higher odds of a psychiatric disorder, and among older adults the link between discrimination and distress was stronger in darker-skinned people. These are associations, not proof of cause. No trial has tested a way to cope with it, so use ordinary mental health support.From the card's plain answer
