Home / Mind / Autism and ADHD: being missed, delayed or misdiagnosed

Question

why is diagnosing autism difficult

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

A US review of 20 studies of inequities in diagnosing mental disorders (17 about autism) found most diagnostic errors were delays. It named 11 contributing factors, including limited health literacy, insurance coverage, provider bias during history-taking and assessment tools that may not suit Black and Hispanic families. The studies were US only and of varying quality.

What this is based on

  • Who was tested: 20 US studies of inequities in diagnosing mental disorders; 17 were about autism. What was found: Most diagnostic errors were delayed diagnosis.
