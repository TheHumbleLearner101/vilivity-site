Question

How do I get my child proper mental-health support?

  • Tier B thin Tested or observed (see the evidence)
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

In one analysis of 26 English child mental health services (BAME children grouped together; Black children not reported separately), minority-background children were more often referred through education and social services than through a doctor, so do not assume school is the only way in. If your child's low mood, anxiety, anger or withdrawal lasts for weeks or affects school or friendships, see a doctor. The UK's NHS also lists children and young people's mental health services (CYPMHS).
