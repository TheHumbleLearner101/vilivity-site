Home / Mind / Breathwork and slow breathing

Question

is breathing safe

  • Tier B moderate Tested, but not in people like you
  • Founder's own practice
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

In what we read, we found no harm signal for slow breathing. Fast, forceful breathing styles (hyperventilation) were not studied here, and one paper describes intermittent respiratory alkalosis and hypoxia from them, which is a gap in the evidence, and no harm has been shown. If breathing practice makes you dizzy, faint or gives chest pain, stop and see a doctor.

What this is based on

  • Breathing practice makes you dizzy, faint or gives chest pain (stop, and see a doctor).When to see someone

Also asked as

  • is breathwork dangerous

From the card

Slow breathing practice lowers stress and blood pressure a little in trials of adults generally. In the few small studies of African American adults the results were mixed, and one found breathing alone did nothing straight away. It is an extra, not a replacement for treatment.
