Home / Mind / Psychosis, sectioning and misdiagnosis in the UK

Question

What rights do I have if I am sectioned?

  • Tier B moderate Tested or observed (see the evidence)
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

In England, if you are detained, ask which section you are held under, and ask to see an independent mental health advocate. You can appeal to the Mental Health Tribunal or the hospital managers, and you may be eligible for legal aid for a solicitor. You can complain to the CQC if you are unhappy with how the Act was used.

From the card

In England, Black people are detained under the Mental Health Act more often than any other group, and a large review found Black Caribbean and Black African patients were far more likely to be compulsorily admitted. Why is not settled. If you are detained, you can appeal, ask for an advocate and complain.
