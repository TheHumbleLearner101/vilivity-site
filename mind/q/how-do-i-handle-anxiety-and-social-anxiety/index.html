Question

How do I handle anxiety and social anxiety?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

In the UK you can refer yourself to NHS talking therapies for anxiety, social anxiety and panic, as long as you are registered with a GP. Ask about guided self-help, group or video options if in-person therapy is hard to attend, and talk to someone you trust. See a GP or self-refer if anxiety lasts weeks or stops you working, studying or leaving the house.

From the card

Anxiety and depression are common and treatable, and the NHS says to seek help early. In the UK you can refer yourself to NHS talking therapies, including for social anxiety and panic, without seeing a GP. Whether therapies work differently in Black adults is untested in my search, so choose a service you can attend.
