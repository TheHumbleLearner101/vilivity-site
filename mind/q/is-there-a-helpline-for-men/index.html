Home / Mind / Black men, depression and getting support

Question

Is there a helpline for men?

  • Tier B thin Tested or observed (see the evidence)
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

In the UK, call CALM on 0800 58 58 58, open 5pm to midnight every day, or Samaritans on 116 123 (24 hours), or text SHOUT to 85258 (24 hours) if you do not want to talk by phone. In a crisis in the UK call NHS 111 and choose the mental health option (England), or 999 in an emergency; in the US call or text 988 or call 911.

From the card

A survey of African American men found that everyday racism and masculine norms both went with more barriers to seeking help, partly through feeling less in control and more depressed. Depression is a health condition and treatable. You can refer yourself to NHS talking therapies, and the CALM helpline is there for men.
