Home / Mind / Ashwagandha: stress, sleep, thyroid and liver cautions

Question

does ashwagandha help you sleep at night

  • Tier B moderate Tested, but not in people like you
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

In five trials of 400 adults, ashwagandha extract gave a small improvement in overall sleep. The effect was larger in people diagnosed with insomnia, at 600 mg a day or more, and over eight weeks or more. Our search found no trial in Black participants. Ask a pharmacist or doctor first if you take sleep medicine or any other medicine.
