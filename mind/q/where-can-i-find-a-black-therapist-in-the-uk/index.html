Question

Where can I find a Black therapist in the UK?

  • Tier B moderate Tested or observed (see the evidence)
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

In the UK, search BAATN, a network of Black, African and Asian therapists with a searchable directory, or look at Black Minds Matter UK, which points to a Black therapist finder and has its own therapy service (free places are limited at present). You can refer yourself to NHS talking therapies in the UK for anxiety and depression without seeing a doctor first, and ask whether you can state a preference.

From the card

Directories in the UK and US list Black or culturally aware therapists. Research finds people moderately strongly prefer a therapist of their own background, but matching made almost no difference to how well treatment worked on average. The gaps were widest in studies of African American clients. A good fit matters more than the label alone.
