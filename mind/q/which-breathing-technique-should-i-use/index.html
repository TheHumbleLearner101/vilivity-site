Home / Mind / Breathwork and slow breathing

Question

Which breathing technique should I use?

  • Tier B moderate Tested, but not in people like you
  • Founder's own practice
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

The trials mix many techniques, so which one works best is untested. The NHS (UK) exercise for stress is to sit or lie comfortably and breathe gently into your belly: in through the nose, out through the mouth, counting to five. Keep going for at least five minutes, ideally daily, and notice whether it helps you.

What this is based on

  • Untested: breathwork in large trials of Black adults; which technique (the trials mix many); long-term benefit; fast hyperventilation-style breathing in this population; effect in people on blood-pressure drugs. From the card

Slow breathing practice lowers stress and blood pressure a little in trials of adults generally. In the few small studies of African American adults the results were mixed, and one found breathing alone did nothing straight away. It is an extra, not a replacement for treatment.
