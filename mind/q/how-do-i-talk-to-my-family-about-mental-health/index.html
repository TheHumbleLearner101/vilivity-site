Home / Mind / Stigma, talking to family and starting therapy

Question

How do I talk to my family about mental health?

  • Tier B thin Tested or observed (see the evidence)
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Tell one person you trust; starting the conversation is what matters. Our search found no source we could open with scripts for talking to family, so we cannot offer any. Do not stay silent because of what the community may think: stigma and silencing blocked help in the London study.

What this is based on

  • Tell one person you trust; starting the conversation is what matters.Clinical guidance NHS, "Help for suicidal thoughts"
  • Untested or not found: scripts for talking to family; trauma and emotional neglect therapy in Black adults; whether faith-based programmes improve access; UK data beyond one London study.Not tested, and gaps (from the card)
  • Staying silent because of what the community may think: stigma and silencing were what blocked help in the London study.Tested Mantovani 2017

Checked against its sources on 2026-11-16.

From the card

A London study found stigma, silence and beliefs about causes slow help-seeking in African-descended communities, including in church groups. You can start without a diagnosis: in the UK you can refer yourself to NHS talking therapies for anxiety, depression and trauma. Telling one person you trust is a first step.
