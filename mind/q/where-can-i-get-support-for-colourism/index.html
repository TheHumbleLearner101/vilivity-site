Home / Mind / Colourism, skin tone and mental health

Question

Where can I get support for colourism?

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Talk to someone you trust, and self-refer to NHS talking therapies (UK) if low mood, worry or anxiety is weighing on you. For a therapist who understands racism and identity, see our entry on finding a Black therapist: matching made almost no difference to outcomes on average, so fit comes first. In a UK crisis, call NHS 111 and choose the mental health option, or Samaritans free on 116 123.

From the card

In US surveys of Black adults, experiencing colourism within the community went with higher odds of a psychiatric disorder, and among older adults the link between discrimination and distress was stronger in darker-skinned people. These are associations, not proof of cause. No trial has tested a way to cope with it, so use ordinary mental health support.
