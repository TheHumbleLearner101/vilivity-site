Question

Is there research on meditation in Black communities?

  • Tier A moderate Tested in people like you
  • Founder's own practice
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Research includes a trial of 201 Black adults with heart disease tested Transcendental Meditation. Mindfulness was tested in 15 hypertensive African American men with kidney disease, and in a pilot of 20 African American older adults. A scoping review covered six studies in Black women with hypertension. Most are small, and the heart trial has no independent replication found.

What this is based on

  • Who was tested: 201 Black men and women with coronary heart disease, US. What was found: The primary outcome (death, heart attack or stroke) was 48% lower in the TM group (hazard ratio 0.52, 95% CI 0.29-0.92, P=0.025). The wider secondary composite fell 24% (HR 0.76, 0.51-1.13, not significant). Systolic blood pressure was 4.9 mmHg lower, and anger scores improved. Adherence was linked to survival.Tested Schneider 2012 Study page
  • Who was tested: 15 hypertensive African American men with chronic kidney disease. What was found: Mindfulness meditation acutely lowered systolic and diastolic pressure, heart rate and muscle sympathetic nerve activity compared with the control.Tested Park 2014 Study page
  • Who was tested: 20 low-income African American older adults in a residence (pilot). What was found: Feasible and acceptable with high attendance. Average systolic pressure fell in both groups; the intervention group had a 21.92 mmHg lower systolic pressure than the social support group afterwards (p=0.020) and a 16.70 mmHg lower diastolic (p=0.003). The authors call the results preliminary and call for larger trials.Tested Palta 2012 Study page
  • Who was tested: Black women with hypertension (6 studies). What was found: The authors report stronger evidence from one randomised trial of transcendental meditation, with reductions in systolic and diastolic blood pressure up to 7 mmHg, and describe non-randomised pilots with reductions up to 9 mmHg systolic.Tested Collins 2022 Study page
  • Untested or unreplicated: the TM mortality result outside the original team; other techniques (mindfulness, prayer, faith-based practice) on heart outcomes in Black adults; long-term adherence; younger adults; effect of cultural fit.Not tested, and gaps (from the card)

Checked against its sources on 2026-11-16.

From the card

Meditation is among the better-tested practices here. A trial in Black adults with heart disease found fewer deaths, heart attacks and strokes with Transcendental Meditation. A large review found benefit for anxiety, depression and pain, but no better than other treatments. It adds to medical care and does not replace it.
