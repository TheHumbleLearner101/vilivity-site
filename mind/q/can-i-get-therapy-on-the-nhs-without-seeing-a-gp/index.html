Question

Can I get therapy on the NHS without seeing a GP?

  • Tier B thin Tested or observed (see the evidence)
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

In the UK you can refer yourself to NHS talking therapies online if you are 18 or over and registered with a GP, so you do not need to see a GP first. The NHS says some conditions, such as eating disorders, bipolar disorder, psychosis or drug and alcohol problems, usually need a GP referral.
