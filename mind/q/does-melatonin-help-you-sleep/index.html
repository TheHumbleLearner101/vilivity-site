Home / Mind / Melatonin for sleep: what it does, dose and rules by country

Question

Does melatonin help you sleep?

  • Tier B thin Tested, but not in people like you
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Melatonin may help some people fall asleep a little faster, but reviews disagree. In 24 trials of chronic insomnia it did not significantly improve sleep onset, total sleep time or sleep efficiency in adults, while 23 trials in adults with various conditions found small gains in self-rated sleep quality. Ethnicity was not reported in the first review's abstract.

What this is based on

  • Who was tested: People with chronic insomnia in 24 randomised placebo-controlled trials, analysed by age group. What was found: In adults with chronic insomnia melatonin did not significantly improve sleep onset, total sleep time or sleep efficiency. In children and adolescents it did, for sleep onset and total sleep time. The authors say further studies by age are needed.Tested Choi 2022 Study page
  • Who was tested: Adults with various conditions in 23 randomised trials. What was found: Melatonin improved Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index scores by 1.24 points (95% CI 0.71 to 1.77), with large differences between studies (I2 80.7 per cent). Gains were larger in people with respiratory diseases and metabolic disorders than in people with sleep disorders (0.67 points).Tested Fatemeh 2022 Study page
