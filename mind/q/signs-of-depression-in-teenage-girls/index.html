Question

signs of depression in teenage girls

  • Tier B thin Tested or observed (see the evidence)
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Low mood, anxiety, anger or withdrawal that lasts for weeks, or affects school or friendships, is a reason to see a doctor about your child. If your child talks about harming themselves or not wanting to live, treat it as urgent and use a crisis line.

From the card

In English child mental health services, children from Black and other minority backgrounds were more often referred through school or social care than through a doctor. The UK's NHS has guides for parents and information on services for young people. Childline offers free help to anyone under 19.
