Home / Mind / Black men, depression and getting support

Question

How do I cope with depression, particularly as a Black man?

  • Tier B thin Tested or observed (see the evidence)
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Depression is a health condition and treatable. See a GP if you think you may be depressed, as the NHS (UK) says not to delay, and do not wait for it to pass if it has lasted weeks or months. Tell one person you trust what is going on, and do not carry racism's strain alone.

What this is based on

  • A survey of African American men found that everyday racism and masculine norms both went with more barriers to seeking help, partly through feeling less in control and more depressed.
