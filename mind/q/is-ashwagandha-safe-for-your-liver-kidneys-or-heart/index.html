Question

Is ashwagandha safe for your liver, kidneys or heart?

  • Tier B thin Tested, but not in people like you
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-17
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

It has been linked to liver injury, deaths in people with liver disease, and one kidney-transplant rejection.

From the card

In short trials ashwagandha lowered cortisol and stress scores, and a review of 14 trials in mental disorders (mostly anxiety) found less anxiety, with depression less certain. We found no human trial for tension headaches, stress acne or gut. It has been linked to liver injury, deaths in people with liver disease, and one kidney-transplant rejection. Ask a pharmacist first.
