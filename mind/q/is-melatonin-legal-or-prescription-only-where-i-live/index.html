Home / Mind / Melatonin for sleep: what it does, dose and rules by country

Question

Is melatonin legal or prescription-only where I live?

  • Tier B thin Tested, but not in people like you
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

In the UK, melatonin is only available with a prescription, according to the NHS. In the US it is sold as a dietary supplement, according to NCCIH, and several other countries treat it as a prescription drug, so check your own country. Our entry opened no sources for other countries, so it states no other rule.

From the card

Melatonin may help you fall asleep a little faster for some people, but reviews disagree, and it did not clearly help adults with long-term insomnia. In Black patients it has been tested only for night blood pressure, with no effect. Rules differ by country: prescription-only in the UK, a supplement in the US. Labels often miss the real dose.
