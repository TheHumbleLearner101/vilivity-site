Home / Mind / Stress, burnout and blood pressure

Question

What is burnout and is it more common in Black workers?

  • Tier B thin Tested or observed (see the evidence)
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

The WHO describes burnout as exhaustion, distance or cynicism about your job and reduced effectiveness, from chronic workplace stress that has not been managed. We found no study of burnout in Black workers generally, so whether it is more common is untested. One review of 16 studies, mostly medical students and physicians, found differences between minority and other groups inconclusive.

What this is based on

  • Who was tested: Burnout in racial and ethnic minority groups. What was found: Differences in burnout between underrepresented minority and other groups were inconclusive, though several studies had nuanced findings. The authors call for better measurement and mitigation among underrepresented groups.Tested Lawrence 2022 Study page
  • Untested or not found: burnout in Black workers; whether workplace stress programmes work in Black employees; UK blood-pressure data by ethnicity; long-term trials of stress reduction on blood pressure in Black adults beyond one meditation trial. 12 Nov: burnout now has the WHO definition and one review (doctors and medical students; inconclusive by race); no study of burnout in Black workers generally and no trial of a burnout programme.Not tested, and gaps (from the card)

From the card

Reported discrimination is linked to a higher chance of high blood pressure, but the link is small and not proven to be cause. Stress relief such as meditation may add to treatment, not replace it. If stress is wearing you down, talk to someone and get your blood pressure checked.
