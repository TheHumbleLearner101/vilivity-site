Home / Mind / Racism, racial stress and mental health

Question

Does racism affect mental health?

  • Tier B moderate Tested or observed (see the evidence)
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

People who report experiencing racism have worse mental health, including more depression, anxiety and psychological stress. A review pooling 293 studies, mainly US, found this link, and it was stronger in cross-sectional than longitudinal data, so it shows a link and not proof of cause. The strain is real and it is not a personal failing. Do not wait until it is severe before asking for help.

  • Who was tested: 293 studies (333 articles), mainly US. What was found: Reported racism was linked to poorer mental health (negative mental health r = -0.23, 95% CI -0.24 to -0.21, 227 studies), including depression, anxiety and psychological stress, and to poorer general (r = -0.13) and physical health (r = -0.09). Effects were stronger in cross-sectional than longitudinal data. Ethnicity moderated some effects; the abstract text I read was cut off before the Black-specific comparison.Tested Paradies 2015 Study page
