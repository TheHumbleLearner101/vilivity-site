Home / Mind / Ashwagandha: stress, sleep, thyroid and liver cautions

Question

is ashwagandha safe for your thyroid

  • Tier B moderate Tested, but not in people like you
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

NCCIH says to avoid ashwagandha if you have a thyroid or autoimmune condition, unless your doctor agrees. One 50-person trial in India, in people with subclinical hypothyroidism, found improved TSH, T3 and T4 against placebo over 8 weeks, and our search found no repeat. If you take thyroid tablets, speak to your GP or pharmacist first.

What this is based on

From the card

Ashwagandha eased stress and helped sleep a little in short trials, but our search found no trial in Black participants. NCCIH says avoid it in pregnancy, while breastfeeding and with thyroid or autoimmune conditions, and rare liver injury is reported. Ask a pharmacist first, and stop if your skin or eyes turn yellow.
