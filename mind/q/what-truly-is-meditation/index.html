Question

What truly is meditation?

  • Tier A moderate Tested in people like you
  • Founder's own practice
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Meditation is an old practice across many cultures, including prayerful stillness, widely believed to calm the mind and protect the body from stress. In practice you pick a regular time and notice your breath, body or sounds. Tested types include Transcendental Meditation and mindfulness, and the evidence differs by type.

What this is based on

  • Meditation and prayerful stillness are old practices across many cultures and are widely believed to calm the mind and protect the body from stress.Tradition and common belief, not evidence
  • Pick a regular time, then notice your breath, body or sounds; bring your attention back when it wanders.Clinical guidance NHS, "Mindfulness"
  • Assuming all types have the same evidence: the heart trial was Transcendental Meditation.Tested Schneider 2012

From the card

Meditation is among the better-tested practices here. A trial in Black adults with heart disease found fewer deaths, heart attacks and strokes with Transcendental Meditation. A large review found benefit for anxiety, depression and pain, but no better than other treatments. It adds to medical care and does not replace it.
