Question

does meditation lower blood pressure long term

  • Tier A moderate Tested in people like you
  • Founder's own practice
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

In one trial of 201 Black men and women with heart disease, systolic pressure was 4.9 mmHg lower after an average 5.4 years than with health education, and deaths, heart attacks and strokes were 48% lower. It is one trial of one technique, with no independent replication found, so do not assume other types match it.

What this is based on

  • Meditation is among the better-tested practices here. A trial in Black adults with heart disease found fewer deaths, heart attacks and strokes with Transcendental Meditation. A large review found benefit for anxiety, depression and pain, but no better than other treatments. It adds to medical care and does not replace it.From the card's plain answer
  • Assuming all types have the same evidence: the heart trial was Transcendental Meditation.Tested Schneider 2012
  • Who was tested: 201 Black men and women with coronary heart disease, US. What was found: The primary outcome (death, heart attack or stroke) was 48% lower in the TM group (hazard ratio 0.52, 95% CI 0.29-0.92, P=0.025). The wider secondary composite fell 24% (HR 0.76, 0.51-1.13, not significant). Systolic blood pressure was 4.9 mmHg lower, and anger scores improved. Adherence was linked to survival.Tested Schneider 2012 Study page
