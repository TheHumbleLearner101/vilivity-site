Question

Is there a helpline to talk to someone?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

If you are in a crisis or thinking about suicide, in the UK call NHS 111 and choose the mental health option (England), call Samaritans free on 116 123 (24 hours), or call 999 in an emergency. In the US, call or text 988, or call 911.

From the card

Anxiety and depression are common and treatable, and the NHS says to seek help early. In the UK you can refer yourself to NHS talking therapies, including for social anxiety and panic, without seeing a GP. Whether therapies work differently in Black adults is untested in my search, so choose a service you can attend.
