Question

How do I heal from childhood trauma and emotional neglect?

  • Tier B thin Tested or observed (see the evidence)
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

For trauma, PTSD is among the problems NHS talking therapies in the UK treat by self-referral. See a GP or self-refer to NHS talking therapies if low mood, anxiety or trauma symptoms last more than a couple of weeks or stop you functioning. Therapy for trauma and emotional neglect in Black adults is untested or not found in this card.

From the card

A London study found stigma, silence and beliefs about causes slow help-seeking in African-descended communities, including in church groups. You can start without a diagnosis: in the UK you can refer yourself to NHS talking therapies for anxiety, depression and trauma. Telling one person you trust is a first step.
