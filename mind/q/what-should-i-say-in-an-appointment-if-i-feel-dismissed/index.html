Home / Mind / Being believed by doctors, and pain

Question

What should I say in an appointment if I feel dismissed?

  • Tier B moderate Tested or observed (see the evidence)
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Say what the pain stops you doing and how it affects your life; in the UK the GP is expected to ask and record your pain level. Note one or two things you most want to focus on beforehand. If pain lasts more than 12 weeks, ask for a specialist pain clinic referral, and ask what the plan is. The effect of these steps is untested.

From the card

Research backs your experience. In US studies Black patients were less likely than white patients to be given painkillers, and many white trainee doctors held false beliefs about biological differences that went with lower pain ratings. It is not you. Prepare, describe what the pain stops you doing, and ask for a plan.
