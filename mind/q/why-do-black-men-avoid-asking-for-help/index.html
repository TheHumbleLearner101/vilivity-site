Home / Mind / Black men, depression and getting support

Question

Why do Black men avoid asking for help?

  • Tier B thin Tested or observed (see the evidence)
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

In a survey of 458 African American men, recruited mainly in US barbershops, everyday racism and masculinity norms both went with higher barriers to seeking help; racism's link was partly explained by a lower sense of control and more depressive symptoms. It is one cross-sectional survey, so it cannot show cause. Many Black men say they are taught to be strong and carry on.

What this is based on
