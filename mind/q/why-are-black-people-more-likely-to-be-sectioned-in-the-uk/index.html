Question

Why are Black people more likely to be sectioned in the UK?

  • Tier B moderate Tested or observed (see the evidence)
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Why Black people are detained more often in England is not settled. In a review of 71 studies with about 1.95 million participants, Black Caribbean patients had 2.53 times the odds, and Black African patients 2.27 times, of being compulsorily admitted compared with white patients. Explanations offered include higher prevalence of psychosis, perceived risk of violence, police contact, mistrust of GPs and ethnic disadvantage. The authors say these need testing group by group.
