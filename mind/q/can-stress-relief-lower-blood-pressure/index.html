Home / Mind / Stress, burnout and blood pressure

Question

Can stress relief lower blood pressure?

  • Tier B thin Tested or observed (see the evidence)
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Stress relief such as meditation may add to treatment, not replace it. In Black women with hypertension, one randomised trial of transcendental meditation found blood pressure reductions of up to 7 mmHg. Ask your doctor before relying on any practice, get your blood pressure checked, and never stop prescribed treatment because of it.
