Home / Mind / Childhood trauma and where to start

Question

What are the common symptoms of trauma?

  • Tier B moderate Tested, but not in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

See a doctor, or self-refer to talking therapies where that exists, if you have flashbacks, nightmares, feeling numb or cut off, intense shame or guilt, or difficulty controlling emotions that last more than a few weeks. For a child who has been hurt or frightened and is not themselves, with changes in sleep, school, anger or fear, see a doctor, school or child mental health service.

Also asked as

  • What are the characteristics and symptoms of trauma and stressor-related disorders in children?

From the card

Hard things in childhood, such as abuse, neglect or violence at home, raise the chance of anxiety, depression and PTSD later. What happened was not your fault. Trauma-focused therapies help, and you can start with a GP or, in England, a self-referral to NHS talking therapies. You do not have to tell the whole story at once.
