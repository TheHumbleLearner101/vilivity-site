Home / Mind / Sleep, short sleep and sleep apnoea

Question

how to sleep better with anxiety

  • Tier B moderate Tested or observed (see the evidence)
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

See a GP if sleep problems come with low mood or anxiety, or if poor sleep lasts 3 months or more. Regular habits help insomnia: wake at the same time every day, keep the bedroom dark and quiet, and relax for at least an hour before bed. In a crisis in the UK, call NHS 111 and choose the mental health option (England), or call 999 in an emergency.

From the card

In a US study Black adults were more likely to sleep under six hours and to have sleep apnoea than white adults, and most people with apnoea had never been told. Discrimination is also linked to shorter sleep. If you snore loudly, gasp or are always tired, see a GP. Regular habits help insomnia.
