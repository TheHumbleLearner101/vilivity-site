Question

Do talking therapies work for Black people?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Whether talking therapies work differently in Black adults is untested in our search. Choose a service you can attend, and ask for a service or therapist you feel comfortable with, which is common advice and untested. In the UK, NHS talking therapies take self-referrals.

What this is based on

  • Anxiety and depression are common and treatable, and the NHS says to seek help early. In the UK you can refer yourself to NHS talking therapies, including for social anxiety and panic, without seeing a GP.
