Tier B: Tested or observed (see the evidence) (15)

  • Ashwagandha: stress, sleep, thyroid and liver cautions Tier B moderate Harm signalAshwagandha eased stress and helped sleep a little in short trials, but our search found no trial in Black participants.
  • Autism and ADHD: being missed, delayed or misdiagnosed Tier B moderateA US review of 20 studies found delayed diagnosis and misdiagnosis of autism were the main errors, driven by limited knowledge, insurance, provider bias and assessment tools that may not suit Black and Hispanic families.
  • Being believed by doctors, and pain Tier B moderateResearch backs your experience.
  • Black men, depression and getting support Tier B thinA survey of African American men found that everyday racism and masculine norms both went with more barriers to seeking help, partly through feeling less in control and more depressed.
  • Breathwork and slow breathing Tier B moderate Founder's own practiceSlow breathing practice lowers stress and blood pressure a little in trials of adults generally.
  • Childhood trauma and where to start Tier B moderateHard things in childhood, such as abuse, neglect or violence at home, raise the chance of anxiety, depression and PTSD later.
  • Colourism, skin tone and mental health Tier B moderateIn US surveys of Black adults, experiencing colourism within the community went with higher odds of a psychiatric disorder, and among older adults the link between discrimination and distress was stronger in darker-skinned people.
  • Finding a Black therapist or one who understands being Black Tier B moderateDirectories in the UK and US list Black or culturally aware therapists.
  • Getting a child mental health support Tier B thinIn English child mental health services, children from Black and other minority backgrounds were more often referred through school or social care than through a doctor.
  • Melatonin for sleep: what it does, dose and rules by country Tier B thin Harm signalMelatonin may help you fall asleep a little faster for some people, but reviews disagree, and it did not clearly help adults with long-term insomnia.
  • Psychosis, sectioning and misdiagnosis in the UK Tier B moderateIn England, Black people are detained under the Mental Health Act more often than any other group, and a large review found Black Caribbean and Black African patients were far more likely to be compulsorily admitted.
  • Racism, racial stress and mental health Tier B moderateMany studies find that people who report experiencing racism have worse mental health, including more depression, anxiety and stress.
  • Sleep, short sleep and sleep apnoea Tier B moderateIn a US study Black adults were more likely to sleep under six hours and to have sleep apnoea than white adults, and most people with apnoea had never been told.
  • Stigma, talking to family and starting therapy Tier B thinA London study found stigma, silence and beliefs about causes slow help-seeking in African-descended communities, including in church groups.
  • Stress, burnout and blood pressure Tier B thinReported discrimination is linked to a higher chance of high blood pressure, but the link is small and not proven to be cause.
