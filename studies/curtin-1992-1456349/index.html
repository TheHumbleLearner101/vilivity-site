  • Salt and blood pressure Tier A moderate

    D Historical evidence on salt, the slave trade and conditions of slavery (n=not applicable)

    Historical review of the slavery hypothesis.

    The review finds there was no salt deficiency in the parts of Africa that supplied slaves, present-day West Africans do not have a high incidence of hypertension, deaths aboard slave ships were not shown to have been mainly salt-depleting, and the West Indies evidence was not relevant to the US.
