Study: Wise 2012, American Journal of Epidemiology

Hair relaxer use and risk of uterine leiomyomata in African-American women

Citation

Wise LA, Palmer JR, Reich D, Cozier YC et al (2012). American Journal of Epidemiology. PubMed id 22234483.

Open on PubMed

  • Chemical relaxers and hair straighteners Tier B moderate

    B 23,580 premenopausal Black women, Black Women's Health Study; 7,146 fibroid cases (n=23580)

    Prospective cohort 1997-2009.

    Ever against never use of relaxers: incidence rate ratio 1.17 (95% CI 1.06-1.30), with trends for frequency, duration and number of scalp burns. The authors say the findings raise the hypothesis that relaxer use increases fibroid risk.
