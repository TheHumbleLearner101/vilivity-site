  • Sarcoidosis: symptoms, risk and follow-up Tier B thin

    B 1,071 adults with confirmed pulmonary sarcoidosis in the British Thoracic Society ILD Registry, 2013 to 2024 (ethnicity recorded for 918: 55.4% white, 14.2% non-white including Black, South Asian and mixed) (n=1071)

    Retrospective registry study with multivariable logistic regression.

    Non-white patients presented younger (52 against 54 years) with more breathlessness (46% against 33%) and cough (44% against 27%), more CT nodularity (54% against 36%) and abnormal liver tests (16% against 9%), and more comorbidity. Lung physiology was comparable. Immunosuppression was more likely with non-white ethnicity (odds ratio 3.20) and male sex (2.34). The authors say treatment decisions were more influenced by sex and ethnicity than by lung function or imaging.

    As recorded in this card: Bączek KK, Leng Cheng S, Amanda G, Achaiah A et al (2025), BMJ Open Respiratory Research, [primary]

    Note: UK registry; non-white is a pooled group, so Black adults alone are not separated in the abstract. Abstract read.
