Study: Schneider 2012, Circulation: Cardiovascular Quality and Outcomes

Stress reduction in the secondary prevention of cardiovascular disease: randomized, controlled trial of transcendental meditation and health education in Blacks

Citation

Schneider RH, Grim CE, Rainforth MV, Kotchen T et al (2012). Circulation: Cardiovascular Quality and Outcomes. PubMed id 23149426.

Open on PubMed

  • Meditation Tier A moderate

    A 201 Black men and women with coronary heart disease, US (n=201)

    Randomised controlled trial of the Transcendental Meditation (TM) programme against health education, 5.4 years average follow-up.

    The primary outcome (death, heart attack or stroke) was 48% lower in the TM group (hazard ratio 0.52, 95% CI 0.29-0.92, P=0.025). The wider secondary composite fell 24% (HR 0.76, 0.51-1.13, not significant). Systolic blood pressure was 4.9 mmHg lower, and anger scores improved. Adherence was linked to survival.

    As recorded in this card: Schneider RH, Grim CE, Rainforth MV, Kotchen T et al (2012), Circulation: Cardiovascular Quality and Outcomes, [primary]

    Note: One trial of one technique (TM), small number of events, no independent replication found in my search. Who funded and ran it was not checked; the research group's link to TM was not checked (UNVERIFIED).
