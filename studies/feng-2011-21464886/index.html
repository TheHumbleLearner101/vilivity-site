Study: Feng 2011, Journal of Clinical and Aesthetic Dermatology

Reduction of "Ashiness" in Skin of Color with a Lipid-rich Moisturizing Body Wash

Citation

Feng L, Hawkins S (2011). Journal of Clinical and Aesthetic Dermatology. PubMed id 21464886.

Open on PubMed

Each card gives its own wording for who was tested, how and what was found.

  • Moisturising and 'ashy' skin Tier A thin

    A 55 healthy women with skin types IV-VI and visible ashiness, Dallas (n=55)

    Randomised, double-blind, 3 weeks: a moisturising body wash (marketed product) against a marketed syndet bar.

    The body wash improved transepidermal water loss and expert- and self-assessed dryness; participants reported reduced ashiness at all sites. Similar results were seen with the syndet bar. The authors concluded that regular use of mild cleansers that support the barrier was associated with reduced ashiness.

    As recorded in this card: Feng L, Hawkins S (2011), Journal of Clinical and Aesthetic Dermatology, [primary]

    Note: Marketed products; funding not stated in the abstract, funding not checked. No untreated group, and both products improved ashiness, so it does not isolate a moisturiser.
