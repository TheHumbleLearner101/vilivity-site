Study: Evang 2021, European Journal of Nutrition

Can the supplementary consumption of baobab (Adansonia digitata L.) fruit pulp improve the hemoglobin levels and iron status of schoolchildren in Kenya? Findings of a randomized controlled intervention trial

Citation

Evang EC, Habte TY, Owino WO, Krawinkel MB (2021). European Journal of Nutrition. PubMed id 33355689.

Open on PubMed

Each card gives its own wording for who was tested, how and what was found.

  • Baobab fruit: blood sugar, iron and vitamin C Tier B thin

    A 58 Kenyan schoolchildren aged 6 to 12 with haemoglobin below 12.2 g/dl (n=58 (29 per group))

    Single-blind randomised trial: a drink with baobab fruit pulp or an equal-energy drink daily for 12 weeks.

    Haemoglobin, ferritin and soluble transferrin receptor did not differ significantly between the groups. Haemoglobin rose 2.2% with baobab and fell 1.2% in the control group, a difference the authors did not find significant.

    As recorded in this card: Evang EC, Habte TY, Owino WO, Krawinkel MB (2021), European Journal of Nutrition, [primary]

    Note: Small, in children, for iron status; the authors suggest the pulp's vitamin C could still help iron absorption.
