Study: Kindred 2011, Cutis

Comparative evaluation of men's depilatory composition versus razor in black men

Citation

Kindred C, Oresajo CO, Yatskayer M, Halder RM (2011). Cutis. PubMed id 21916277.

  • Shaving and razor bumps (pseudofolliculitis barbae) Tier A thin

    A Black men (single centre, 1 week) (n=not stated)

    Randomised, split-face trial comparing three depilatory compositions with a manual razor.

    The depilatory creams produced fewer papules and more irritation than the razor; the irritation was transient. One composition was withdrawn because of a high rate of adverse events. Skin looked and felt smoother with the creams. The authors recommend depilatories for people who develop PFB or are unsatisfied with razors.

    As recorded in this card: Kindred C, Oresajo CO, Yatskayer M, Halder RM (2011), Cutis, [primary]

    Note: One week only, preliminary (the abstract's own wording). Funding and author affiliations not checked.
