Study: Dos 2019, Heliyon

Raman spectroscopy and electronic microscopy structural studies of Caucasian and Afro human hair

Citation

Dos Santos et al (2019). Heliyon. PubMed id 31111104.

  • Colouring and bleaching afro-textured hair Tier D

    C Caucasian and Afro hair samples, treated with heat, bleach and straightening alone and together (n=not stated)

    Laboratory: Raman spectroscopy and electron microscopy.

    Bleaching, straightening and heat changed chemical bonds and protein structure; excessive heat caused the largest change, and applying several treatments to the same fibres caused even more pronounced changes. The two hair groups showed small differences for each treatment.

    As recorded in this card: Dos Santos et al (2019), Heliyon, [primary]

    Note: Hair samples, not people.
