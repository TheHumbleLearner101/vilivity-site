Study: Oladele 2016, European Journal of Nutrition

Impact of resistant starch in three plantain (Musa AAB) products on glycaemic response of healthy volunteers

Citation

Oladele EO, Williamson G (2016). European Journal of Nutrition. PubMed id 25542206.

  • Plantain, yam and cassava: blood sugar and safety Tier B thin

    B Ten healthy volunteers (UK study with Nigerian plantain products) (n=10)

    Test meals of boiled unripe plantain, plantain crisps and ripe plantain against white bread.

    Mean glycaemic index was 44.9 for boiled unripe plantain, 55.0 for the crisps and 38 for ripe raw plantain, lower than the Nigerian review's reported plantain values. The authors say the link with resistant starch is confounded by other constituents.

    As recorded in this card: Oladele EO, Williamson G (2016), European Journal of Nutrition, [primary]

    Note: Ten people; ancestry not stated; one plantain variety and 25 g carbohydrate portions.
