Study: Del 2018, Journal of the American Academy of Dermatology

Randomized, placebo-controlled, double-blind study of oral tranexamic acid in the treatment of moderate-to-severe melasma

Citation

Del Rosario E, Florez-Pollack S, Zapata L et al (2018). Journal of the American Academy of Dermatology. PubMed id 28987494.

Open on PubMed

  • Melasma Tier B moderate

    B 44 people with moderate-to-severe melasma, mostly Hispanic women, single centre (n=44)

    Randomised, placebo-controlled trial of oral tranexamic acid for 3 months, with sunscreen.

    Melasma severity score fell 49% on tranexamic acid and 18% on placebo at 3 months; 3 months after stopping the gap had narrowed (26% against 19% from baseline). No serious adverse events.

    As recorded in this card: Del Rosario E, Florez-Pollack S, Zapata L et al (2018), Journal of the American Academy of Dermatology, [primary]

    Note: Predominantly Hispanic women, per its own limitations line. Tranexamic acid is a prescription medicine.
