Study: Liu 2005, Clinical and Experimental Dermatology

Clinical profiles of vitiligo in China: an analysis of 3742 patients

Citation

Liu JB, Li M, Yang S, et al (2005). Clinical and Experimental Dermatology. PubMed id 15953059.

Open on PubMed

Cards that cite this study (1)

Each card gives its own wording for who was tested, how and what was found.

  • Vitiligo, spread, starting age and treatment options Tier B thin

    B 3,742 outpatients with vitiligo in China (n=3742)

    Retrospective questionnaire survey.

    Mean age of onset was 18.88 years and more than 60% were affected before 20. Segmental vitiligo began earlier (15.55 years). After 3 to 5 years, 99% of active vitiligo had worsened or shifted from one clinical type to another.

    As recorded in this card: Liu JB, Li M, Yang S, et al (2005), Clinical and Experimental Dermatology, [primary]

    Note: Chinese clinic population, retrospective, and the 99% applies to patients whose vitiligo was classed as active, not to everyone with vitiligo.
