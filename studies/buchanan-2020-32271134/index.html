Study: Buchanan 2020, Sports Health

Sudden Death Associated With Sickle Cell Trait Before and After Mandatory Screening

Citation

Buchanan BK, Siebert DM, Zigman Suchsland ML, Drezner JA et al (2020). Sports Health. PubMed id 32271134.

Open on PubMed

  • Sickle cell trait and hard exercise Tier B moderate

    B 8,309,050 NCAA athlete-years, 2000-2019 (n=8309050)

    Observational comparison of sudden death associated with sickle cell trait before and after mandatory NCAA screening and education in 2010.

    In Division I football the incidence of death associated with the trait fell from 1 in 28,145 athlete-years before legislation (9 deaths) to 1 in 250,468 after (1 death), a relative risk of 0.112 (95% CI 0.003-0.811). Cases outside football were rare. The authors' stated hypothesis was that legislation would reduce incidence.

    As recorded in this card: Buchanan BK, Siebert DM, Zigman Suchsland ML, Drezner JA et al (2020), Sports Health, [primary]

    Note: Before-and-after, not randomised; the policy bundled screening and education, so it cannot separate them.
