  • Psychosis, sectioning and misdiagnosis in the UK Tier B moderate

    B 83 citations, people aged 16 to 64 with first-onset psychosis, England 1950 to 2009 (n=83 studies)

    Systematic review and meta-analysis of incidence.

    Rates of psychosis were higher in several ethnic minority groups than in white British people. For schizophrenia the pooled rate ratio was 5.6 for Black Caribbean people (95% CI 3.4 to 9.2) and 4.7 for Black African people (3.3 to 6.8). The studies were mostly cross-sectional, which limits conclusions about cause.

    As recorded in this card: Kirkbride JB, Errazuriz A, Croudace TJ et al (2012), PLoS One, [review]

    Note: Abstract read. Incidence studies were mostly cross-sectional, with high heterogeneity; the review cannot say why rates differ.
