Study: Jillella 2026, Phytotherapy Research

A Prospective, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Clinical Study of Efficacy and Safety of Ashwagandha Root Extract Capsule on Skin and Hair Health in Healthy Adults

Citation

Jillella A, Keerthi N, Venkateswar Reddy J (2026). Phytotherapy Research. PubMed id 41770039.

Open on PubMed

Cards that cite this study (1)

Each card gives its own wording for who was tested, how and what was found.

  • Ashwagandha: cortisol, mood, skin, hair, heart, kidneys and liver Tier B thin

    B Adults aged 18 to 60 with hair loss or skin conditions (number not stated in the abstract), one clinic in Hyderabad, India (n=not stated in the abstract)

    Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, 300 mg standardised root extract twice a day for 75 days.

    Greater improvement than placebo in transepidermal water loss (skin barrier), in hair density, growth and anagen:telogen ratio, and in skin and hair quality-of-life scores. No adverse events. The extract was supplied by the manufacturer, Ixoreal Biomed.

    As recorded in this card: Jillella A, Keerthi N, Venkateswar Reddy J (2026), Phytotherapy Research, [primary]

    Note: One clinic, manufacturer-supplied product, sample size not in the abstract. Ethnicity and hair type not stated, so nothing is known about afro-textured hair or skin of colour.
