A Systematic Review of Nutrition, Supplement, and Herbal-Based Adjunctive Therapies for Vitiligo

Shakhbazova A, Wu H, Chambers CJ (2021). Journal of Alternative and Complementary Medicine. PubMed id 33337930.

  • Vitiligo, spread, starting age and treatment options Tier B thin

    B Human studies of diet, supplements and herbs in vitiligo (26 studies reviewed) (n=not stated)

    Systematic review of adjunctive therapies.

    Supplements reviewed included Ginkgo biloba, oral Polypodium leucotomos, alpha lipoic acid, vitamins B12, D and E, folic acid, phenylalanine and Nigella sativa oil. The authors call the evidence promising and say more studies are needed.
