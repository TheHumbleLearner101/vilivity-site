Study: Sriperumbuduri 2020, Kidney International Reports

Ashwagandha and Kidney Transplant Rejection

Citation

Sriperumbuduri S, Umar MS, Lajoie-Starkell G, Fairhead TR et al (2020). Kidney International Reports. PubMed id 33305135.

  • Ashwagandha: cortisol, mood, skin, hair, heart, kidneys and liver Tier B thin

    B One 69-year-old male kidney transplant recipient, Ottawa, Canada (n=1)

    Case report.

    He developed acute rejection requiring removal of the transplanted kidney after using ashwagandha, while taking tacrolimus, mycophenolate and prednisone. The authors recommend warning transplant recipients about herbal products including ashwagandha.

    As recorded in this card: Sriperumbuduri S, Umar MS, Lajoie-Starkell G, Fairhead TR et al (2020), Kidney International Reports, [primary]

    Note: One case. It cannot show that ashwagandha caused the rejection. I read the introduction and case presentation, not the whole discussion.
