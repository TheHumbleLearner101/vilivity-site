Study: Inker 2021, New England Journal of Medicine

New Creatinine- and Cystatin C-Based Equations to Estimate GFR without Race

Citation

Inker LA, Eneanya ND, Coresh J, Tighiouart H et al (2021). New England Journal of Medicine. PubMed id 34554658.

Open on PubMed

  • Kidney disease, the APOL1 gene and the eGFR race adjustment Tier B moderate

    B 8,254 participants (31.5% Black) for the creatinine equation and 5,352 (39.7% Black) for creatinine plus cystatin C; validated in 4,050 (14.3% Black) (n=4050 in validation)

    Development and validation of eGFR equations against measured GFR.

    The current equation with a race term overestimated measured GFR in Black participants by a median of 3.7 mL/min/1.73 m2 and slightly in others (0.5). Omitting the race term underestimated it in Black participants by 7.1. A new race-free creatinine equation underestimated in Black participants by 3.6 and overestimated in non-Black by 3.9. Creatinine plus cystatin C equations were more accurate with smaller differences between groups.

    As recorded in this card: Inker LA, Eneanya ND, Coresh J, Tighiouart H et al (2021), New England Journal of Medicine, [primary]

    Note: US data; funded by the National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases.
