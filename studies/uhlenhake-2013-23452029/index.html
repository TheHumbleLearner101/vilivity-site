Study: Uhlenhake 2013, International Journal of Dermatology

Prospective histologic examinations in patients who practice traumatic hairstyling

Citation

Uhlenhake E, Mehregan DR (2013). International Journal of Dermatology. PubMed id 23452029.

Open on PubMed

Cards that cite this study (2)

Each card gives its own wording for who was tested, how and what was found.

  • CCCA: scarring hair loss at the crown Tier B thin

    B 8 African American women with no clinical hair loss who used chemical or heat straighteners (n=8)

    Prospective scalp biopsy (4 mm punch).

    Every biopsy showed inflammation around the follicle opening and a ring of fibrosis, with no follicle loss. The authors say these are findings characteristic of CCCA and suggest a histological prelude, and that CCCA may be a common pathway of inflammation and scarring that traumatic hairstyling can set off in genetically predisposed people.

    As recorded in this card: Uhlenhake E, Mehregan DR (2013), International Journal of Dermatology, [primary]

    Note: Eight women, no comparison group: cannot show that styling caused the changes.
