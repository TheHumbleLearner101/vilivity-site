Dietary supplements for treating osteoarthritis: a systematic review and meta-analysis

Liu X, Machado GC, Eyles JP, Ravi V, Hunter DJ (2018). British Journal of Sports Medicine. PubMed id 29018060.

  • Collagen for hair, nails and joints Tier B thin

    B People with hand, hip or knee osteoarthritis in 69 randomised trials of 20 dietary supplements (n=not recorded)

    Systematic review and meta-analysis with GRADE rating.

    Collagen hydrolysate was one of 7 supplements with large short-term pain effects. No supplement had clinically important effects on pain at long term, and evidence quality was rated very low to high, very low overall.

    As recorded in this card: Liu X, Machado GC, Eyles JP, Ravi V, Hunter DJ (2018), British Journal of Sports Medicine, [review]

    Note: Collagen is one of 20 supplements here. Ethnicity not reported in the abstract.
