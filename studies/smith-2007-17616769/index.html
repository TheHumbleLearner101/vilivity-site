Study: Smith 2007, American Journal of Clinical Nutrition

A low-glycemic-load diet improves symptoms in acne vulgaris patients: a randomized controlled trial

Citation

Smith RN, Mann NJ, Braue A, Makelainen H et al (2007). American Journal of Clinical Nutrition. PubMed id 17616769.

  • Does diet cause acne? Sugar, dairy and foods linked to spots Tier B thin

    B 43 male acne patients aged 15 to 25 in Australia (n=43)

    Randomised controlled trial, 12 weeks: low-glycaemic-load diet against a carbohydrate-dense control diet.

    Total lesion counts fell more with the low-glycaemic-load diet (-23.5 vs -12.0, P=0.03). Weight and insulin sensitivity also improved more, so the authors say weight loss and diet effects cannot be separated.

    As recorded in this card: Smith RN, Mann NJ, Braue A, Makelainen H et al (2007), American Journal of Clinical Nutrition, [primary]

    Note: Young men only; not a trial in Black participants; 43 people.
