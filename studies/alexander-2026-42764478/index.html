Study: Alexander 2026, AIDS Care

A digital mindfulness application for African American caregivers of people living with HIV/AIDS: feasibility trial findings

Citation

Alexander K, Carswell SB, Woolworth R, Simpson A et al (2026). AIDS Care. PubMed id 42764478.

Open on PubMed

Each card gives its own wording for who was tested, how and what was found.

  • Coping with anxiety day to day Tier B thin

    A 30 African American caregivers of people with HIV/AIDS, US (n=30)

    Four-week single-group feasibility trial of a mindfulness app.

    All 30 completed it, and 83.3% rated usability acceptable. Baseline anxiety and depression risk were high. Change in anxiety was not tested; the authors call for a randomised trial.

    As recorded in this card: Alexander K, Carswell SB, Woolworth R, Simpson A et al (2026), AIDS Care, [primary]

    Note: Feasibility only: it does not show that the app reduces anxiety.
