Study: Bernardi 2016, BMC Women's Health

The association between subjective assessment of menstrual bleeding and measures of iron deficiency anemia in premenopausal African-American women: a cross-sectional study

Citation

Bernardi LA, Ghant MS, Andrade C, Recht H et al (2016). BMC Women's Health. PubMed id 27524363.

Open on PubMed

Cards that cite this study (1)

Each card gives its own wording for who was tested, how and what was found.

  • Iron, heavy periods and fibroids Tier B moderate

    B 44 premenopausal African American women, a pilot (n=44)

    Cross-sectional survey and blood tests.

    Iron deficiency was present in 68.2% and anaemia in 18.2%. Women who reported heavy or very heavy periods had lower haemoglobin, haematocrit and ferritin and more anaemia symptoms. Of those reporting heavy periods, 35% were anaemic. The authors recommend evaluation for iron deficiency and anaemia whenever African American women report heavy bleeding.

    As recorded in this card: Bernardi LA, Ghant MS, Andrade C, Recht H et al (2016), BMC Women's Health, [primary]

    Note: Pilot of 44; not representative.
