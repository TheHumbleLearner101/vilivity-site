Study: Hochman 2015, Australasian Journal of Dermatology

Does ear keloid formation depend on the type of earrings or piercing jewellery?

Citation

Hochman B, Isoldi FC, Silveira TS, Borba GC et al (2015). Australasian Journal of Dermatology. PubMed id 25715880.

Open on PubMed

  • Keloids and piercings Tier B moderate

    B 141 ear keloids, Brazil (n=141)

    Case series on jewellery type.

    Most keloids sat on the back of the earlobe and most earrings had metallic backs. The authors suggest the back of the earring may worsen local neurogenic inflammation.

    As recorded in this card: Hochman B, Isoldi FC, Silveira TS, Borba GC et al (2015), Australasian Journal of Dermatology, [primary]

    Note: Hypothesis only; no comparison with other jewellery.
