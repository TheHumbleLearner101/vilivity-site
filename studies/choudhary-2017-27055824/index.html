Study: Choudhary 2017, Journal of Evidence-Based Complementary & Alternative Medicine

Body Weight Management in Adults Under Chronic Stress Through Treatment With Ashwagandha Root Extract: A Double-Blind, Randomized, Placebo-Controlled Trial

Citation

Choudhary D, Bhattacharyya S, Joshi K (2017). Journal of Evidence-Based Complementary & Alternative Medicine. PubMed id 27055824.

Open on PubMed

Cards that cite this study (1)

Each card gives its own wording for who was tested, how and what was found.

  • Ashwagandha: cortisol, mood, skin, hair, heart, kidneys and liver Tier B thin

    B 52 adults under chronic stress (country and ethnicity not stated in the abstract) (n=52)

    Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, 300 mg root extract twice a day for 8 weeks.

    Perceived stress, food cravings, eating behaviour, serum cortisol, body weight and BMI all improved compared with placebo. The extract was reported safe and tolerable.

    As recorded in this card: Choudhary D, Bhattacharyya S, Joshi K (2017), Journal of Evidence-Based Complementary & Alternative Medicine, [primary]

    Note: Small. The size of the weight change is not in the abstract. Waist circumference was not reported.
