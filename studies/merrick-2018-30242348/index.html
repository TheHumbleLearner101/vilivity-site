  • Childhood trauma and where to start Tier B moderate

    B 214,157 adults in 23 US states in the 2011 to 2014 Behavioral Risk Factor Surveillance System (n=214157)

    Cross-sectional nationally representative telephone survey of adverse childhood experiences (ACEs).

    61.55% had at least 1 ACE and 24.64% reported 3 or more. Higher ACE scores were reported by people who identified as Black (mean 1.69, 95% CI 1.62 to 1.76), Hispanic (1.80) or multiracial (2.52), and by those with lower income, education and employment, and by gay, lesbian or bisexual people.

    As recorded in this card: Merrick MT, Ford DC, Ports KA, Guinn AS (2018), JAMA Pediatrics, [primary]

    Note: US adults; self-reported memories of childhood, which can under- or over-report. Abstract text cut after the list of groups.
