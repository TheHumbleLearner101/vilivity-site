Effects of Withania somnifera root extract serum application on hair health in healthy adults: A prospective, double-blind, randomized, parallel, placebo-controlled study

Citation

Yerram C, Jillella A, Reddy V (2023). Journal of Ayurveda and Integrative Medicine. PubMed id 38006746.

  • Ashwagandha: cortisol, mood, skin, hair, heart, kidneys and liver Tier B thin

    B Healthy adults aged 18 to 45 with mild to moderate hair loss (61 analysed), India (n=61)

    Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, topical ashwagandha root-extract serum for 75 days.

    Hair shedding fell in the 60-second comb test and hair density, growth and thickness improved compared with placebo at day 75.

    As recorded in this card: Yerram C, Jillella A, Reddy V (2023), Journal of Ayurveda and Integrative Medicine, [primary]

    Note: A serum on the scalp, not a capsule. Hair type not stated.
