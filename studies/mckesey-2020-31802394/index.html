Study: McKesey 2020, American Journal of Clinical Dermatology

Melasma Treatment: An Evidence-Based Review

Citation

McKesey J, Tovar-Garza A, Pandya AG (2020). American Journal of Clinical Dermatology. PubMed id 31802394.

Open on PubMed

  • Melasma Tier B moderate

    B Participants in 113 controlled studies of melasma (6,897 people); groups not broken down in the abstract (n=6897)

    Systematic review of randomised and controlled clinical trials.

    Triple combination cream (hydroquinone, tretinoin, steroid) and hydroquinone alone were the most effective and best studied. Peels and lasers were equal or inferior to topical creams with more adverse effects. Oral tranexamic acid is promising. The authors cite small samples, mixed designs and lack of long-term follow-up.

    As recorded in this card: McKesey J, Tovar-Garza A, Pandya AG (2020), American Journal of Clinical Dermatology, [review]
