Melatonin Natural Health Products and Supplements: Presence of Serotonin and Significant Variability of Melatonin Content

  • Melatonin for sleep: what it does, dose and rules by country Tier B thin

    C 31 commercial melatonin supplements (different brands and forms) (n=31)

    Laboratory analysis of melatonin and serotonin content by chromatography.

    Melatonin content ranged from 83 per cent below to 478 per cent above the label. Lot-to-lot variation reached 465 per cent. More than 71 per cent of products missed the label by more than 10 per cent, and serotonin was found in eight (about a quarter).

    As recorded in this card: Erland LA, Saxena PK (2017), Journal of Clinical Sleep Medicine, [primary]

    Note: Products sold in one market at one time. It is a reason to doubt labels and ranks no product.
