Study: Roshdy 2013, International Journal of Women's Health

Treatment of symptomatic uterine fibroids with green tea extract: a pilot randomized controlled clinical study

Citation

Roshdy E, Rajaratnam V, Maitra S, Sabry M et al (2013). International Journal of Women's Health. PubMed id 23950663.

Open on PubMed

  • Fibroids, food and supplements Tier B thin

    B 39 women aged 18-50 with symptomatic fibroids (33 completed), Egypt (n=39)

    Pilot double-blind placebo-controlled randomised trial of green tea extract (EGCG).

    Fibroid volume fell significantly in the green tea extract group (22 women) while it rose 24.3% in the placebo group (11 women). Symptom severity fell 32.4%, quality of life improved 18.5%, haemoglobin rose 0.7 g/dL and monthly blood loss fell from 71 to 45 mL. No adverse effects were observed.

    As recorded in this card: Roshdy E, Rajaratnam V, Maitra S, Sabry M et al (2013), International Journal of Women's Health, [primary]

    Note: Pilot with a 2:1 split and small groups; funding not checked.
