The effect of apple cider vinegar on lipid profiles and glycemic parameters: a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized clinical trials

Citation

Hadi A, Pourmasoumi M, Najafgholizadeh A, Clark CCT et al (2021). BMC Complementary Medicine and Therapies. PubMed id 34187442.

  • Apple cider vinegar: weight, blood sugar and safety Tier B thin

    B Adults in nine randomised trials (ten study arms) of apple cider vinegar for at least two weeks (ethnicity not stated in the abstract) (n=not stated in the abstract)

    Systematic review and meta-analysis of randomised trials: lipids and blood sugar.

    Apple cider vinegar lowered total cholesterol by about 6 mg/dL, fasting glucose by about 8 mg/dL and HbA1c by 0.5 points. There was no effect on LDL, HDL, insulin or HOMA-IR. Results were stronger in people with type 2 diabetes and with longer use, and variation between studies was moderate to high.

    As recorded in this card: Hadi A, Pourmasoumi M, Najafgholizadeh A, Clark CCT et al (2021), BMC Complementary Medicine and Therapies, [review]
