Hair relaxer use and risk of uterine cancer in the Black Women's Health Study

Citation

Bertrand KA, Delp L, Coogan PF, Cozier YC et al (2023). Environmental Research. PubMed id 37821068.

Open on PubMed

  • Chemical relaxers and hair straighteners Tier B moderate

    B 44,798 Black women with an intact uterus in the Black Women's Health Study; 347 uterine cancers (n=44798)

    Prospective cohort, 1997-2019.

    Heavy use (15 or more years, at least 5 times a year) against never or light use gave a hazard ratio of 1.18 (95% CI 0.81-1.71), not significant. Among postmenopausal women, heavy use was 1.64 (1.01-2.64) and 20 or more years of use 1.71 (1.08-2.72). Premenopausal women: null.
