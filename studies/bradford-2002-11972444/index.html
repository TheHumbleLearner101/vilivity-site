  • How some medicines work differently by gene Tier B moderate

    B Published allele-frequency studies of CYP2D6 in European, Asian and African populations (n=not stated in the abstract)

    Review of allele frequencies.

    Functional alleles are about 71% in European Caucasians, about 50% in Asians and about 50% in Africans and African Americans. Reduced-function alleles are about 35% in African populations (mainly *17); non-functional alleles are 14.5% in African Americans and 6.3% in Africans. Non-functional and reduced-function alleles make up about half of alleles in Black populations, with greater variety.

    As recorded in this card: Bradford LD (2002), Pharmacogenomics, [review]
