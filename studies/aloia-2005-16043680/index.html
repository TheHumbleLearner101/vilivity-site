Study: Aloia 2005, Archives of Internal Medicine

A randomized controlled trial of vitamin D3 supplementation in African American women

Citation

Aloia JF, Talwar SA, Pollack S, Yeh J (2005). Archives of Internal Medicine. PubMed id 16043680.

Open on PubMed

  • Vitamin D and dark skin Tier A moderate

    A 208 healthy Black postmenopausal American women aged 50-75 (n=208)

    Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, 3 years, vitamin D3 at two doses with calcium given to all.

    No significant difference in bone mineral density between vitamin D and placebo groups at any point; no relationship between blood level reached and bone loss. No significant adverse events were attributed to vitamin D.

    As recorded in this card: Aloia JF, Talwar SA, Pollack S, Yeh J (2005), Archives of Internal Medicine, [primary]
