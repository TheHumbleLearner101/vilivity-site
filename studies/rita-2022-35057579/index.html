Study: Rita 2022, Nutrients

Adansonia digitata L. (Baobab Fruit) Effect on Postprandial Glycemia in Healthy Adults: A Randomized Controlled Trial

Citation

Rita K, Bernardo MA, Silva ML, Brito J et al (2022). Nutrients. PubMed id 35057579.

Open on PubMed

Each card gives its own wording for who was tested, how and what was found.

  • Baobab fruit: blood sugar, iron and vitamin C Tier B thin

    B 31 healthy adults in Portugal (n=31)

    Randomised controlled trial: oral glucose tolerance test with or without 250 mL baobab aqueous extract.

    The rise in blood glucose (incremental area under the curve) and the peak glucose were significantly lower in the baobab group than in the control group after a glucose load. The extract had high polyphenol content.

    As recorded in this card: Rita K, Bernardo MA, Silva ML, Brito J et al (2022), Nutrients, [primary]

    Note: One sitting, healthy adults, a lab-made extract; authors report no conflicts of interest.
