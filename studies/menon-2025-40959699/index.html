  • Lion's mane and reishi mushroom supplements Tier B thin

    B Humans and laboratory models: five randomised trials, three pilot trials, one cohort study and a case report on lion's mane, plus 15 laboratory studies (n=5 randomised trials and 3 pilot trials)

    Systematic review of lion's mane as a supplement (benefits and side effects).

    The authors report cognitive benefits in the trials, including a combined Mini-Mental State Examination gain of about 1 point. Side effects are commonly unreported; those that were reported include stomach discomfort, headache and allergic reactions.

    As recorded in this card: Menon A, Jalal A, Arshad Z, Nawaz FA et al (2025), Frontiers in Nutrition, [review]

    Note: A very small evidence base, mostly in people with cognitive problems. Ethnicity not described in the abstract.
