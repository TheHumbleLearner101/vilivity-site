Study: Dorgham 2020, Journal of the European Academy of Dermatology and Venereology

Lasers for reduction of unwanted hair in skin of colour: a systematic review and meta-analysis

Citation

Dorgham NA, Dorgham DA (2020). Journal of the European Academy of Dermatology and Venereology. PubMed id 31587390.

  • Laser hair removal on dark skin Tier B thin

    B Participants with Fitzpatrick skin types III-VI in 12 comparative trials (9 randomised) (n=12 trials)

    Systematic review and meta-analysis of lasers and IPL for hair reduction.

    Alexandrite laser was superior to IPL for hair count; Nd:YAG and diode were not significantly different from IPL for hair count. Nd:YAG had fewer dark marks than IPL (OR 0.26). Diode and alexandrite had comparable safety to IPL, with more pain.

    As recorded in this card: Dorgham NA, Dorgham DA (2020), Journal of the European Academy of Dermatology and Venereology, [review]

    Note: III-VI covers medium to dark skin; Black participants are not separated.
