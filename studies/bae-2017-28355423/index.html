Study: Bae 2017, JAMA Dermatology

Phototherapy for Vitiligo: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis

Citation

Bae JM, Jung HM, Hong BY, Lee JH, Choi WJ, Lee JH, Kim GM (2017). JAMA Dermatology. PubMed id 28355423.

Open on PubMed

  • Vitiligo, spread, starting age and treatment options Tier B thin

    B 1,428 people in 35 prospective studies of generalised vitiligo (29 studies of narrowband UVB, 9 of PUVA); ancestry not given in the abstract (n=1428)

    Systematic review and meta-analysis of single-arm response rates.

    With narrowband UVB, at least mild repigmentation (25% or more) was seen in 74.2% of 232 patients at 6 months and 75.0% of 512 at 12 months; marked repigmentation (75% or more) in 19.2% of 266 at 6 months and 35.7% of 540 at 12 months. After 6 months or more, marked response on the face and neck was 44.2%, on the trunk 26.1%, on the limbs 17.3%, and none on the hands and feet. The authors recommend long-duration treatment.

    As recorded in this card: Bae JM, Jung HM, Hong BY, Lee JH, Choi WJ, Lee JH, Kim GM (2017), JAMA Dermatology, [review]

    Note: Single-arm studies, no untreated comparison; the abstract does not give participants' ancestry or skin types; funding not checked.
