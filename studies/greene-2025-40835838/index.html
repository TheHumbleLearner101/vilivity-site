Study: Greene 2025, Nature Communications

Multi-ancestry meta-analysis of keloids uncovers novel susceptibility loci in diverse populations

Citation

Greene CA, Hampton G, Jaworski J, Shuey MM et al (2025). Nature Communications. PubMed id 40835838.

  • Keloids and piercings Tier B moderate

    B 7,837 keloid cases and 1,593,009 controls across continental ancestry groups (n=7837)

    Multi-ancestry genome-wide association meta-analysis.

    26 genetic loci were found, 12 replicated. Estimated heritability was 6% in European, 21% in East Asian and 34% in African ancestry analyses. The authors state keloids are more likely in darker-skinned people of African and Asian descent than in Europeans.
