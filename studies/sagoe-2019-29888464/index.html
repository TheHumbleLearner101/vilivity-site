Study: Sagoe 2019, International Journal of Dermatology

The global prevalence and correlates of skin bleaching: a meta-analysis and meta-regression analysis

Citation

Sagoe D et al (2019). International Journal of Dermatology. PubMed id 29888464.

Open on PubMed

  • Skin lightening and bleaching creams Tier B moderate

    B 68 studies, 67,665 people worldwide (n=67665)

    Meta-analysis and meta-regression of the prevalence of skin bleaching.

    Pooled lifetime prevalence of skin bleaching was 27.7%, and 27.1% in Africa, with very high heterogeneity, so the figure is a rough average of unlike samples. The highest prevalence in a subgroup analysis was 51.8%, associated with topical corticosteroid use (the abstract does not say whether this is the share of users who applied steroids); the pooled sample is global, not Black-specific.

    As recorded in this card: Sagoe D et al (2019), International Journal of Dermatology, [review]
