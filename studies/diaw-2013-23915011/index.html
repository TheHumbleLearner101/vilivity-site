Study: Diaw 2013, Chronobiology International

Intraday blood rheological changes induced by Ramadan fasting in sickle cell trait carriers

Citation

Diaw M, Connes P, Samb A, Sow AK et al (2013). Chronobiology International. PubMed id 23915011.

Open on PubMed

Cards that cite this study (1)

Each card gives its own wording for who was tested, how and what was found.

  • Fasting in Ramadan with diabetes or sickle cell Tier B thin

    A 20 African male students in Senegal: 10 with sickle cell trait and 10 with normal haemoglobin (n=20)

    Measurements on day 14 of Ramadan and 6 weeks after.

    Blood viscosity rose markedly in the evening in the people with sickle cell trait during Ramadan, and not in the normal-haemoglobin group, which the authors say indicates higher risk of impaired blood flow in small vessels. Plasma glucose was lower in both groups during Ramadan. They call for specific medical recommendations for trait carriers who fast.

    As recorded in this card: Diaw M, Connes P, Samb A, Sow AK et al (2013), Chronobiology International, [primary]

    Note: Ten people with trait; a measurement of blood thickness, not of illness.
