Study: Cheah 2021, PLoS One

Effect of Ashwagandha (Withania somnifera) extract on sleep: A systematic review and meta-analysis

Citation

Cheah KL, Norhayati MN, Husniati Yaacob L, Abdul Rahman R (2021). PLoS One. PubMed id 34559859.

  • Ashwagandha: stress, sleep, thyroid and liver cautions Tier B moderate

    B Adults aged 18 and over in five randomised trials (insomnia subgroup included) (n=400)

    Systematic review and meta-analysis of randomised trials of ashwagandha extract against placebo for sleep.

    Ashwagandha extract gave a small but significant improvement in overall sleep (standardised mean difference -0.59). Effects were larger in people diagnosed with insomnia, at 600 mg a day or more, and over eight weeks or more.

    As recorded in this card: Cheah KL, Norhayati MN, Husniati Yaacob L, Abdul Rahman R (2021), PLoS One, [review]

    Note: Five trials only. Ethnicity not reported in the abstract.
