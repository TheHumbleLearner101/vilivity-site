Disparities in dermatology AI performance on a diverse, curated clinical image set

  • Finding a dermatologist who knows dark skin Tier B thin

    B 656 skin images (207 Fitzpatrick V-VI) in the Diverse Dermatology Images dataset; dermatologists and AI (n=656)

    Test of AI models and dermatologists on skin images by skin tone.

    AI models did worse on Fitzpatrick V-VI than I-II (for example ROC-AUC 0.55 against 0.64 for ModelDerm, with overlapping 95% intervals of 0.46 to 0.64 and 0.55 to 0.70; DeepDerm 0.50 against 0.61, HAM10000 0.57 against 0.72; figures from the full text, PMC9374341, opened 12 Nov 2026); dermatologists were also worse on dark skin; fine-tuning on the dataset closed the AI gap.

    As recorded in this card: Daneshjou R, Vodrahalli K, Novoa RA et al (2022), Science Advances, [primary]
