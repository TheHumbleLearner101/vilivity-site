Epidemiology, Behavioral Determinants, Symptom Burden, and Severity of Traction Alopecia Among Ghanaian Women: A Cross-Sectional Study

Each card gives its own wording for who was tested, how and what was found.

  • Braids, twists and extensions: tension, how long, itch Tier B moderate

    B 197 Ghanaian women, mean age 23, hairline status scored on the Marginal Traction Alopecia Score (n=197)

    Cross-sectional survey, logistic regression.

    40.6% had hairline recession consistent with traction alopecia. More than two high-frequency heat-styling sessions a month was associated with higher odds (adjusted odds ratio 5.65, 95% CI 1.35-23.62). Keeping one hairstyle for over 6 weeks showed higher estimated odds that were not significant (1.81, 95% CI 0.59-5.60).

    As recorded in this card: Ayugane et al (2026), Health Science Reports, [primary]

    Note: Small, self-reported, wide confidence intervals.
