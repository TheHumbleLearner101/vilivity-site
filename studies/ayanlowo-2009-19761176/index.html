Study: Ayanlowo 2009, West African Journal of Medicine

Characteristics of vitiligo in Lagos, Nigeria

Citation

Ayanlowo O, Olumide YM, Akinkugbe A (2009). West African Journal of Medicine. PubMed id 19761176.

Open on PubMed

Each card gives its own wording for who was tested, how and what was found.

  • Vitiligo, spread, starting age and treatment options Tier B thin

    B 186 patients with vitiligo (2.8% of dermatology clinic attenders) at one hospital in Lagos, Nigeria, 2003 to 2006 (n=186)

    Consecutive clinic case series.

    Vitiligo affected all ages, from 1 to 86 years. The face and limbs were most often affected. Fifteen patients (0.81% as printed in the abstract) had features suggesting an associated autoimmune disease such as type 1 diabetes.

    As recorded in this card: Ayanlowo O, Olumide YM, Akinkugbe A (2009), West African Journal of Medicine, [primary]

    Note: Describes who attends one clinic, so it cannot give a population age of onset. The abstract's percentage for the 15 patients does not match 15 of 186 and is quoted as printed.
