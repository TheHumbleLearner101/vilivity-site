Study: Palta 2012, Journal of Urban Health

Evaluation of a mindfulness-based intervention program to decrease blood pressure in low-income African-American older adults

Citation

Palta P, Page G, Piferi RL, Gill JM et al (2012). Journal of Urban Health. PubMed id 22302233.

Open on PubMed

Cards that cite this study (1)

Each card gives its own wording for who was tested, how and what was found.

  • Meditation Tier A moderate

    A 20 low-income African American older adults in a residence (pilot) (n=20)

    Randomised mindfulness-based intervention against social support control, 8 weeks.

    Feasible and acceptable with high attendance. Average systolic pressure fell in both groups; the intervention group had a 21.92 mmHg lower systolic pressure than the social support group afterwards (p=0.020) and a 16.70 mmHg lower diastolic (p=0.003). The authors call the results preliminary and call for larger trials.

    As recorded in this card: Palta P, Page G, Piferi RL, Gill JM et al (2012), Journal of Urban Health, [primary]

    Note: Pilot, 20 people; the primary aim was feasibility. The very large differences are likely to be unreliable at this size; the authors call them preliminary.
