  • Shaving and razor bumps (pseudofolliculitis barbae) Tier A thin

    B Review 1987-2023 (n=not stated)

    Literature review of treatments.

    Treatment needs a multifaceted approach: preventive measures, antibiotics, corticosteroids, keratolytics, depilatories or laser. Topical therapies are the mainstay; laser hair removal is a potential long-term option needing more study of efficacy and permanence.
