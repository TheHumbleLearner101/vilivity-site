Classification of High Curl Pattern Hair: A Systematic Review and Clinical Perspective

Citation

Callender V, Burgess C, Harvey VM, Heath C et al (2026). Journal of Cosmetic Dermatology. PubMed id 41928381.

  • Hair porosity and moisture Tier D

    C Hair shape classification systems (n=4 systems)

    Systematic review of hair-type classifications without reference to race or ethnicity.

    Four mainstream classification systems exist (Andre Walker, LOIS, FIA, L'Oreal). The authors state hair type classification is under-used in clinical practice and argue it should be based on shape criteria rather than race.

    As recorded in this card: Callender V, Burgess C, Harvey VM, Heath C et al (2026), Journal of Cosmetic Dermatology, [review]

  • Hair types 4a, 4b, 4c: what typing tells you Tier D

    C Systematic review of curl classification (n=not stated)

    Systematic review.

    Curl classification may have predictive value for scalp disease such as seborrhoeic dermatitis, but further validation is required.

    As recorded in this card: Callender VD, Burgess C et al (2026), Journal of Cosmetic Dermatology, [review]
