Study: Challand 2009, Journal of Alternative and Complementary Medicine

A clinical trial of the traditional medicine Vernonia amygdalina in the treatment of uncomplicated malaria

Citation

Challand S, Willcox M (2009). Journal of Alternative and Complementary Medicine. PubMed id 19922255.

Open on PubMed

  • Bush teas and herbal remedies (cerasee, bitter leaf) with medicines Tier B thin

    B Patients aged 12 and over with uncomplicated malaria, a traditional remedy trial in Africa (n=not stated in the abstract)

    Uncontrolled clinical trial of an infusion of fresh Vernonia amygdalina (bitter leaf).

    An adequate clinical response at day 14 occurred in 67% of cases, but complete parasite clearance occurred in only 32% of those, and 71% of those had recrudescence. No significant side effects or toxicity were seen. The authors say more studies are needed.

    As recorded in this card: Challand S, Willcox M (2009), Journal of Alternative and Complementary Medicine, [primary]

    Note: Uncontrolled, in malaria; it says nothing about blood sugar. Not evidence that bitter leaf works.
