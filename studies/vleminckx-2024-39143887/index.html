Study: Vleminckx 2024, Journal of Cosmetic Dermatology

Influence of collagen peptide supplementation on visible signs of skin and nail health and -aging in an East Asian population: A double blind, randomized, placebo-controlled trial

Citation

Vleminckx S, Virgilio N, Asserin J, Prawitt J, Silva CIF (2024). Journal of Cosmetic Dermatology. PubMed id 39143887.

Open on PubMed

Cards that cite this study (1)

Each card gives its own wording for who was tested, how and what was found.

  • Collagen for hair, nails and joints Tier B thin

    B 85 healthy women aged 43 to 65, East Asian population (n=85)

    Double-blind randomised placebo-controlled trial, 5 g collagen peptides daily for 84 days, plus a standard face cream.

    Skin density and moisture improved over placebo at 84 days. Nail colour and overall beauty perception improved within 28 days in the collagen group; the placebo group showed the same effects only at 84 days. Hair was not measured.

    As recorded in this card: Vleminckx S, Virgilio N, Asserin J, Prawitt J, Silva CIF (2024), Journal of Cosmetic Dermatology, [primary]

    Note: The only nail result found, measured as nail colour, not growth or strength. East Asian women only; skin type not reported in the abstract. Funding not stated in the abstract.
