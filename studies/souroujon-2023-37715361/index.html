Autologous cell transplant as a treatment for stable segmental vitiligo: a systematic review

Citation

Souroujon AA, Guttman I, Levin N (2023). International Journal of Dermatology. PubMed id 37715361.

  • Vitiligo, spread, starting age and treatment options Tier B thin

    B Six studies of autologous (own-skin) cell transplants for stable segmental vitiligo; ancestry not given in the abstract (n=not stated)

    Systematic review.

    Cultured and non-cultured transplants each pigmented 80% or more of the treated area in the studies reviewed, with cultured cells doing better. The authors note the small amount of literature, and the cost and logistics that limit access.

    As recorded in this card: Souroujon AA, Guttman I, Levin N (2023), International Journal of Dermatology, [review]

    Note: Applies to stable segmental vitiligo only, not the common generalised type. These are the cell treatments sometimes described as stem cell treatment; our search found no randomised trial for generalised vitiligo that used the term stem cell and was opened.
