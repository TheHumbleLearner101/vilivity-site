Dietary glycemic index and load in relation to risk of uterine leiomyomata in the Black Women's Health Study

Radin RG, Palmer JR, Rosenberg L, Kumanyika SK et al (2010). American Journal of Clinical Nutrition. PubMed id 20200259.

  • Fibroids, food and supplements Tier B thin

    B 21,861 premenopausal Black women in the Black Women's Health Study, followed 1997-2007 (n=21861)

    Prospective cohort with food-frequency questionnaires.

    During 162,604 person-years, 5,800 fibroid cases were diagnosed. Dietary glycaemic index was weakly associated with fibroid risk overall (highest against lowest quintile 1.09, 95% CI 0.99-1.19; P for trend 0.04), with stronger links in women under 35 for glycaemic load (1.18) and in college-educated women for glycaemic index (1.17).
