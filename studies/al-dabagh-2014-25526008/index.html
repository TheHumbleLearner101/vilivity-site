Study: Al-Dabagh 2014, Dermatology Online Journal

Rosacea in skin of color: not a rare diagnosis

Citation

Al-Dabagh A, Davis SA, McMichael AJ, Feldman SR (2014). Dermatology Online Journal. PubMed id 25526008.

Open on PubMed

  • How skin conditions look on dark skin (redness, rashes, rosacea, sunburn) Tier B thin

    B US national ambulatory care survey 1993-2010 (n=not stated)

    Survey of rosacea diagnosis by race.

    2.0% of patients with a rosacea diagnosis were Black. When Black patients attended with "other diseases of the skin", rosacea was the diagnosis for 2.2% against 8.3% of White patients; the authors suggest underdiagnosis in skin of colour.

    As recorded in this card: Al-Dabagh A, Davis SA, McMichael AJ, Feldman SR (2014), Dermatology Online Journal, [primary]

    Note: Cannot separate lower rates from underdiagnosis.
