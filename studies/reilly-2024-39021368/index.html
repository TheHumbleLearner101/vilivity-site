Study: Reilly 2024, Dermatology Research and Practice

A Clinical Trial Shows Improvement in Skin Collagen, Hydration, Elasticity, Wrinkles, Scalp, and Hair Condition following 12-Week Oral Intake of a Supplement Containing Hydrolysed Collagen

Citation

Reilly DM, Kynaston L, Naseem S, Proudman E, Laceby D (2024). Dermatology Research and Practice. PubMed id 39021368.

Open on PubMed

  • Collagen for hair, nails and joints Tier B thin

    B Adults taking a hydrolysed collagen plus vitamin C supplement, daily or every 48 hours (number not given in the abstract) (n=not recorded)

    Randomised double-blind placebo-controlled trial, 12 weeks.

    At 12 weeks the supplement group had better skin hydration and elasticity. The number of hairs counted rose 27.6% against placebo, which was not statistically significant (p not significant); the rating for healthy hair appearance rose 31.9% (p<0.01).

    As recorded in this card: Reilly DM, Kynaston L, Naseem S, Proudman E, Laceby D (2024), Dermatology Research and Practice, [primary]

    Note: A branded product that also contains vitamin C. Participant number, skin type and funding not in the abstract.
