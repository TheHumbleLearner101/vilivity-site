Bovine Colostrum Applications in Sick and Healthy People: A Systematic Review

Citation

Guberti M, Botti S, Capuzzo MT, Nardozi S et al (2021). Nutrients. PubMed id 34202206.

  • Bovine colostrum supplements for immunity and gut health Tier B thin

    B Healthy and sick people in 28 papers (reviews, randomised trials, observational studies and case series) (n=28)

    Systematic review of bovine colostrum by every route of use.

    Studies were highly varied in people, doses and risk of bias. As a supplement it showed "interesting effects" on upper respiratory illness in sportsmen, immune response and intestinal permeability. Results in children were conflicting, and there is little evidence in older adults.

    As recorded in this card: Guberti M, Botti S, Capuzzo MT, Nardozi S et al (2021), Nutrients, [review]
