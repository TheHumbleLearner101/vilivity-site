Study: Katon 2023, Fertility and Sterility

Racial disparities in uterine fibroids and endometriosis: a systematic review and application of social, structural, and political context

Citation

Katon JG, Plowden TC, Marsh EE (2023). Fertility and Sterility. PubMed id 36682686.

Open on PubMed

Cards that cite this study (2)

Each card gives its own wording for who was tested, how and what was found.

  • Fibroids: symptoms, treatment and pregnancy Tier B moderate

    B US studies of fibroids and endometriosis in Black and White women (n=not stated in the abstract)

    Systematic review of racial disparities in prevalence, with a social and structural context framework.

    Prevalence of fibroids was consistently higher in Black than in White women, with the size of the difference varying by population and case definition. Black women with fibroids or endometriosis experienced worse clinical and surgical outcomes. The authors link the disparities to differing exposures, poor access to highly skilled gynaecologic surgeons, and bias within health care.

    As recorded in this card: Katon JG, Plowden TC, Marsh EE (2023), Fertility and Sterility, [review]

    Note: US data; funding not checked.

  • PCOS, endometriosis and heavy periods Tier B thin

    B US studies of fibroids and endometriosis in Black and White women (n=not stated in the abstract)

    Systematic review of racial disparities in prevalence.

    Prevalence of endometriosis varied considerably with population and case definition but was the same or lower in Black than White women. Data suggest no racial difference in incidence of endometriosis. Black women with fibroids or endometriosis had worse clinical and surgical outcomes. The authors link disparities to exposures, access to skilled surgeons and bias.

    As recorded in this card: Katon JG, Plowden TC, Marsh EE (2023), Fertility and Sterility, [review]

    Note: US data. Funding not checked.
