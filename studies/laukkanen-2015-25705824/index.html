Study: Laukkanen 2015, JAMA Internal Medicine

Association between sauna bathing and fatal cardiovascular and all-cause mortality events

Citation

Laukkanen T, Khan H, Zaccardi F, Laukkanen JA (2015). JAMA Internal Medicine. PubMed id 25705824.

Open on PubMed

  • Sauna and heat bathing: heart health, recovery and risks Tier B thin

    B Middle-aged men (42 to 60) in eastern Finland, Kuopio Ischaemic Heart Disease cohort (n=2315)

    Prospective cohort, median follow-up 20.7 years; sauna frequency and duration at baseline.

    Compared with one session a week, men using a sauna 4 to 7 times a week had about two-thirds lower risk of sudden cardiac death (hazard ratio 0.37, 95% CI 0.18 to 0.75), and lower fatal heart and all-cause mortality. Sessions over 19 minutes were linked to lower risk than sessions under 11 minutes.

    As recorded in this card: Laukkanen T, Khan H, Zaccardi F, Laukkanen JA (2015), JAMA Internal Medicine, [primary]

    Note: Observational, Finnish men, dry sauna habits. It cannot show that sauna caused the lower risk.
