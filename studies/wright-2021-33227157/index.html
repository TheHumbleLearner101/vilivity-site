Study: Wright 2021, Journal of the American Geriatrics Society

Mindfulness in Motion and Dietary Approaches to Stop Hypertension (DASH) in Hypertensive African Americans

Citation

Wright KD, Klatt MD, Adams IR, Nguyen CM et al (2021). Journal of the American Geriatrics Society. PubMed id 33227157.

Open on PubMed

Each card gives its own wording for who was tested, how and what was found.

  • Breathwork and slow breathing Tier B moderate

    B African American older adults with mild cognitive impairment and hypertension (pilot, US community centre) (n=not stated in the abstract)

    Cluster randomised pilot of Mindfulness in Motion plus DASH diet (includes breathing exercises, mindful movement and guided meditation) against attention-only and control groups, 3 months.

    Feasible and culturally acceptable. Systolic blood pressure fell 7.2 mmHg in the intervention group against 0.7 in the attention-only group, but there was no difference from the true control group, and no changes in diet or mindfulness.

    As recorded in this card: Wright KD, Klatt MD, Adams IR, Nguyen CM et al (2021), Journal of the American Geriatrics Society, [primary]

    Note: A mixed package, not breathwork alone, and a feasibility pilot. Counts toward 'a trial in African Americans exists' only.
