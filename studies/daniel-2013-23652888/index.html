Study: Daniel 2013, Journal of Drugs in Dermatology

Shave frequency and regimen variation effects on the management of pseudofolliculitis barbae

Citation

Daniel A, Gustafson CJ, Zupkosky PJ, Candido A et al (2013). Journal of Drugs in Dermatology. PubMed id 23652888.

Open on PubMed

  • Shaving and razor bumps (pseudofolliculitis barbae) Tier A thin

    A 90 African American men with pseudofolliculitis barbae (PFB) (n=90)

    Randomised to three groups for 12 weeks: shaving 2-3 times a week with standard products, daily with standard products, or daily with advanced products.

    Papule and pustule counts did not differ between groups at 12 weeks, and investigator-graded severity did not differ (P=.43). Ingrown hairs were significantly fewer in the 2-3 times a week group than in either daily group (P=.005 against daily standard, P=.04 against daily advanced). Participants in the daily advanced products group reported more perceived improvement (P=.007) and less itching (P=.002) than the 2-3 times a week group.

    As recorded in this card: Daniel A, Gustafson CJ, Zupkosky PJ, Candido A et al (2013), Journal of Drugs in Dermatology, [primary]
