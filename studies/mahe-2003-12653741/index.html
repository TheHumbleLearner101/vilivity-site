Study: Mahe 2003, British Journal of Dermatology

Skin diseases associated with the cosmetic use of bleaching products in women from Dakar, Senegal

Citation

Mahe A, Ly F, Aymard G, Dangou JM (2003). British Journal of Dermatology. PubMed id 12653741.

Open on PubMed

Cards that cite this study (1)

Each card gives its own wording for who was tested, how and what was found.

  • Skin lightening and bleaching creams Tier B moderate

    B 425 women who used bleaching products, plus 368 women screened, in Dakar, Senegal (n=425)

    Cross-sectional clinic study with examination and analysis of product samples.

    52.7% of 368 women questioned were current users. Users reported hydroquinone (89%), glucocorticoids (70%), mercury iodide (10%) and caustic agents (17%). Hydroquinone in analysed samples was 4% to 8.7%. The main skin problems in users included dermatophyte infections (105 women) and scabies (69).
