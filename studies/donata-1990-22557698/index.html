Study: Donata 1990, Ancient Science of Life

Clinical trial of certain ayurvedic medicines indicated in vitiligo

Citation

Donata, Kesavan M, Austin, et al (1990). Ancient Science of Life. PubMed id 22557698.

Open on PubMed

Cards that cite this study (1)

  • Vitiligo, spread, starting age and treatment options Tier B thin

    B 10 patients with vitiligo given an ayurvedic preparation by mouth and a herbal paste on the skin (n=10)

    Small clinical trial (comparison group not described in the abstract).

    Four of 10 had relief within six months, three had relief with an adverse skin reaction, and the rest did not respond.

    As recorded in this card: Donata, Kesavan M, Austin, et al (1990), Ancient Science of Life, [primary]

    Note: Very small and old; no control group described in the abstract, so it cannot show the preparation works. The paste included Psoralea corylifolia.
