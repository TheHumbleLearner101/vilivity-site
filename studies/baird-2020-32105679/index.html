Study: Baird 2020, American Journal of Obstetrics and Gynecology

Uterine fibroid incidence and growth in an ultrasound-based, prospective study of young African Americans

Citation

Baird DD, Patchel SA, Saldana TM, Umbach DM et al (2020). American Journal of Obstetrics and Gynecology. PubMed id 32105679.

Open on PubMed

  • Iron, heavy periods and fibroids Tier B moderate

    B 1,693 African American women aged 23-35 with no prior fibroid diagnosis (n=1693)

    Prospective, community-based, ultrasound at enrolment and about 18 months.

    Among 1,123 fibroid-free women with follow-up, incidence was 9.4% over about 18 months, rising from 6% at ages 23-25 to 13% at 32-35. Women with existing fibroids were more than twice as likely to develop new ones (relative risk 2.3). Average fibroid growth was 89% per 18 months.
