Establishing Reference Intervals for White Blood Cell and Absolute Neutrophil Counts in Duffy Null Individuals

Barouqa M, Ashfaq MA, Jiang KJ et al (2026). Hematology Reports. PubMed id 42345734.

  • Low haemoglobin, low or high white cells: causes, thresholds and pregnancy Tier B thin

    A 122 healthy adults with confirmed Duffy-null [Fy(a-b-)] phenotype, drawn from 1,695 adults with a blood count and red cell phenotyping at one institution (2020 to 2026) (n=122)

    Retrospective reference-interval study (CLSI method).

    Median neutrophil count was 1.45 against 4.45 x10^3 per microlitre in the institution's reference. The Duffy-null 95% reference interval for neutrophils was 1.01 to 4.34 x10^3 per microlitre, with the lower bound below the standard neutropenia cut-off. The authors support Duffy-null-specific ranges.

    As recorded in this card: Barouqa M, Ashfaq MA, Jiang KJ et al (2026), Hematology Reports, [primary]

    Note: Single institution (University of South Alabama, US). Participants selected by Duffy status.
