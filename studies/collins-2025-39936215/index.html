Study: Collins 2025, Dermatitis

Contact Sensitization to Allergens in Synthetic Hair and Related Hair Practices: A Review

Citation

Collins M, Yu J et al (2025). Dermatitis. PubMed id 39936215.

  • Braids, twists and extensions: tension, how long, itch Tier B moderate

    B Review of documented cases of contact dermatitis from synthetic hair and related practices (n=not stated)

    Review of case reports.

    Synthetic hair extensions are known to cause contact dermatitis through allergens such as acrylates and disperse dyes. Scalp contact dermatitis can show up as itch, thinning or an eczema-like rash and is easily misdiagnosed. Braiding, wigs and the products used to maintain them are discussed.

    As recorded in this card: Collins M, Yu J et al (2025), Dermatitis, [review]

    Note: Case reports, not a prevalence study; the abstract does not name which glues or products.
