Study: Lebwohl 2012, Alimentary Pharmacology and Therapeutics

Risk of colorectal adenomas and advanced neoplasia in Hispanic, black and white patients undergoing screening colonoscopy

Citation

Lebwohl B, Capiak K, Neugut AI (2012). Alimentary Pharmacology and Therapeutics. PubMed id 22540887.

Open on PubMed

  • Colonoscopy: when to start and how often Tier B moderate

    B 5,075 adults aged 50 and over having a first screening colonoscopy at one tertiary centre in the US (591 Black, 942 Hispanic, 3,542 white) (n=5075)

    Cross-sectional cohort.

    At least one adenoma was found in 26% of Black patients, 22% of Hispanic and 19% of white patients. Black patients had a higher adenoma rate (risk ratio 1.76) and more advanced adenomas (odds ratio 1.91) than white patients.

    As recorded in this card: Lebwohl B, Capiak K, Neugut AI (2012), Alimentary Pharmacology and Therapeutics, [primary]

    Note: Single centre; ages 50 and over, so it says nothing about 40 to 49.
