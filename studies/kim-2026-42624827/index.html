  • Collagen for hair, nails and joints Tier B thin

    B Korean adults aged 19 to 60 with mild to moderate hair damage and no alopecia (number not given in the abstract) (n=not recorded)

    Randomised double-blind placebo-controlled trial, 3 g/day low-molecular-weight collagen peptide for 24 weeks.

    Compared with placebo, hair gloss, elasticity (tensile strength) and diameter improved, and scalp elasticity rose. No treatment-related adverse events.
