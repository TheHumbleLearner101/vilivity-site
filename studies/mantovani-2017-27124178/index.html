Study: Mantovani 2017, Health Expectations

Exploring the relationship between stigma and help-seeking for mental illness in African-descended faith communities in the UK

Citation

Mantovani N, Pizzolati M, Edge D (2017). Health Expectations. PubMed id 27124178.

Open on PubMed

  • Stigma, talking to family and starting therapy Tier B thin

    B 26 men and women in Christian faith communities, south London (n=26)

    Qualitative interviews on stigma and help-seeking for mental illness.

    Help-seeking was shaped by beliefs about the causes of mental illness, silencing driven by stigma, and stigma kept up at community level. People with a diagnosis could face triple jeopardy in stigma. The authors say one-size-fits-all approaches will not meet diverse needs.

    As recorded in this card: Mantovani N, Pizzolati M, Edge D (2017), Health Expectations, [primary]

    Note: Small qualitative study in one setting; shows themes, not how common they are.
