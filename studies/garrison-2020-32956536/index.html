    B People with skeletal muscle cramps in 11 randomised trials (five in pregnancy, five idiopathic, one cirrhosis) (n=735)

    Cochrane systematic review of magnesium supplements for cramp prevention.

    For idiopathic cramps, largely in older adults (mean age about 62 to 69), cramp frequency differences between magnesium and placebo were small and statistically non-significant (moderate-certainty evidence). The pregnancy and cirrhosis trials were at high risk of bias.

    As recorded in this card: Garrison SR, Korownyk CS, Kolber MR, Allan GM et al (2020), Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, [review]

    Note: Ethnicity not reported in the abstract.
