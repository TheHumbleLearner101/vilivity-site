Study: Yeary 2025, Journal of the Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics

An Intermittent Fasting Intervention for Black Adults Can Be Feasibly Implemented in Black Churches: A Cluster Randomized Controlled Pilot Study

Citation

Yeary KHK, Johnson D, Harris N, Yu H et al (2025). Journal of the Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics. PubMed id 38986868.

Open on PubMed

Each card gives its own wording for who was tested, how and what was found.

  • Intermittent fasting for weight and health Tier B moderate

    A Black adults with BMI of 25 or more, from five Black churches in Western New York State (n=42)

    Cluster randomised pilot: faith-based intermittent fasting (three churches) against continuous calorie reduction (two churches), 6 months, led by community health workers.

    Weight fell in both arms (fasting -3.5 kg, calorie reduction -2.9 kg). The fasting group ate about 414 kcal a day less than the calorie-reduction group, but ate less fruit, vegetables and fibre. Satisfaction was high. The authors conclude it is feasible and larger studies are needed.

    As recorded in this card: Yeary KHK, Johnson D, Harris N, Yu H et al (2025), Journal of the Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics, [primary]

    Note: The one randomised trial in Black adults I found. A pilot built to test feasibility, with no proof that fasting beats calorie reduction.
