Study: Wu 2025, Cancer Epidemiology, Biomarkers and Prevention

Racial Differences in Colorectal Adenomas at Screening Colonoscopy in the United States

Citation

Wu Y, de Groen PC, Natafgi N et al (2025). Cancer Epidemiology, Biomarkers and Prevention. PubMed id 40063509.

Open on PubMed

  • Colonoscopy: when to start and how often Tier B moderate

    B 28,100 average-risk adults aged 40 to 89 at first screening colonoscopy in South Carolina, 58.4% Black (n=28100)

    Cross-sectional observational study.

    Black patients had lower age- and sex-adjusted odds of an adenoma (odds ratio 0.88) than white patients, and similar odds of advanced adenoma or three or more non-advanced adenomas. The authors say polyp profiles were similar and that other factors must explain the higher bowel cancer rates in Black Americans.

    As recorded in this card: Wu Y, de Groen PC, Natafgi N et al (2025), Cancer Epidemiology, Biomarkers and Prevention, [primary]

    Note: Racially balanced sample, one US state; includes ages 40 to 49.
