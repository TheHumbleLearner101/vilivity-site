Impact of time-restricted eating on reproductive hormones and androgen markers in adult females: A systematic review

Citation

Jirapak A, Adams E, Black GM, Devlin BL (2026). Nutrition and Health. PubMed id 42429418.

  • Intermittent fasting for women, older adults, teenagers and people with heart or thyroid conditions Tier B thin

    B 6 studies of time-restricted eating in adult females (randomised trials in women with obesity; non-randomised studies in women with PCOS) (n=None)

    Systematic review of reproductive hormones and androgen markers.

    Randomised trials in women with obesity found no significant change in testosterone, oestrogen, progesterone, LH, cortisol, DHEA or SHBG. Non-randomised PCOS studies reported lower testosterone and LH, possibly because of weight loss. The authors call the evidence inconclusive.
