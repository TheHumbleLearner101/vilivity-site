  • Colonoscopy: when to start and how often Tier B moderate

    B Patients in Europe after complete polyp removal at a high-quality colonoscopy (n=not applicable)

    Guideline update from the European Society of Gastrointestinal Endoscopy.

    ESGE says 1 to 4 small adenomas (under 10 mm, low grade) or a small serrated polyp need no surveillance and return to screening (or a repeat colonoscopy at 10 years if there is no organised screening); an adenoma of 10 mm or more, high-grade dysplasia, 5 or more adenomas or a large serrated polyp needs a colonoscopy at 3 years.

    As recorded in this card: Hassan C, Antonelli G, Dumonceau JM et al (2020), Endoscopy, [consensus]

    Note: Abstract read; recommendations are strong with moderate-quality evidence.
