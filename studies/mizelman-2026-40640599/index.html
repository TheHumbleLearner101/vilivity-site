Bovine colostrum supplementation in rugby

  • Bovine colostrum supplements for immunity and gut health Tier B thin

    B Club-level rugby players (26 men, 3 women), 8 weeks (n=29)

    Randomised, double-blind trial of bovine colostrum 38 g a day against soy protein.

    Vertical jump height rose by about 1 cm with colostrum and fell by 0.5 cm with soy (p = 0.0023). There were no differences in upper or lower body strength, aerobic power, body composition, saliva immune markers or reported illness.

    As recorded in this card: Mizelman E, Shaw KA, Kaviani M, Brenna E et al (2026), European Journal of Applied Physiology, [primary]

    Note: A small trial in athletes. Ethnicity not reported in the abstract.
