Study: Nwachukwu 2015, Nigerian Journal of Clinical Practice

Effect of Hibiscus sabdariffaon blood pressure and electrolyte profile of mild to moderate hypertensive Nigerians: A comparative study with hydrochlorothiazide

Citation

Nwachukwu DC, Aneke E, Nwachukwu NZ, Obika LF et al (2015). Nigerian Journal of Clinical Practice. PubMed id 26289514.

Open on PubMed

Cards that cite this study (1)

Each card gives its own wording for who was tested, how and what was found.

  • Hibiscus (sorrel, zobo, bissap) and blood pressure Tier A thin

    A 80 newly diagnosed, untreated Nigerian adults with mild to moderate hypertension, Enugu (n=80)

    Randomised, three groups for 4 weeks: placebo, hydrochlorothiazide, or hibiscus.

    Both hibiscus and the drug lowered blood pressure compared with placebo. The authors reported hibiscus as more effective than the drug and without electrolyte imbalance, and the fall persisted after withdrawal. No side effects were reported.

    As recorded in this card: Nwachukwu DC, Aneke E, Nwachukwu NZ, Obika LF et al (2015), Nigerian Journal of Clinical Practice, [primary]

    Note: Single small trial. Placebo, dose and blinding details were not in the abstract. The 'more effective than the drug' conclusion is the authors' own.
