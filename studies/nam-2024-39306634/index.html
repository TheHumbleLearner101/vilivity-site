Study: Nam 2024, Journal of Behavioral Medicine

Mindfulness-based therapy for insomnia in Black women: a pilot randomized controlled trial

Citation

Nam S, Jeon S, Ordway M, Mazure C et al (2024). Journal of Behavioral Medicine. PubMed id 39306634.

  • Coping with anxiety day to day Tier B thin

    A 30 Black women with insomnia, US (n=30)

    Pilot randomised trial of online mindfulness-based therapy for insomnia against lifestyle health education, 10 weeks.

    Insomnia improved in both groups; anxiety and depression symptoms improved significantly only in the mindfulness group. Nearly all attended 6 to 8 of 8 sessions. The authors call it feasible and acceptable.

    As recorded in this card: Nam S, Jeon S, Ordway M, Mazure C et al (2024), Journal of Behavioral Medicine, [primary]

    Note: A pilot of 30 women with insomnia, not a trial of anxiety.
