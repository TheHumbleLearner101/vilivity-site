Study: Garcia-Coronado 2019, International Orthopaedics

Effect of collagen supplementation on osteoarthritis symptoms: a meta-analysis of randomized placebo-controlled trials

Citation

Garcia-Coronado JM, Martinez-Olvera L, Elizondo-Omana RE, Acosta-Olivo CA et al (2019). International Orthopaedics. PubMed id 30368550.

  • Collagen for hair, nails and joints Tier B thin

    B Adults with osteoarthritis in randomised placebo-controlled trials of oral collagen (n=not recorded)

    Meta-analysis (WOMAC and VAS scores).

    Total WOMAC score and VAS pain score fell with collagen, and stiffness improved. The WOMAC pain and function sub-scores did not differ significantly from placebo.

    As recorded in this card: Garcia-Coronado JM, Martinez-Olvera L, Elizondo-Omana RE, Acosta-Olivo CA et al (2019), International Orthopaedics, [review]

    Note: Number of participants and ethnicity not in the abstract. The mixed sub-scores are why this card stays thin.
