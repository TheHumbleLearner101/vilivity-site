Study: Forsyth 2024, Science of the Total Environment

Evidence of turmeric adulteration with lead chromate across South Asia

Citation

Forsyth JE, Mistree D, Nash E, Angrish M et al (2024). Science of the Total Environment. PubMed id 39053552.

Open on PubMed

Each card gives its own wording for who was tested, how and what was found.

  • Turmeric and curcumin supplements Tier B moderate

    C Turmeric root and powder samples from bazaars in 23 cities in India, Pakistan, Sri Lanka and Nepal (2020 to 2021) (n=356)

    Laboratory survey of lead and chromium in 356 samples.

    Fourteen per cent (51 samples) had lead above 2 micrograms per gram, and samples at 18 or more had lead-to-chromium ratios pointing to lead chromate added as a colouring. Lead levels exceeded 1,000 micrograms per gram in Patna (India), Karachi and Peshawar (Pakistan).

    As recorded in this card: Forsyth JE, Mistree D, Nash E, Angrish M et al (2024), Science of the Total Environment, [primary]

    Note: A product survey in South Asia, not a study of people. It matters because the spice is traded worldwide. It is a warning about loose spice from uncertain sources.
