Screening for Colorectal Cancer: US Preventive Services Task Force Recommendation Statement

Citation

US Preventive Services Task Force, Davidson KW, Barry MJ, Mangione CM et al (2021). JAMA. PubMed id 34003218.

  • Bowel (colon) cancer screening Tier B moderate

    B Asymptomatic adults aged 45 or over at average risk, US; the review also examined whether findings varied by age, sex or race/ethnicity (n=not stated in the abstract)

    US Preventive Services Task Force recommendation statement based on a commissioned systematic review and modelling.

    High certainty that screening aged 50 to 75 has substantial net benefit; moderate certainty that screening at 45 to 49 has moderate net benefit. About 10.5% of new US colorectal cancers are in people under 50, and incidence in 40 to 49 year olds rose by almost 15% between 2000-2002 and 2014-2016. The abstract does not report results by race.

    As recorded in this card: US Preventive Services Task Force, Davidson KW, Barry MJ, Mangione CM et al (2021), JAMA, [consensus]

    Note: General US population, not Black-specific; the abstract states the review looked at race/ethnicity but gives no result.
