    B 310 children aged 0 to 24 months, six trials (n=310)

    Cochrane review of treatments for infantile seborrhoeic dermatitis (cradle cap).

    Six trials compared oral biotin against placebo, proprietary products against placebo or control shampoo, and topical steroids against other products; the trials were short (10 to 42 days) and the results were based on very low-quality evidence.

    As recorded in this card: Victoire A, Magin P et al (2019), Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, [review]

    Note: Trials not in Black children specifically.
