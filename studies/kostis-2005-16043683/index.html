Study: Kostis 2005, Archives of Internal Medicine

Incidence and characteristics of angioedema associated with enalapril

Citation

Kostis JB, Kim HJ, Rusnak J, Casale T et al (2005). Archives of Internal Medicine. PubMed id 16043683.

  • How some medicines work differently by gene Tier B moderate

    B 12,557 people with hypertension in a randomised, double-blind trial of enalapril 5 to 40 mg daily (n=12557)

    Prospective ascertainment and expert adjudication of angioedema within a randomised controlled trial.

    Angioedema occurred in 86 people (0.68%). Black race (odds ratio 2.88, 95% CI 1.72 to 4.82), history of drug rash (3.78), age over 65 (1.60) and seasonal allergies (1.79) were independent risk factors. Incidence was 3.6 per 1,000 patients per month soon after starting and fell to 0.4. Fatal angioedema or angioedema needing airway protection did not occur.

    As recorded in this card: Kostis JB, Kim HJ, Rusnak J, Casale T et al (2005), Archives of Internal Medicine, [primary]

    Note: A trial population of mixed ancestry, with race as a stand-in for unmeasured genes; abstract read in full.
