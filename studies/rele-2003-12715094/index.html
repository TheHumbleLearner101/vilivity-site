Study: Rele 2003, Journal of Cosmetic Science

Effect of mineral oil, sunflower oil, and coconut oil on prevention of hair damage

Citation

Rele AS, Mohile RB (2003). Journal of Cosmetic Science. PubMed id 12715094.

Open on PubMed

Cards that cite this study (1)

Each card gives its own wording for who was tested, how and what was found.

  • Scalp oiling and greasing Tier D

    C Hair fibres of several types (an industry combing study) (n=not stated)

    Laboratory and in-vivo combing damage tests of coconut, mineral and sunflower oils.

    Coconut oil was the only one of the three oils found to reduce protein loss in undamaged and damaged hair when used before or after washing. Sunflower and mineral oils did not reduce protein loss.

    As recorded in this card: Rele AS, Mohile RB (2003), Journal of Cosmetic Science, [primary]

    Note: Described in the 12 Nov research file (agent4) as an industry study from an Indian hair oil company; the abstract does not say, funding not checked here. Tests the hair fibre, not the scalp, and not afro-textured hair specifically.
