Safety of Ashwagandha Root Extract: A Randomized, Placebo-Controlled, study in Healthy Volunteers

Citation

Verma N, Gupta SK, Tiwari S, Mishra AK (2021). Complementary Therapies in Medicine. PubMed id 33338583.

  • Ashwagandha: cortisol, mood, skin, hair, heart, kidneys and liver Tier B thin

    B 80 healthy adults (40 men, 40 women), Lucknow, India (n=80)

    Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled safety study, 300 mg root extract twice a day for 8 weeks.

    No untoward effects in any treated volunteer. Blood counts, biochemistry including liver tests, vital signs including blood pressure, and thyroid hormones showed no significant abnormal change.

    As recorded in this card: Verma N, Gupta SK, Tiwari S, Mishra AK (2021), Complementary Therapies in Medicine, [primary]

    Note: Healthy people, 8 weeks. Ethnicity not stated.
