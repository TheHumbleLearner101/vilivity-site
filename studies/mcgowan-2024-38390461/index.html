Study: McGowan 2024, Open Forum Infectious Diseases

Investigating and Summarizing Information Resources Related to the Clinical Presentation and Diagnosis of Cutaneous Manifestations of Infectious Diseases in Patients With Skin of Color

Citation

McGowan D, Kermani A, Sheagren J, et al (2024). Open Forum Infectious Diseases. PubMed id 38390461.

  • Rashes on dark skin: what to look for Tier D

    B Published literature on skin of colour (the review does not give study numbers in its abstract) (n=not stated)

    Scoping review of what is written about how infections look and are diagnosed on skin of colour: HIV, shingles, impetigo, scarlet fever, Lyme disease, toxic shock syndrome, scabies, rickettsioses and fungal infections.

    The review found literature gaps, including a lack of visual examples and of precise descriptions of common infectious rashes in patients with skin of colour. The authors say the lack of good-quality resources may contribute to barriers to care.

    As recorded in this card: McGowan D, Kermani A, Sheagren J, et al (2024), Open Forum Infectious Diseases, [review]

    Note: A finding about the evidence, not about how a rash looks; "skin of colour" papers are mostly Asian, Latin American and Middle Eastern populations and are not evidence about Black skin; funding not checked.
