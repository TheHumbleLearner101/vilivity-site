    Systematic review and meta-analysis of melatonin and sleep quality on the Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index.

    Melatonin improved Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index scores by 1.24 points (95% CI 0.71 to 1.77), with large differences between studies (I2 80.7 per cent). Gains were larger in people with respiratory diseases and metabolic disorders than in people with sleep disorders (0.67 points).
