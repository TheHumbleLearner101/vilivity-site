Bridging diagnostic safety and mental health: a systematic review highlighting inequities in autism spectrum disorder diagnosis

Citation

Srivarathan A, Bradford A, Shearkhani S, Heimlich L et al (2026). BMJ Quality & Safety. PubMed id 40854799.

Open on PubMed

Each card gives its own wording for who was tested, how and what was found.

  • Autism and ADHD: being missed, delayed or misdiagnosed Tier B moderate

    B 20 US studies of inequities in diagnosing mental disorders; 17 were about autism (n=20 studies)

    Systematic review with narrative synthesis, studies 2015-2024, quality appraisal with the Mixed-Methods Appraisal Tool; studies of varying quality.

    Most diagnostic errors were delayed diagnosis. Eleven factors contributed: limited health literacy was the leading cause in recognising symptoms, insurance coverage delayed engagement, provider bias during history-taking was a key cause of delays and misdiagnoses, and culturally inequivalent assessment measures might cause misdiagnosis and delayed diagnosis for Black/African American and Hispanic/Latino people.

    As recorded in this card: Srivarathan A, Bradford A, Shearkhani S, Heimlich L et al (2026), BMJ Quality & Safety, [review]

    Note: US only; studies of varying methodological quality; mostly autism, so it says little about ADHD or bipolar.
