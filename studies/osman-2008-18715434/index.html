Cocoa butter lotion for prevention of striae gravidarum: a double-blind, randomised and placebo-controlled trial

  • Stretch marks and fading scars Tier B moderate

    B 210 first-time pregnant women in Lebanon, 175 completed (n=210)

    Multicentre, double-blind, randomised placebo-controlled trial of cocoa butter lotion until delivery.

    Stretch marks developed in 45.1% with cocoa butter and 48.8% with placebo (P=0.730), with no difference in severity.

    As recorded in this card: Osman H, Usta IM, Rubeiz N et al (2008), BJOG, [primary]

    Note: Not in women of African descent as far as the abstract shows.
