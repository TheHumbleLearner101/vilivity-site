  • Head lice in afro-textured hair and locs Tier B thin

    B 310 schoolchildren aged 1 to 13, Paraná, Brazil (n=310)

    Cross-sectional school study; logistic regression.

    Prevalence of head lice was 49.35%. Longer and thicker hair raised the risk; hair colour, shape and scale type also changed the risk when added to the model.

    As recorded in this card: Valero MA et al (2024), Acta Tropica, [primary]

    Note: Brazil; not specific to afro hair.
