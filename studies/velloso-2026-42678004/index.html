Blood Cell Counts and the Duffy Null Phenotype: Beyond Neutropenia

  • Low haemoglobin, low or high white cells: causes, thresholds and pregnancy Tier B thin

    A 311 healthy adult blood donors at two Brazilian blood banks; 46.6% self-identified as Black or mixed-race; 146 (46.9%) Duffy-null (n=311)

    Prospective reference-interval study with Duffy phenotyping.

    Duffy-null donors had a median neutrophil count of 2,250 against 4,070 per microlitre; the 95% reference range was 1,180 to 4,800 against 2,200 to 7,250. They also had higher red cell counts, haemoglobin, haematocrit and platelet counts. The authors support Duffy-specific intervals.

    As recorded in this card: Velloso EDRP, Dos Santos AO, Dinardo CL et al (2026), International Journal of Laboratory Hematology, [primary]

    Note: Participants grouped by Duffy status. Blood donors are healthier than average.
