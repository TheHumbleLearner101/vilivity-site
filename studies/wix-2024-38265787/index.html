    B 48 Black patients with melanoma at two Dallas centres, 2006-2022 (n=48)

    Case series.

    Of 40 primary cutaneous melanomas, 30 (75%) were on acral skin. 13 patients (27%) developed stage IV disease. Melanoma in Black individuals is about 1 in 100,000 a year.
