  • G6PD deficiency: who carries it and what to avoid Tier B moderate

    B Studies of G6PD deficiency prevalence in the ten African countries most affected by malaria, 2000 to 2023 (n=19)

    Systematic review (PRISMA), 19 full texts.

    Prevalence ranged from under 1% to 23.9%, more in men than in women. Antimalarials such as primaquine can cause severe haemolytic anaemia in G6PD-deficient people; the authors say prescribing should consider G6PD status and that several regions need to review diagnostic and screening protocols.
