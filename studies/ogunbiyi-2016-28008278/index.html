    B Review of acne keloidalis nuchae (folliculitis keloidalis nuchae) (n=not stated)

    Narrative review.

    AKN is a chronic scarring folliculitis seen mostly in men of African descent after puberty and is classed as a primary scarring alopecia. The cause is unknown; suggested inciting factors include androgens, inflammation, infection, trauma, genetics and ingrowing hairs.
