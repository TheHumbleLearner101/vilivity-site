  • Chemical relaxers and hair straighteners Tier B moderate

    B Seven cohort studies and one case-control study in US Black women (n=8 studies)

    Systematic review of hair relaxer use and reproductive and breast outcomes.

    The authors conclude that the weight of the evidence does not support relaxers being a risk factor for gynaecological and breast cancers in US Black women. They note one study found a statistically significant link with fibroids and that none of the studies characterised the chemical constituents.
