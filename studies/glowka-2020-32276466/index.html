Study: Glowka 2020, Nutrients

Immunological Outcomes of Bovine Colostrum Supplementation in Trained and Physically Active People: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

Citation

Glowka N, Durkalec-Michalski K, Wozniewicz M (2020). Nutrients. PubMed id 32276466.

Open on PubMed

  • Bovine colostrum supplements for immunity and gut health Tier B thin

    B Athletes and physically active adults in ten randomised trials of bovine colostrum (n=239)

    Systematic review and meta-analysis of immune outcomes.

    Bovine colostrum had no or only a fairly low effect on serum immunoglobulins (IgA, IgG), lymphocytes, neutrophils and saliva IgA. The authors note earlier work saying it reduces upper respiratory tract infections, and say the mechanism is unclear.

    As recorded in this card: Glowka N, Durkalec-Michalski K, Wozniewicz M (2020), Nutrients, [review]

    Note: Trained and active people only. Ethnicity not reported in the abstract.
