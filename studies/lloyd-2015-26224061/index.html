Study: Lloyd 2015, BMC Medicine

Lifetime risk of being diagnosed with, or dying from, prostate cancer by major ethnic group in England 2008-2010

Citation

Lloyd T, Hounsome L, Mehay A, Mee S et al (2015). BMC Medicine. PubMed id 26224061.

Open on PubMed

  • Prostate cancer risk and PSA testing Tier B moderate

    B Prostate cancer registrations and deaths in England 2008-2010 by ethnic group, from Public Health England (n=not stated)

    Registry analysis of lifetime risk of diagnosis and death using DevCan, with adjustments for incomplete ethnicity data.

    Lifetime risk of diagnosis was about 1 in 8 (13.3%) for White men, 1 in 4 (29.3%, range 23.5-37.2%) for Black men and 1 in 13 (7.9%) for Asian men. Lifetime risk of death was about 1 in 24 (4.2%) for White, 1 in 12 (8.7%) for Black and 1 in 44 (2.3%) for Asian men. The authors say Black men are at twice the risk of being diagnosed with and dying from the disease.

    As recorded in this card: Lloyd T, Hounsome L, Mehay A, Mee S et al (2015), BMC Medicine, [primary]

    Note: Ethnicity and mortality data were incomplete and needed assumptions. Authors are from Prostate Cancer UK and Public Health England.
