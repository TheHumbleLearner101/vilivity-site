Study: Hill 2005, Journal of the American Dietetic Association

Esophageal injury by apple cider vinegar tablets and subsequent evaluation of products

Citation

Hill LL, Woodruff LH, Foote JC, Barreto-Alcoba M (2005). Journal of the American Dietetic Association. PubMed id 15983536.

  • Apple cider vinegar: weight, blood sugar and safety Tier B thin

    C One adult who reported an adverse event, and eight apple cider vinegar tablet products tested (n=8)

    Case report with laboratory testing of products (pH, acid content, microbial growth).

    Apple cider vinegar tablets injured the oesophagus in one reported case. The eight products varied widely in tablet size, acidity, acid content and label claims, and the authors doubted whether apple cider vinegar was actually an ingredient in some of them.

    As recorded in this card: Hill LL, Woodruff LH, Foote JC, Barreto-Alcoba M (2005), Journal of the American Dietetic Association, [primary]

    Note: One case and a product check, 2005. A harm signal for the tablet form, not the liquid.
