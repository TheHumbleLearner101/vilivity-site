Study: Agatha 1978, Nigerian Medical Journal

Some preliminary observations on the effects of kola nut on the cardiovascular system

  • Kola nut: caffeine, the heart and health claims Tier D

    C Anaesthetised cats (n=not stated)

    Laboratory observation of aqueous kola nut extract on blood pressure and heart.

    Low doses raised blood pressure by 8 to 9% and high doses lowered it by 18 to 71%. With larger doses a slow heart rate developed and cardiac arrhythmias occurred as a terminal event. The authors say the effects are probably due to caffeine and that the role of habitual chewing in heart disease needs discussion.

    As recorded in this card: Agatha M, Breckenridge C, Soyemi EA (1978), Nigerian Medical Journal, [primary]

    Note: A preliminary animal study from 1978; the doses are not comparable with chewing a nut.
