Study: Wang 2024, Nutrients

Effects of Creatine Supplementation and Resistance Training on Muscle Strength Gains in Adults <50 Years of Age: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

Citation

Wang Z, Qiu B, Li R, Han Y et al (2024). Nutrients. PubMed id 39519498.

Open on PubMed

  • Creatine supplements: strength, kidneys and your kidney test Tier B moderate

    B Adults under 50 in 23 trials of creatine with resistance training (460 men and 49 women across the included studies) (n=509)

    Systematic review and meta-analysis of randomised trials.

    Creatine with resistance training raised upper-body strength by 4.4 kg and lower-body strength by 11.4 kg more than placebo. Males gained strength; females showed no significant gains, though only three studies included women. Ethnicity was not reported.

    As recorded in this card: Wang Z, Qiu B, Li R, Han Y et al (2024), Nutrients, [review]

    Note: Mostly young men. Strength outcomes, not health outcomes.
