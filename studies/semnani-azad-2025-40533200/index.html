Study: Semnani-Azad 2025, BMJ

Intermittent fasting strategies and their effects on body weight and other cardiometabolic risk factors: systematic review and network meta-analysis of randomised clinical trials

Citation

Semnani-Azad Z, Khan TA, Chiavaroli L, Chen V et al (2025). BMJ. PubMed id 40533200.

  • Intermittent fasting for weight and health Tier B moderate

    B Adults in 99 randomised trials (720 healthy, 5,862 with health conditions) (n=6582)

    Systematic review and network meta-analysis of intermittent fasting (alternate-day, time-restricted, whole-day) against continuous calorie restriction or eating freely.

    All fasting and calorie-restriction diets reduced weight compared with eating freely. Compared with continuous calorie restriction, only alternate-day fasting gave extra weight loss (1.29 kg, moderate certainty). In trials of 24 weeks or longer, strategies only beat eating freely.

    As recorded in this card: Semnani-Azad Z, Khan TA, Chiavaroli L, Chen V et al (2025), BMJ, [review]

    Note: Mostly short trials. Ethnicity not reported in the abstract.
