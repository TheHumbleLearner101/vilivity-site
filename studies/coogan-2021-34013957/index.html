Study: Coogan 2021, Carcinogenesis

Hair product use and breast cancer incidence in the Black Women's Health Study

Citation

Coogan PF, Rosenberg L, Palmer JR, Cozier YC et al (2021). Carcinogenesis. PubMed id 34013957.

Open on PubMed

  • Chemical relaxers and hair straighteners Tier B moderate

    B 50,543 Black women, Black Women's Health Study; 2,311 breast cancers (n=50543)

    Prospective cohort 1997-2017.

    Heavy use of relaxers gave a hazard ratio for breast cancer of 1.13 (95% CI 0.96-1.33), not significant. For heavy use of lye-containing relaxers the hazard ratio for ER-positive breast cancer was 1.32 (0.97-1.80), also not significant. The authors call the results largely null.
