Study: Baird 2003, American Journal of Obstetrics and Gynecology

High cumulative incidence of uterine leiomyoma in black and white women: ultrasound evidence

Citation

Baird DD, Dunson DB, Hill MC, Cousins D et al (2003). American Journal of Obstetrics and Gynecology. PubMed id 12548202.

  • Fibroids: symptoms, treatment and pregnancy Tier B moderate

    B 1,364 randomly selected women aged 35-49 in a US urban health plan (Black and White) (n=1364)

    Ultrasound screening plus records, estimating cumulative incidence of fibroids by age.

    By age 50, cumulative incidence of fibroids was above 80% for Black women and nearly 70% for White women (odds ratio 2.9, 95% CI 2.5-3.4). Fibroids developed at earlier ages in Black women. Fifty-one percent of premenopausal women with no previous diagnosis had ultrasound evidence of fibroids.

    As recorded in this card: Baird DD, Dunson DB, Hill MC, Cousins D et al (2003), American Journal of Obstetrics and Gynecology, [primary]
