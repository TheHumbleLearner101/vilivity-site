Study: Roberts 2015, Journal of Physiology

Post-exercise cold water immersion attenuates acute anabolic signalling and long-term adaptations in muscle to strength training

Citation

Roberts LA, Raastad T, Markworth JF, Figueiredo VC et al (2015). Journal of Physiology. PubMed id 26174323.

Open on PubMed

Each card gives its own wording for who was tested, how and what was found.

  • Ice baths and cold exposure Tier B moderate

    B 21 physically active men (12-week study) and 9 men (muscle biopsy study) (n=30)

    Randomised comparison of 10 minutes of cold-water immersion against active recovery after strength training.

    Strength and muscle mass increased more with active recovery than with cold-water immersion, and several markers of muscle growth signalling were lower after cold-water immersion.

    As recorded in this card: Roberts LA, Raastad T, Markworth JF, Figueiredo VC et al (2015), Journal of Physiology, [primary]

    Note: Counter-evidence to the recovery claim: relevant if you lift to get bigger or stronger. Men only; ethnicity not reported in the abstract.
