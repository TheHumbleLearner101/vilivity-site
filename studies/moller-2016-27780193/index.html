Study: Moller 2016, British Journal of Cancer

Short-term breast cancer survival in relation to ethnicity, stage, grade and receptor status: national cohort study in England

Citation

Moller H, Henson K, Luchtenborg M, Broggio J et al (2016). British Journal of Cancer. PubMed id 27780193.

  • Breast cancer, triple-negative disease and screening Tier B moderate

    B 86,852 women with breast cancer diagnosed in England 2012-2013 (n=86852)

    National cancer-registry cohort, Cox models for two-year death.

    Triple-negative cancers had an adjusted hazard ratio for death of 2.00 (95% CI 1.84-2.17). Black women had an age-adjusted hazard ratio of 1.77 (1.48-2.13) against White women. The authors say the excess mortality has contributions from socio-economic factors, stage distribution and tumour biology.

    As recorded in this card: Moller H, Henson K, Luchtenborg M, Broggio J et al (2016), British Journal of Cancer, [primary]

    Note: Two-year deaths only; one author was formerly employed by BUPA and two received HCA International funding for unrelated work.
