Study: Velez 2014, Human Genetics

Admixture mapping identifies a locus at 15q21.2-22.3 associated with keloid formation in African Americans

Citation

Velez Edwards DR, Tsosie KS, Williams SM, Edwards TL et al (2014). Human Genetics. PubMed id 25280642.

  • Keloids and piercings Tier B moderate

    B 478 African Americans (122 keloid cases, 356 controls) (n=478)

    Admixture mapping and exome association.

    A region on chromosome 15 and variants in two myosin genes were associated with keloid risk. The authors give the figure that keloids occur about 20 times more often in people of African than of European descent.

    As recorded in this card: Velez Edwards DR, Tsosie KS, Williams SM, Edwards TL et al (2014), Human Genetics, [primary]

    Note: The 20-fold figure is quoted from the paper's introduction and is the authors' background statement, not their finding.
