Study: Mathey 2024, Journal of Allergy and Clinical Immunology

Meta-analysis of ACE inhibitor-induced angioedema identifies novel risk locus

Citation

Mathey CM, Maj C, Eriksson N, Krebs K et al (2024). Journal of Allergy and Clinical Immunology. PubMed id 38300190.

Open on PubMed

  • How some medicines work differently by gene Tier B moderate

    B More than 1,000 European patients with ACE inhibitor angioedema from 8 cohorts, plus an African-American cohort (n=more than 1000)

    Genome-wide association meta-analysis.

    Three genome-wide significant risk loci were found, one new (20q11.22). Highlighted genes included BDKRB2 and F5. Lead variants showed similar effect sizes and direction in an African-American cohort.

    As recorded in this card: Mathey CM, Maj C, Eriksson N, Krebs K et al (2024), Journal of Allergy and Clinical Immunology, [primary]

    Note: Discovery cohorts were European; the African-American analysis was exploratory and its size is not stated in the abstract.
