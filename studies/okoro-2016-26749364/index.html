Study: Okoro 2016, International Journal of Dermatology

Association of diet with acne vulgaris among adolescents in Ibadan, southwest Nigeria

Citation

Okoro EO, Ogunbiyi AO, George AO, Subulade MO (2016). International Journal of Dermatology. PubMed id 26749364.

  • Does diet cause acne? Sugar, dairy and foods linked to spots Tier B thin

    B 464 adolescents (mean age 13.6) in schools in Ibadan, southwest Nigeria; 64.4% had acne (n=464)

    Cross-sectional survey with examination for facial acne and food-frequency questions.

    Acne was more common in students who reported at least daily milk as a drink (72.6% vs 62.0%), corn (76.6% vs 62.3%), fried beef (75.0% vs 62.1%) and cake (77.8% vs 62.3%), and less common with daily bananas (55.3% vs 67.6%). The authors say an interventional dietary study is needed.

    As recorded in this card: Okoro EO, Ogunbiyi AO, George AO, Subulade MO (2016), International Journal of Dermatology, [primary]

    Note: A cross-sectional snapshot: it cannot show that food caused acne. Funding not checked.
