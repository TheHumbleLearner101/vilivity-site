How each entry is built and graded

Each entry gives a plain answer first, what to do and avoid, when to see someone, and then the evidence behind it. Every claim carries a source you can open and a grade: A (tested in people like you), B (tested in other people, or observed in Black populations), C (lab only) or D (untested), each with a strength of strong, moderate or thin. Practices the founder stands behind are marked as his own and never mixed into the grade.
