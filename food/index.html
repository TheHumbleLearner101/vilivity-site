Food

Foods and drinks that people use for health.

Tier A: Tested in people like you (2)

  • Hibiscus (sorrel, zobo, bissap) and blood pressure Tier A thinHibiscus tea lowered blood pressure a little in several trials, including one in Nigerian adults.
  • Salt and blood pressure Tier A moderateCutting salt lowers blood pressure in Black and non-Black adults alike, and the DASH eating pattern did too in a large US feeding trial that was mostly Black.

Tier B: Tested or observed (see the evidence) (15)

  • Apple cider vinegar: weight, blood sugar and safety Tier B thin Harm signalApple cider vinegar may lower blood sugar and weight slightly in short trials, but the trials are small and varied, and one well-known weight trial was retracted.
  • Baobab fruit: blood sugar, iron and vitamin C Tier B thinBaobab fruit is a traditional African food and a source of vitamin C.
  • Black seed oil (Nigella sativa) Tier B moderateA 2025 review of 82 studies of black seed (Nigella sativa) found small improvements in blood pressure, blood sugar, cholesterol and body fat, and called it a promising add-on.
  • Bovine colostrum supplements for immunity and gut health Tier B thinBovine colostrum, the first milk from cows, has small, mixed trials in athletes and a few other groups, with little effect on immune markers.
  • Bush teas and herbal remedies (cerasee, bitter leaf) with medicines Tier B thin Harm signalNatural does not mean safe with medicine.
  • Cod liver oil: vitamin D, vitamin A and colds Tier B moderate Harm signalCod liver oil is a source of vitamin D, which the UK's NHS says people with dark skin should consider taking all year.
  • Does diet cause acne? Sugar, dairy and foods linked to spots Tier B thinNo diet is proven to treat acne, and the British Association of Dermatologists says a healthy, balanced diet is all it advises.
  • Fasting in Ramadan with diabetes or sickle cell Tier B thin Harm signalWith diabetes, fasting in Ramadan can cause low blood sugar and dehydration, and the evidence on sickle cell disease is small and mixed.
  • Fibroids, food and supplements Tier B thinNo food has been shown to shrink fibroids.
  • Intermittent fasting for weight and health Tier B moderate Harm signalIntermittent fasting lost weight in trials, about the same as ordinary calorie cutting, and one small pilot of Black adults in churches found it workable.
  • Lion's mane and reishi mushroom supplements Tier B thinLion's mane and reishi have only small trials behind them, mostly low quality, in older Japanese adults with memory loss, healthy adults, and cancer patients on treatment.
  • Moringa: blood sugar, blood pressure and general health Tier B thinMoringa leaf is a nutritious food, but a 2025 review of nine trials found no consistent benefit for blood sugar, weight or cholesterol, and rated the evidence very low certainty.
  • Plantain, yam and cassava: blood sugar and safety Tier B thin Harm signalIn glucose tests, cassava, pounded yam and several West African staples raised blood sugar quickly, while breadfruit, white yam, eddoes and tannia were in the middle.
  • Probiotics and gut health Tier B moderate Harm signalProbiotics reduced diarrhoea caused by antibiotics in trials, and the UK's NHS says they may ease some IBS symptoms, but evidence for most other claims is thin.
  • Turmeric and curcumin supplements Tier B moderate Harm signalTurmeric and curcumin supplements may ease joint pain a little in low-quality trials, but NCCIH says we do not know enough to say they help.

Tier D: Untested (5)

  • Caribbean and West African diets and health Tier DNo trial has tested a traditional Caribbean or West African diet for blood pressure or diabetes, so nobody can say whether it is better or worse.
  • Kola nut: caffeine, the heart and health claims Tier D Harm signalKola nut is a traditional stimulant chewed and shared across West Africa.
  • Sea moss (Irish moss): health claims, iodine and heavy metals Tier D Harm signalMy search found no human study showing sea moss improves health.
  • Soursop (graviola): cancer cure, blood sugar and safety Tier D Harm signalThere is no proof that soursop treats cancer; human studies are lacking.
  • Vitamins and supplements: what the NHS says Tier DThe NHS says most people get all the nutrients they need from a varied, balanced diet.
