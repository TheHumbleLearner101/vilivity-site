Home / Food / Taking too many vitamins and supplements

Question

What happens if you take too many vitamins?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-17
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Add up everything you take, and ask a pharmacist or doctor if you are pregnant.

Also asked as

  • Can you take too much vitamin C?
  • Can you take too much vitamin B12?
  • Can you take too many vitamins when pregnant?

From the card

Yes. Food rarely causes trouble, but supplements can, and the safe daily ceiling differs by vitamin and by country. Some taken in large amounts cause stomach upset; others build up in the body, such as vitamin A and vitamin D. Add up everything you take, and ask a pharmacist or doctor if you are pregnant.
