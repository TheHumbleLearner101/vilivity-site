Home / Food / Does diet cause acne? Sugar, dairy and foods linked to spots

Question

does dairy cause acne breakouts

  • Tier B thin Tested, but not in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Dairy and acne is unclear. A review of 14 observational studies found any dairy, milk, yogurt and cheese linked to higher odds of acne, but with bias and smaller adjusted results, and it may depend on sex, ethnicity and diet habits. Observational links cannot show cause, so do not assume milk causes your acne.

What this is based on

  • Who was tested: 78,529 children, adolescents and young adults aged 7 to 30 in 14 observational studies. What was found: Any dairy, milk, yogurt and cheese were each associated with higher odds of acne (for example any dairy OR 1.25, 95% CI 1.15 to 1.36). Adjusted results were smaller, there was publication bias and heterogeneity, and the authors say results should be read with caution.Tested Juhl 2018 Study page
  • Who was tested: Mixed populations in 34 included studies (Ottawa quality scale). What was found: High glycaemic index and glycaemic load and carbohydrate intake were positively associated with acne and its severity, and this was supported by randomised trials. The dairy literature is mixed and may depend on sex, ethnicity and cultural dietary habits. The authors call the effect modest and say further trials are needed.Tested Meixiong 2022 Study page
  • Assuming milk causes your acne: the dairy evidence is observational and mixed.Tested Meixiong 2022

From the card

No diet is proven to treat acne, and the British Association of Dermatologists says a healthy, balanced diet is all it advises. Some studies link sugary, refined foods and dairy to more spots. A 12-week trial in 43 Australian men found a low-glycaemic diet reduced spots. In Nigerian teenagers, milk, fried beef and cake were linked to more acne.
