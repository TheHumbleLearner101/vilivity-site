Question

What is the downside of taking colostrum?

  • Tier B thin Tested, but not in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

We found no harm signal in the abstracts we read. It is a cow's milk product, so check first if dairy upsets you. A rash, swelling or breathing difficulty after taking it needs urgent care, and tummy pain, bloating or diarrhoea after each dose should be seen by a pharmacist or GP (UK). Long-term use is untested.

From the card

Bovine colostrum, the first milk from cows, has small, mixed trials in athletes and a few other groups, with little effect on immune markers. Our search found no trial of colostrum in Black participants. It is a cow's milk product, so check first if dairy upsets you, and do not use it to treat an infection.
