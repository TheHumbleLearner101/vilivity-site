Home / Food / Apple cider vinegar: weight, blood sugar and safety

Question

Who should avoid apple cider vinegar?

  • Tier B thin Tested, but not in people like you
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Avoid apple cider vinegar tablets: oesophageal injury was reported, and tablet products varied widely in what they contained. If you have diabetes and are thinking of using it alongside your medicine, ask your diabetes nurse or pharmacist first. Avoid drinking it undiluted or at bedtime, and see a doctor or urgent care for pain on swallowing.

From the card

Apple cider vinegar may lower blood sugar and weight slightly in short trials, but the trials are small and varied, and one well-known weight trial was retracted. Our search found no trial of apple cider vinegar in Black participants. Dilute it or use it in food, and avoid the tablets: one injured a throat.
