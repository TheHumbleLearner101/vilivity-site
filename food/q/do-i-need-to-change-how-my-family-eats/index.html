Question

Do I need to change how my family eats?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Judge your own plate on the tested pattern rather than on where the food comes from: plenty of fruit and vegetables, low-fat dairy and less salt, less saturated fat and less sugar. Keep salt to no more than 6g a day, counting stock, sauces, bread and processed foods. See a GP (UK) or dietitian first if you have high blood pressure, type 2 diabetes or kidney disease and want to change your diet a lot.

What this is based on

  • No trial has tested a traditional Caribbean or West African diet for blood pressure or diabetes, so nobody can say whether it is better or worse. What is tested is the pattern: more fruit and vegetables, less salt and saturated fat lowered blood pressure in a mostly Black US trial.
