Question

Which foods feed or shrink fibroids?

  • Tier B thin Tested, but not in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

No food has been shown to shrink fibroids. A balanced diet with plenty of fruit and vegetables is advised, as for everyone. Small trials of vitamin D and of green tea extract found smaller fibroids, but not in Black women. For heavy or painful periods, see a doctor rather than treating with food alone.

What this is based on

  • No food has been shown to shrink fibroids. In Black women, a high glycaemic index diet was only weakly linked to fibroid risk. Five trials of vitamin D in other populations found smaller fibroids, and one tiny trial of green tea extract found the same; both need larger trials.
