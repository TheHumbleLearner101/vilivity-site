Home / Food / Salt and blood pressure

Question

how much salt should i eat with high blood pressure

  • Tier A moderate Tested in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

The NHS (UK) advises adults to eat no more than 6g of salt a day, and the same limit applies to every adult. Get your blood pressure checked even if you feel well. If you take blood pressure medicine, tell your GP or pharmacist before changing it because you have cut salt.

What this is based on

  • Eat no more than 6g of salt a day as an adult: about three-quarters of the salt we eat is already in the food we buy.Clinical guidance NHS, "8 tips for healthy eating"
  • Get your blood pressure checked, even if you feel well.Clinical guidance NHS, "High blood pressure (hypertension)"
  • Stopping or changing blood pressure medicine because you have cut salt: tell your doctor or pharmacist first.Clinical guidance NHS, "High blood pressure (hypertension)"

Checked against its sources on 2026-11-16.

From the card

Cutting salt lowers blood pressure in Black and non-Black adults alike, and the DASH eating pattern did too in a large US feeding trial that was mostly Black. Salt matters, but a special inherited salt gene is not established, and historians dispute the slavery-era theory behind it. Aim for no more than 6g a day, like every adult.
