Home / Food / Does diet cause acne? Sugar, dairy and foods linked to spots

Question

Do foods cause acne?

  • Tier B thin Tested, but not in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

No diet is proven to treat acne, and the British Association of Dermatologists advises only a healthy, balanced diet. Some foods are linked to more spots, but the link is not proven. Check your hair and skin products first, because the NHS says products that make skin greasy can cause acne. Avoid strict diets that cut everything out.
