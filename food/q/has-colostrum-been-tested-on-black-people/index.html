Home / Food / Bovine colostrum supplements for immunity and gut health

Question

Has colostrum been tested on Black people?

  • Tier B thin Tested, but not in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Our search found no trial of colostrum in Black participants: a PubMed search with terms for African American, Black adults and similar returned one record, a lactation study in mothers, and ClinicalTrials.gov returned one multi-ingredient supplement study. The same search style for vitamin D returned 930 records, so the search works. Any effect in Black participants is untested.

What this is based on

  • Bovine colostrum, the first milk from cows, has small, mixed trials in athletes and a few other groups, with little effect on immune markers. Our search found no trial of colostrum in Black participants. It is a cow's milk product, so check first if dairy upsets you, and do not use it to treat an infection.From the card's plain answer
