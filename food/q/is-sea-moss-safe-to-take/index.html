Home / Food / Sea moss (Irish moss): health claims, iodine and heavy metals

Question

is sea moss safe to take

  • Tier D Untested
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Sea moss carries iodine, which in excess can upset the thyroid, and seaweed can hold heavy metals such as arsenic, lead, cadmium and mercury. How much a typical gel or drink contains is untested. If you use it often, tell your doctor or pharmacist about any herbal product you take, and see a doctor about a swelling at the front of the neck, a racing heart or unexplained weight change.

What this is based on

  • Tell your doctor or pharmacist about any herbal product you take.Clinical guidance NHS, "Herbal medicines and complementary therapies"
  • Who was tested: Breastfeeding mothers and infants (database summary). What was found: Sea moss (Irish moss, Chondrus crispus) is used as food and medicine, although no medicinal value has been proven. It contains carrageenan and minerals including iodine, and some toxic heavy metals such as arsenic, lead, cadmium and mercury from the water. Excess iodine from seaweed in a mother's diet has caused thyroid suppression in breastfed infants, so it should be avoided while breastfeeding unless iodine and heavy metal levels are shown to be low enough.Tested Drugs 2006 Study page
  • Untested: sea moss for immunity, fertility, skin, energy or any other claim; how much iodine or metal typical sea moss gel or drink contains (the metals paper tested dried seaweeds, not gel); whether regular use harms the thyroid (no case series or cohort opened); the results of NCT07127393 (not read). My PubMed search for 'Chondrus crispus seaweed human trial' returned 467 records, none a trial of sea moss for health; one ClinicalTrials.gov record for the species.Not tested, and gaps (from the card)

From the card

My search found no human study showing sea moss improves health. It contains iodine, which in excess can upset the thyroid, and seaweed can carry heavy metals such as arsenic and cadmium. Avoid it while breastfeeding, and ask your doctor first if you have a thyroid condition.
