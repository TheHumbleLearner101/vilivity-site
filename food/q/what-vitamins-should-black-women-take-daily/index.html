Question

what vitamins should black women take daily

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

The NHS in the UK says to consider a daily vitamin D supplement all year if your background is African, African-Caribbean or South Asian, and everyone should consider one in autumn and winter. In a US study, Black participants had lower vitamin D than white participants, though higher bone density, so whether a supplement helps Black adults is contested. Beyond that, get nutrients from food. This entry gives no doses.

What this is based on

  • Aim to get your nutrients from a varied and balanced diet first.Clinical guidance NHS, "Vitamins and minerals"
  • Consider a daily vitamin D supplement all year if your background is African, African-Caribbean or South Asian; everyone should consider one in autumn and winter.Clinical guidance NHS, "Vitamin D"
  • Who was tested: 2,085 Black and white US adults. What was found: Mean total 25(OH)D was 15.6 against 25.8 ng/mL in Black against white participants, and vitamin D-binding protein was 168 against 337 micrograms/mL; Black participants had higher bone mineral density. Genetic variants explained about 79% of variation in the binding protein.Tested Powe 2013 Study page
  • Not opened: folic acid and pregnancy supplement pages; B12 and calcium for vegetarians or dairy avoiders; any trial of multivitamins in Black adults; the UK's NHS vitamins overview page I opened has a review date of 3 August 2023, now past. From the card

The NHS says most people get all the nutrients they need from a varied, balanced diet. The main exception for you is vitamin D: people with African, African-Caribbean or South Asian backgrounds should consider a daily supplement all year. Do not take iron without a blood test, and tell a pharmacist about any herbal remedy if you take other medicines.
