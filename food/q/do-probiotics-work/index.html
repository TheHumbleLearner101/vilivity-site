Home / Food / Probiotics and gut health

Question

Do probiotics work?

  • Tier B moderate Tested, but not in people like you
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Probiotics reduced antibiotic-related diarrhoea by 38 per cent in a review of 36 randomised, placebo-controlled trials with 9,312 adults taking antibiotics. The NHS (England) says they may ease some IBS symptoms, but evidence for most other claims is thin. That review did not report ethnicity, and our search found no gut trial in Black participants.

  • Who was tested: Adults taking antibiotics in 36 randomised, placebo-controlled trials. What was found: Probiotics reduced antibiotic-associated diarrhoea by 38 per cent (relative risk 0.62, 95% CI 0.51 to 0.74), more when started early. Adverse events were no more common than with placebo (relative risk 1.00).Tested Liao 2021 Study page
