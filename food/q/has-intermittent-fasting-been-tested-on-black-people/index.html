Home / Food / Intermittent fasting for weight and health

Question

Has intermittent fasting been tested on Black people?

  • Tier B moderate Tested, but not in people like you
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

One randomised pilot in Black adults exists: in five Black churches in Western New York State, weight fell about 3.5 kg in six months with faith-based fasting against 2.9 kg with calorie reduction. It tested feasibility, so it does not show fasting beats calorie reduction. In an exploratory analysis of 13 Black participants, early time-restricted eating improved blood pressure and glucose more than in White participants, a hint only.

What this is based on

  • Who was tested: Black adults with BMI of 25 or more, from five Black churches in Western New York State. What was found: Weight fell in both arms (fasting -3.5 kg, calorie reduction -2.9 kg). The fasting group ate about 414 kcal a day less than the calorie-reduction group, but ate less fruit, vegetables and fibre. Satisfaction was high. The authors conclude it is feasible and larger studies are needed.Tested Yeary 2025 Study page
  • Who was tested: Adults aged 25 to 75 with obesity (BMI 30 to 60) in a 14-week weight-loss trial; the race analysis used 13 Black participants. What was found: Early time-restricted eating lowered blood pressure in adults with hypertension and HbA1c in prediabetes, and raised cholesterol in people with severe obesity. Blood pressure and glucose measures improved more in Black than in White participants, but the authors say the Black group was small and the result needs further investigation.Tested Rehman 2026 Study page
  • Intermittent fasting lost weight in trials, about the same as ordinary calorie cutting, and one small pilot of Black adults in churches found it workable. Avoid it if you have had an eating disorder or are pregnant or breastfeeding, and plan it with your care team if you have diabetes. Religious fasting has its own guidance.From the card's plain answer
