Home / Food / Hibiscus (sorrel, zobo, bissap) and blood pressure

Question

Can I drink zobo instead of taking my tablets?

  • Tier A thin Tested in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

No. Do not stop or skip prescribed medicine because you drink zobo, and do not treat a high reading as something a drink will fix. Hibiscus tea lowered blood pressure a little in several trials, but it is a reasonable drink, not a replacement for blood-pressure medicine. Tell your doctor or pharmacist you drink it.

What this is based on

  • Hibiscus tea lowered blood pressure a little in several trials, including one in Nigerian adults. A stricter review found too little reliable evidence, so the effect is contested. It is a reasonable drink, not a replacement for blood-pressure medicine.From the card's plain answer

Hibiscus tea lowered blood pressure a little in several trials, including one in Nigerian adults. A stricter review found too little reliable evidence, so the effect is contested. It is a reasonable drink, not a replacement for blood-pressure medicine.
