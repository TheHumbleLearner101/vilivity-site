Question

Is black seed oil safe with medicines?

  • Tier B moderate Tested, but not in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Check with a pharmacist before taking black seed if you take other medicines, including blood pressure and diabetes medicines, and tell your doctor or pharmacist about any herbal product. Our search did not open any study of drug interactions, so we cannot say what not to mix with it.

From the card

A 2025 review of 82 studies of black seed (Nigella sativa) found small improvements in blood pressure, blood sugar, cholesterol and body fat, and called it a promising add-on. The trials were not in Black people, and I did not read how sure the authors were. It is not a cure, and not a replacement for medicine.
