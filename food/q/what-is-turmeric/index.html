Question

What is turmeric?

  • Tier B moderate Tested, but not in people like you
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Turmeric is a yellow root used in cooking and in Chinese, Ayurvedic, Islamic and Thai traditional medicine for digestion, skin and joint problems. Curcumin is its main compound, now sold in concentrated capsules. The trials tested curcumin and turmeric extract in arthritis and found improved pain and inflammation markers, in low-quality trials.

What this is based on

  • Turmeric is a yellow root used in cooking and in Chinese, Ayurvedic, Islamic and Thai traditional medicine for digestion, skin and joint problems. Curcumin, its main compound, is now sold in concentrated capsules.Tradition and common belief, not evidence
  • Who was tested: People with arthritis (osteoarthritis, rheumatoid, ankylosing spondylitis, juvenile arthritis, gout) in 29 randomised trials. What was found: Curcumin and turmeric extract improved pain and inflammation markers in arthritis in the pooled trials and appeared safe. The authors warn the trials are low quality and few, and that the conclusions need careful interpretation.Tested Zeng 2022 Study page

From the card

Turmeric and curcumin supplements may ease joint pain a little in low-quality trials, but NCCIH says we do not know enough to say they help. Our search found no trial of turmeric or curcumin in Black participants. Rare serious liver injury is reported, and some loose turmeric powder carries lead. Stop and see a doctor if you turn yellow.
