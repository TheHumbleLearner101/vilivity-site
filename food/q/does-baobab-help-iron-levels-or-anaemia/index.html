Home / Food / Baobab fruit: blood sugar, iron and vitamin C

Question

Does baobab help iron levels or anaemia?

  • Tier B thin Tested, but not in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

In a 12-week trial of 58 Kenyan schoolchildren aged 6 to 12 with low haemoglobin, a daily baobab pulp drink did not change haemoglobin, ferritin or soluble transferrin receptor significantly compared with a control drink. Do not use baobab instead of iron tablets or other anaemia treatment. See a GP for a blood test if you are often tired, pale or short of breath.

What this is based on

  • Who was tested: 58 Kenyan schoolchildren aged 6 to 12 with haemoglobin below 12.2 g/dl. What was found: Haemoglobin, ferritin and soluble transferrin receptor did not differ significantly between the groups. Haemoglobin rose 2.2% with baobab and fell 1.2% in the control group, a difference the authors did not find significant.Tested Evang 2021 Study page
  • Using baobab instead of iron tablets or other treatment for anaemia: the Kenyan trial found no significant effect.Tested Evang 2021
  • You are often tired, pale or short of breath and think it could be iron (doctor, for a blood test).When to see someone

From the card

Baobab fruit is a traditional African food and a source of vitamin C. Two small trials in healthy adults in Europe found it lowered the blood sugar rise after a sugary or starchy load. A trial in Kenyan schoolchildren found no significant effect on iron or haemoglobin. My search found no trial in people with diabetes.
