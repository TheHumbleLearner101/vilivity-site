Home / Food / Does diet cause acne? Sugar, dairy and foods linked to spots

Question

Does sugar or dairy make acne worse?

  • Tier B thin Tested, but not in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Sugary, refined foods are linked to acne: a review of 34 studies tied high glycaemic index and load to acne and its severity, and a 12-week trial in 43 Australian men found a low-glycaemic diet cut spots more. The effect is modest and no trial has tested it in Black people. Dairy evidence is mixed.

What this is based on

  • Who was tested: Mixed populations in 34 included studies (Ottawa quality scale). What was found: High glycaemic index and glycaemic load and carbohydrate intake were positively associated with acne and its severity, and this was supported by randomised trials. The dairy literature is mixed and may depend on sex, ethnicity and cultural dietary habits. The authors call the effect modest and say further trials are needed.Tested Meixiong 2022 Study page
  • Who was tested: 43 male acne patients aged 15 to 25 in Australia. What was found: Total lesion counts fell more with the low-glycaemic-load diet (-23.5 vs -12.0, P=0.03). Weight and insulin sensitivity also improved more, so the authors say weight loss and diet effects cannot be separated.Tested Smith 2007 Study page
  • No diet is proven to treat acne, and the British Association of Dermatologists says a healthy, balanced diet is all it advises. Some studies link sugary, refined foods and dairy to more spots. A 12-week trial in 43 Australian men found a low-glycaemic diet reduced spots. In Nigerian teenagers, milk, fried beef and cake were linked to more acne.From the card's plain answer
  • Untested: any dietary trial in Black participants; whether diet changes dark marks after acne; whiteners and home 'blood cleansers'. The UK's NHS acne causes page does not mention diet. Studies were not checked for funding except where the abstract states it. Acne treatment and hair-oil advice sit in the acne entry.Not tested, and gaps (from the card)
