Home / Food / Moringa: blood sugar, blood pressure and general health

Question

Is moringa tea or powder good for you?

  • Tier B thin Tested, but not in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Long-term safety of moringa is untested here. Moringa leaf is a nutritious food and the trials used leaf or powder. Check with a pharmacist if you take other medicine. See a GP for a rash, swelling or tummy pain after taking it, and in the UK call 999 for swelling of the face or throat.

What this is based on

  • Moringa leaf is a nutritious food, but a 2025 review of nine trials found no consistent benefit for blood sugar, weight or cholesterol, and rated the evidence very low certainty. A small trial in Nigerian adults with type 2 diabetes found no difference in blood sugar after two weeks. Do not swap it for medicine.From the card's plain answer
  • Untested or not opened: moringa as a tea (the trials used leaf or powder); long-term safety and interactions with diabetes or blood pressure medicines; moringa for breast milk (a 2025 galactagogue systematic review, PMID 40724308, was found but not opened); seed and root products; trials longer than 12 weeks in people of African descent. No ClinicalTrials.gov search was needed beyond the review, which lists the trials it pooled.Not tested, and gaps (from the card)
  • Check first with a pharmacist before taking moringa capsules or powder if you take other medicine, including diabetes or blood pressure tablets.Clinical guidance NHS, "Herbal medicines and complementary therapies"
  • A rash, swelling or tummy pain after taking it (doctor; 999 in the UK, or your local emergency number for swelling of the face or throat or trouble breathing).When to see someone
