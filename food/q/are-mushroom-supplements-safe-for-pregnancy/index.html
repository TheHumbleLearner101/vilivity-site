Question

are mushroom supplements safe for pregnancy

  • Tier B thin Tested, but not in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Check with a pharmacist before taking mushroom supplements if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, or have kidney or liver problems. Tell your doctor or pharmacist you take them if you are on cancer treatment or any regular medicine. We opened no source on mushroom and medicine interactions, so they are untested here.

From the card

Lion's mane and reishi have only small trials behind them, mostly low quality, in older Japanese adults with memory loss, healthy adults, and cancer patients on treatment. Our search found no trial of these mushrooms in Black participants. Treat claims for memory or immunity as untested for you, and tell your doctor or pharmacist you take them.
