Home / Food / Black seed oil (Nigella sativa)

Question

What does black seed oil actually do for health?

  • Tier B moderate Tested, but not in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

A 2025 review of 82 studies with 5,026 people found black seed supplements gave small improvements in blood pressure, blood sugar, cholesterol and body fat, and the authors called it a promising add-on. The trials were not in Black people. It is not a cure and not a replacement for medicine.

  • Who was tested: 82 studies (79 quantitative and 3 qualitative; the title calls them randomised controlled trials), 5,026 participants, 1 to 48 weeks (doses 200 to 4,600 mg a day). What was found: Nigella sativa supplementation significantly improved body fat percentage, systolic blood pressure, mean arterial pressure, fasting blood sugar, HbA1c, post-meal glucose, insulin sensitivity, C-reactive protein, cholesterol fractions and triglycerides. The authors conclude it could be a promising adjunct therapy for improving cardiovascular risk factors.Tested Jafari 2025 Study page
  • Treat it as an add-on to treatment, not a substitute: the authors describe it only as a promising adjunct.Tested Jafari 2025
