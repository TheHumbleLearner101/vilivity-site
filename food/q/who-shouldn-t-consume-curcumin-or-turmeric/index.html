Home / Food / Turmeric and curcumin supplements

Question

who shouldn't consume curcumin or turmeric

  • Tier B moderate Tested, but not in people like you
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Avoid turmeric supplements in pregnancy: NCCIH (US) says they may be unsafe. Avoid highly bioavailable curcumin products, which NCCIH (US) says may harm your liver. If you take a blood thinner such as warfarin, diabetes medicine or any regular medicine, ask your pharmacist or prescriber before starting a supplement, because the NHS (England) says some supplements and remedies do not mix well with warfarin.

From the card

Turmeric and curcumin supplements may ease joint pain a little in low-quality trials, but NCCIH says we do not know enough to say they help. Our search found no trial of turmeric or curcumin in Black participants. Rare serious liver injury is reported, and some loose turmeric powder carries lead. Stop and see a doctor if you turn yellow.
