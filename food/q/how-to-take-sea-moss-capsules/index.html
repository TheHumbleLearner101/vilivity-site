Home / Food / Sea moss (Irish moss): health claims, iodine and heavy metals

Question

how to take sea moss capsules

  • Tier D Untested
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Check the label for iodine. The NHS in the UK says 0.5mg or less a day of iodine supplements is unlikely to cause harm, but how much iodine typical sea moss products hold is untested. This entry gives no dose or form of sea moss. Tell your doctor or pharmacist about any herbal product you take.

What this is based on

From the card

My search found no human study showing sea moss improves health. It contains iodine, which in excess can upset the thyroid, and seaweed can carry heavy metals such as arsenic and cadmium. Avoid it while breastfeeding, and ask your doctor first if you have a thyroid condition.
