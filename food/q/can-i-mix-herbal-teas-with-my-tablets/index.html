Home / Food / Bush teas and herbal remedies (cerasee, bitter leaf) with medicines

Question

Can I mix herbal teas with my tablets?

  • Tier B thin Tested, but not in people like you
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Natural does not mean safe with medicine: the NHS in the UK says do not mix herbal medicines with certain medicines, and always check with a pharmacist. Tell your GP or pharmacist about every tea or remedy you use, and read the leaflet in your medicine packet for herbal warnings.

What this is based on

  • Natural does not mean safe with medicine. A Cochrane review found insufficient evidence that bitter melon (cerasee) helps type 2 diabetes. Memorial Sloan Kettering warns it can add to diabetes medicines and reports case reports of serious harm.
