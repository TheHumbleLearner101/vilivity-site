Home / Food / Fasting in Ramadan with diabetes or sickle cell

Question

What do I do about my medicines in Ramadan?

  • Tier B thin Tested, but not in people like you
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Talk to your diabetes team before Ramadan about your medicines and a plan: they may change the type, dose or timing. See your GP (UK) or diabetes team beforehand if you take insulin or tablets, with time to adjust. Pre-mixed insulin is not recommended during fasting. Do not change your diabetes medicines yourself, or fast when you cannot reach your team.

From the card

With diabetes, fasting in Ramadan can cause low blood sugar and dehydration, and the evidence on sickle cell disease is small and mixed. None of the studies was of Black Muslims as a group. Diabetes UK says do not fast if your diabetes is poorly controlled. Agree a plan with your diabetes or sickle cell team before Ramadan.
