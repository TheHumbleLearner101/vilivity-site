Question

Is a traditional Caribbean or West African diet healthy?

  • Tier D Untested
  • Last checked 2026-11-12
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

No trial has tested a traditional Caribbean or West African diet for blood pressure or diabetes, so whether it is better or worse is untested. What is tested is the pattern: in the DASH-Sodium trial, 58% self-reported Black, more fruit, vegetables and low-fat dairy with less salt lowered blood pressure. NHS advice in the UK is about amounts of salt, saturated fat and sugar in a dish.
