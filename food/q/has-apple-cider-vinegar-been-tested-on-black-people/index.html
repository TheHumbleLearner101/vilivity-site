Home / Food / Apple cider vinegar: weight, blood sugar and safety

Question

Has apple cider vinegar been tested on Black people?

  • Tier B thin Tested, but not in people like you
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Our search found no trial of apple cider vinegar in Black participants: a PubMed search returned 0 records and so did ClinicalTrials.gov. The trials we found did not state ethnicity in the abstract, and the one weight trial in Lebanese young people was retracted. Any effect in Black participants is untested.

What this is based on

  • Apple cider vinegar may lower blood sugar and weight slightly in short trials, but the trials are small and varied, and one well-known weight trial was retracted.
