Home / Food / Lion's mane and reishi mushroom supplements

Question

Does lion's mane improve memory and focus?

  • Tier B thin Tested, but not in people like you
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

Lion's mane has only small trials behind it. In 30 Japanese adults aged 50 to 80 with mild cognitive impairment, cognitive scores rose more in the lion's mane group at weeks 8, 12 and 16, then fell back after stopping. In 41 healthy adults, one dose was followed by faster Stroop task performance at 60 minutes. Treat memory claims as unproven for you.

What this is based on

  • Lion's mane and reishi have only small trials behind them, mostly low quality, in older Japanese adults with memory loss, healthy adults, and cancer patients on treatment. Our search found no trial of these mushrooms in Black participants. Treat claims for memory or immunity as untested for you, and tell your doctor or pharmacist you take them.From the card's plain answer
  • Who was tested: Japanese men and women aged 50 to 80 with mild cognitive impairment. What was found: Cognitive scores rose more in the lion's mane group at weeks 8, 12 and 16, then fell back four weeks after stopping. Laboratory tests showed no adverse effect.Tested Mori 2009 Study page
  • Who was tested: Healthy adults aged 18 to 45 (country not stated in the abstract). What was found: One dose was followed by faster Stroop task performance at 60 minutes. A trend to lower self-rated stress after 28 days did not reach significance (p = 0.051). The authors say null and limited findings were also seen and call for larger studies.Tested Docherty 2023 Study page
  • Treat memory or immunity claims as unproven for you: the trials were small and in older Japanese adults with memory problems, or cancer patients.Tested Mori 2009
