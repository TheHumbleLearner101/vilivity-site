Home / Food / Intermittent fasting for weight and health

Question

does intermittent fasting work for prediabetes

  • Tier B moderate Tested, but not in people like you
  • Harm signal
  • Last checked 2026-11-16
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

Short answer

In a secondary subgroup analysis of a 14-week trial in adults with obesity (not a Black-specific trial), early time-restricted eating (8 hours, 07:00 to 15:00) lowered HbA1c in prediabetes. It also raised cholesterol in people with severe obesity. If you have diabetes, plan any change in meal times with your diabetes team.

What this is based on

  • Who was tested: Adults aged 25 to 75 with obesity (BMI 30 to 60) in a 14-week weight-loss trial; the race analysis used 13 Black participants. What was found: Early time-restricted eating lowered blood pressure in adults with hypertension and HbA1c in prediabetes, and raised cholesterol in people with severe obesity. Blood pressure and glucose measures improved more in Black than in White participants, but the authors say the Black group was small and the result needs further investigation.Tested Rehman 2026 Study page
  • You have diabetes, especially on insulin or tablets that can cause low blood sugar (diabetes team, before changing meal times).When to see someone
  • Delaying or skipping meals if you have diabetes, unless your diabetes team has planned it with you.Clinical guidance NHS My Health London, "Intermittent fasting"

Checked against its sources on 2026-11-16.

From the card

Intermittent fasting lost weight in trials, about the same as ordinary calorie cutting, and one small pilot of Black adults in churches found it workable. Avoid it if you have had an eating disorder or are pregnant or breastfeeding, and plan it with your care team if you have diabetes. Religious fasting has its own guidance.
