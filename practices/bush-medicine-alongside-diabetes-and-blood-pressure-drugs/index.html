Practice: Body

Bush medicine taken alongside diabetes and blood pressure tablets

  • Tier C thin Lab only
  • Harm: possible
  • Documented
  • Caribbean
  • Not yet clinically reviewed
Harm possible. Interaction risk is predicted (computer modelling), not observed in patients. The practical point: tell the doctor or pharmacist what you are drinking.

What people do

Take bush teas or herbal remedies for "sugar" and "pressure" in addition to, or sometimes instead of, prescribed medicines; about a third of Barbadian church-goers use herbal remedies.

Why people say it works

Tradition and common belief, not evidence.

Plants are "natural", familiar, cheap and passed on by older relatives.

Who documented it

  • Cohall DH et al 2012, West Indian Med J, PMID 23155984 survey

    Barbados: about 33% of Christian church-goers surveyed used herbal remedies, including for chronic conditions; health insurance made no difference to use.

  • Mahabir D, Gulliford MC 1997, Rev Panam Salud Publica, PMID 9128111 survey

    Trinidad: 42% of 622 diabetes patients used bush medicine; 81% of users gathered the plants themselves; insulin users were less frequent users.

The evidence

Tier is the evidence for the claim people make, not for the practice being widespread.

  1. C Drug-herb interactions: 11 popular Barbadian plants against the commonest antihypertensives

    In silico docking only. 27 of 30 compounds showed strong binding to drug targets; Catharanthus roseus, Phyllanthus niruri, Petroselinum crispum and Lantana involucrata flagged. Predicts possible interactions, not proven ones.

    Strength: thin. Barbadian botanicals and antihypertensive drugs, in silico, 2022, ACS Omega, PMID 36530331

Harm

Interaction risk is predicted (computer modelling), not observed in patients. The practical point: tell the doctor or pharmacist what you are drinking.

Gaps

  • No clinical study of an actual interaction found.
  • Non-disclosure rate in Caribbean patients not found (African figure: James 2018, 55.8 to 100% do not disclose).
