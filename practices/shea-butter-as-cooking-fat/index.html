Practice: Food

Cooking with shea butter

  • Tier B thin Observed in Black populations
  • Harm: none found
  • Documented
  • West Africa
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

What people do

Use shea butter as a cooking fat in northern Ghana and the savanna belt of West Africa.

Why people say it works

Tradition and common belief, not evidence.

It is the traditional local fat, regarded as healthy and "medicinal".

Who documented it

  • Cooking with shea butter and blood pressure, Ghana DHS 2014, 2020, Int J Vitam Nutr Res, PMID 30967105 survey

    Ghana Demographic and Health Survey 2014: 9,396 women and 4,388 men; shea butter consumption recorded.

The evidence

Tier is the evidence for the claim people make, not for the practice being widespread.

  1. B Cooking with shea butter vs vegetable oils and blood pressure

    13,784 Ghanaian adults, cross-sectional. Shea butter use associated with 2.43 mmHg lower systolic and 1.78 mmHg lower diastolic pressure; odds of hypertension 0.75 (CI 0.55 to 1.04, not significant). Authors call for rigorous study designs.

    Strength: thin, observational-black-population. Cooking with shea butter and blood pressure, Ghana DHS 2014, 2020, Int J Vitam Nutr Res, PMID 30967105

Harm

No harm identified; it is a saturated-fat food and this is a cross-sectional association.

Gaps

  • Confounding by region and wealth, though adjusted; no trial.
