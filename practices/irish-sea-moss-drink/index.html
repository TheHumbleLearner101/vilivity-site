Practice: Food

Irish moss (sea moss) drink

  • Tier D Untested
  • Harm: possible
  • Reported, not yet documented
  • Caribbean
  • Not yet clinically reviewed
Reported, not yet documented. We have not opened a source that shows people do this, so it is not counted in the main lists. The sources below are pointers, not proof.
Harm possible. Contamination in commercial products (microbes and metals).

What people do

Boil dried sea moss with milk, spices and sweetener for a thick drink; now sold as a gel supplement.

Why people say it works

Tradition and common belief, not evidence.

Believed to build strength and stamina, treat colds, and boost libido.

Who documented it

  • Microbial and heavy metal analyses of commercial sea moss, 2026, J Food Prot, PMID 42710593 study

    No opened account of Jamaican use (Atlas Obscura page returned 403; search snippets only). A 2026 study of 66 commercial products is the only opened source. UNVERIFIED as documentation.

The evidence

Tier is the evidence for the claim people make, not for the practice being widespread.

  1. C Quality of commercial sea moss

    66 US products. Presumptive Salmonella, Bacillus, Staphylococcus; arsenic, cobalt and mercury detected; wide variation.

    Strength: thin. Microbial and heavy metal analyses of commercial sea moss, 2026, J Food Prot, PMID 42710593
