Practice: Hair

Wearing hair natural in later life

  • Tier B thin Observed in Black populations
  • Harm: none found
  • Documented
  • Black British
  • Not yet clinically reviewed

What people do

Stop relaxing and wearing complex styles; wear the natural texture.

Why people say it works

Tradition and common belief, not evidence.

Confidence, identity and less time and cost; resistance to Eurocentric standards.

Who documented it

  • Hair identity and wellbeing of older Black women, UK, 2025, J Women Aging, PMID 39921474 survey

    UK survey of 46 and 10 interviews of older Black women: less frequent complex styles and fewer salon visits over 30 years; subjective wellbeing strengthened by confidence in own hair.

  • Hair grooming practices of women of African descent in London, 2016, J Eur Acad Dermatol Venereol, PMID 27038251 survey

    London, 242 women: natural hairstyles the most frequent current style (52.6%).

The evidence

Tier is the evidence for the claim people make, not for the practice being widespread.

  1. B Natural hair and wellbeing

    Mixed methods, small. Qualitative link between hair confidence and wellbeing; not a health outcome trial.

    Strength: thin, observational-black-population. Hair identity and wellbeing of older Black women, UK, 2025, J Women Aging, PMID 39921474

Harm

No harm identified.
