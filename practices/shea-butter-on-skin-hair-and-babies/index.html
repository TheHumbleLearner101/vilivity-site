Practice: Skin

Shea butter on skin, hair and babies

  • Tier B thin Tested, but not in people like you
  • Harm: low
  • Reported, not yet documented
  • West Africa, Caribbean, African American, Black British
  • Not yet clinically reviewed
Reported, not yet documented. We have not opened a source that shows people do this, so it is not counted in the main lists. The sources below are pointers, not proof.

What people do

Rub raw or whipped shea butter on skin, scalp, hair and babies as a daily moisturiser.

Why people say it works

Tradition and common belief, not evidence.

Traditional West African emollient, called gentle and healing.

Who documented it

  • Micali G et al 2018, G Ital Dermatol Venereol, PMID 29368843 study

    No opened survey of use. Evidence summarised from agent4. UNVERIFIED as documentation.

The evidence

Tier is the evidence for the claim people make, not for the practice being widespread.

  1. B Shea-containing combination device for atopic dermatitis

    Meta-analysis of 4 RCTs. Efficacy of a branded combination; shea cannot be isolated as the active.

    Strength: thin, trial-other-population. Micali G et al 2018, G Ital Dermatol Venereol, PMID 29368843

Harm

Latex allergy with raw shea (Cleveland Clinic, in the bank entry).

Gaps

  • No trial of plain shea butter.
