Practice: Food

Hot water, hot food and pepper soup after birth

  • Tier D Untested
  • Harm: possible
  • Documented
  • West Africa
  • Not yet clinically reviewed
Harm possible. The Nnewi authors judged some of the practices in their list to be dangerous; the abstract does not say which, and I did not read the full text.

What people do

For weeks after birth the mother drinks hot water, eats hot (temperature) and very peppery food and bathes in hot water.

Why people say it works

Tradition and common belief, not evidence.

Birth is believed to leave the body "cold" and "open"; heat restores it and helps the milk.

Who documented it

  • Udigwe GO, Emelumadu OF, Udigwe IB 2013, West Afr J Med, PMID 24488282 survey

    Nnewi, 330 Igbo mothers: 81.8% regularly drank hot water, 85.5% bathed in hot water, 81.8% took hot food and 75.8% took very peppery food.

The evidence

Tier is the evidence for the claim people make, not for the practice being widespread.
