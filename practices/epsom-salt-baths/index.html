Practice: Body

Epsom salt baths

  • Tier C thin Lab only
  • Harm: low
  • Reported, not yet documented
  • African American, Caribbean, Black British
  • Not yet clinically reviewed
Reported, not yet documented. We have not opened a source that shows people do this, so it is not counted in the main lists. The sources below are pointers, not proof.

What people do

Soak in hot water with Epsom salt for aches, colds and "drawing out" illness.

Why people say it works

Tradition and common belief, not evidence.

Magnesium is said to be absorbed through the skin and relax the muscles.

Who documented it

  • agent4-traditional-practices.md, 12 Nov 2026 (research file in this project) study

    Appears in a Root listing of grandmothers' cold and flu remedies (search snippet); page not opened. UNVERIFIED.

The evidence

Tier is the evidence for the claim people make, not for the practice being widespread.

  1. C Magnesium through skin

    Porcine skin ex vivo, 2026. Minimal magnesium crossed intact skin; delivery from a simple solution is unlikely.

    Strength: thin. Does magnesium permeate the skin? ex vivo porcine skin, 2026, PMID 42372834

Harm

A case report exists of hypermagnesaemia after swallowing a bath agent; not a bathing risk.
