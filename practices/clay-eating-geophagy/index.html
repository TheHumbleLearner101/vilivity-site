Practice: Food

Eating clay (geophagy) and ice (pagophagia) in pregnancy

  • Tier B moderate Observed in Black populations
  • Harm: yes
  • Documented
  • African American
  • Not yet clinically reviewed
Harm flagged. Pica is tied in these studies to lower iron stores in pregnancy; clay may raise blood pressure (Vermeer). Tell the midwife.

What people do

Some women, especially in the rural South, eat white or red clay, and some pregnant women eat large amounts of ice or freezer frost.

Why people say it works

Tradition and common belief, not evidence.

A cultural and family habit; women describe cravings and say it settles the stomach or helps the baby.

Who documented it

  • Vermeer DE, Frate DA 1979, Am J Clin Nutr, PMID 484531 ethnography

    Holmes County, Mississippi (Black population): geophagia in 57% of women and 16% of children, none among adult men; average 50 g of clay a day; 25 clay sources located.

  • Edwards CH et al 1994, J Nutr, PMID 8201446 study

    Washington DC, 553 African American women in prenatal clinics: no geophagy observed; ice or freezer frost eating (pagophagia) in 8.1%.

The evidence

Tier is the evidence for the claim people make, not for the practice being widespread.

  1. B Clay eating and anaemia, hunger, worms, potassium

    Rural Mississippi cross-sectional. Geophagia was not correlated with hunger, anaemia or helminths; may contribute to hypertension; did not support the hyperkalaemia idea.

    Strength: thin, observational-black-population. Vermeer DE, Frate DA 1979, Am J Clin Nutr, PMID 484531

  2. B Ice eating and iron

    553 pregnant women, DC. Women with pica had lower serum ferritin and lower mean cell haemoglobin; infants had smaller head circumference.

    Strength: moderate, observational-black-population. Edwards CH et al 1994, J Nutr, PMID 8201446

Harm

Pica is tied in these studies to lower iron stores in pregnancy; clay may raise blood pressure (Vermeer). Tell the midwife.

Gaps

  • 1979 and 1994 data; current prevalence not found.
  • Clay content and contaminants not examined.
